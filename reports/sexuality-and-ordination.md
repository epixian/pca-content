## Scope

This pass covers how the PCA dealt with homosexuality, same-sex attraction and fitness for office from 2018 to 2026:

- **Revoice 2018 and its first effects.** The conference at Memorial Presbyterian Church, St. Louis; Covenant Seminary's statement to the 46th General Assembly (GA); letters from other presbyteries to Missouri Presbytery.
- **The 47th GA (2019).** The Nashville Statement vote; the Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality; the declined affirmations on homosexuality.
- **The study report.** It was published in May 2020, and the 48th GA commended it in 2021.
- **Book of Church Order (BCO) amendments.** The 2021 items failed (Overture 23 / BCO 16-4, 53-32; Overture 37 / BCO 21-4 and 24-1, 45-40). The 2022 items passed (new BCO 16-4; revised BCO 21-4 and 24-1), and the 50th GA enacted them in 2023. BCO 7-4 (Overture 15) failed, 48-32. The 2023 chastity amendment to BCO 8-2 and 9-3 passed 77-2, and the 51st GA enacted it in 2024.
- **The Missouri Presbytery and TE Greg Johnson case.** The committee on Memorial; the BCO 31-2 investigation; Speck's two complaints; the Standing Judicial Commission (SJC) Cases 2020-12, 2020-05, 2020-02 and 2022-12; the five original-jurisdiction requests; Memorial's departure in 2022.
- **Later events.** In 2025, the 52nd GA ended its relationship with the Reformed Churches in the Netherlands.

I did not cover the earlier Assembly statements on homosexuality (5th GA 1977, 24th GA 1996, 27th GA 1999), except where 2018–2023 documents cite them.

## Record vs opinion

**Record.** Almost every event rests on the GA minutes (M46GA–M52GA). This includes the SJC decisions that the minutes print in Appendix T.

**The presbytery's acts come from the SJC.** Every Missouri Presbytery act from 2018 to 2020 rests on the SJC's "Summary of the Facts" in Cases 2020-12 and 2020-05 (M49GA pp. 730–741, 826–831). These summaries quote Missouri's minutes and reports. However, I did not see the presbytery's own records (the CIM report, the CRM report, the 49 affirmations and denials, or the minutes). The event text for the first act says so. The two 2022 acts rest on Missouri's public letter of December 6, 2022, which M50GA prints (pp. 920–921). I did not use any overture's "Whereas" clause as fact for what Missouri did.

**Study.** The 2020 report is a study. In 2021 the Assembly "commended" it and called it "a biblically faithful declaration". In 2022 the SJC wrote that the report "does not have Constitutional status" (M49GA p. 843). The events keep this difference.

**Opinion.** This pass has no opinion sources. The record is rich, but the published debate is not shown. I found no Revoice or Johnson writings, no responses to the report, and no commentary on the overtures from either side. This is a balance gap (see follow-ups).

### Points where the record and other accounts differ

1. **M49GA p. 735 misattributes Overture 2.** The SJC summary says Savannah River adopted Overture 4 "concurring with Calvary's Overture 2". The overture itself (M48GA p. 955) and the same summary one page earlier (p. 734) say Overture 2 came from Central Georgia. The event follows the overtures and does not quote that line.
2. **The presbytery tallies for 2022–2023.** M50GA (pp. 133–165) printed tallies with 68 of 88 presbyteries reporting; it gave BCO 7-4 as 39-29 and BCO 16-4 as 67-1. M51GA Part IV (p. 1217) corrects them: "The vote tallies published did not include the onsite revision". The corrected totals are 48-32, 79-1 and 76-4 (80 reporting). The event uses the corrected totals and mentions the earlier ones.
3. **The date of the Nashville Statement vote.** The survey gives 2019-06-28. The minutes put it in the Fourth Session, "Thursday Evening June 27, 2019", which recessed at 12:05 a.m. The vote on Overture 4 came early in that session. I used 2019-06-27 for all three GA47 events. The vote on Overture 28 may have come just after midnight.
4. **The GA48 Sixth Session ran past midnight.** It ran from 9:00 p.m. on July 1 to 12:43 a.m. on July 2. The minutes head it "Thursday Evening July 1, 2021", and I dated the three events 2021-07-01. The final vote on Overture 37 is recorded only as "adopted", with no count.
5. **Struck text.** The extracted text has the deleted words next to the new ones in these places: M47GA pp. 104–106 (Overture 42: "two three", "$15,000"), M48GA p. 108 (the original BCO 17-4), M49GA pp. 107–108 and 125 ("identify describe"), and M47GA pp. 97–98 (Overture 11). I quoted amendment wording only from the clean sent-down texts (M49GA pp. 158, 162; M50GA pp. 133, 141; M51GA p. 982), and the $25,000 budget from the Clerk's note (M47GA p. 77).
6. **The survey's "Overture 15 (sent down 2022)".** This is correct. However, the Overtures Committee had recommended answering it by reference to Overture 29, and the floor adopted the minority report. The event records both steps.

## Gaps and open questions

- **Missouri Presbytery's own records.** These are the CIM report (2019), the open letter (January 2020), the CRM report (July 2020), the 49 affirmations and denials, and the 2017 report "Homosexuality and the Gospel of Grace". The SJC summary gives Google Drive links for them. I did not fetch them.
- **The 2022 remand.** The SJC sent Case 2020-05 back to Missouri for a new hearing (March 2022). I found no record of that hearing. The December 2022 letter says only that the presbytery "had recently empowered its moderator" to appoint a new BCO 31-2 committee.
- **The constitutional business dissent (M49GA p. 435).** A minority of the Committee on Constitutional Business dissented from its 6-2 finding of no exceptions to the SJC's April 30, 2021 minutes in Case 2020-12. I did not read it in full or find the Assembly's action on it.
- **GA47 Overture 11 (Fellowship).** It asked the Assembly to commend the RPCNA report "Contemporary Perspectives on Sexual Orientation". It was answered "in the affirmative as amended" (M47GA pp. 97–98). The text has struck and added words together, so I could not tell what was adopted. There is no event.
- **Central Carolina Presbytery's Revoice report.** M47GA p. 105 cites it ("Study Committee Report on 2018 Revoice Conference"). It is not sourced.
- **The 2020 report's exact date.** The minutes' URL has "5-28-20", and the SJC gives "05/2020". The event uses 2020-05.
- **The 2021–2022 presbytery vote dates.** M49GA gives the result as reported on June 21, 2022, but no closing date. The event spans 2021-07/2022-06-21.
- **Unread hits.** I did not read the Revoice hits at M49GA pp. 149 and 194–195. GA47 ruled out of order Savannah River's Overture 35 and Southern New England's Overture 39 (M47GA pp. 77, 103); they have no event.
- **Not searched.** These were not searched: Covenant Seminary statements after GA46; Revoice's own site; byFaith, WORLD, Christianity Today and RNS coverage; the 2023–2024 TAR "vote update" posts listed in the survey; other SJC cases on sexuality after 2023; and presbytery minutes.
