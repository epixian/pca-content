Bryce Avenue Presbyterian Church, White Rock, NM) in Rio Grande Presbytery (RGP),
2024-2026. The sources are news reports, documents that a party posted online, opinion pieces, and X posts.

## 2. Record, reported, alleged

- **Public record (court).** None found. I could not find a presbytery website, minutes, or statement. RGP did not reply to three news outlets.
- **Court documents posted by others.** These are the two letters, the committee's majority report, and the indictment. Jon Harris links to all of them. The letters and the report are on a Squarespace file host. I could not identify its owner: it is not brycepresbyterian.org, jonharrismedia.com, or berithpress.com. The upload time in the URLs is about 2026-01-08. The indictment is a Google Drive scan with a date of 2026-02-04 in its metadata. The Tennessean says it got the "indictment letter" from Ryan Biese, an ally of Garris. I consider these documents authentic in substance: the news reports quote the same texts. But no court published them. I gave them `kind: statement`, not `minutes`, and wrote the provenance in each source title. The lead may prefer another kind.
- **Reported (news).** The verdict, the charges in outline, and the suspension. The Tennessean states the outcome in its own voice; it had the indictment letter (from Biese) and an email from Garris. The Christian Post and the Roys Report attribute the outcome to McGowan's X post. Each outlet asked the presbytery for comment, and the presbytery did not reply.
- **Roys quote on the complaints.** The `rgp-garris-letters` event cites the Roys Report sentence "numerous PCA ministers found fault with the exchange and complained to Garris' presbytery." In the article, that sentence begins "According to a Substack post by podcaster Jon Harris". The letters themselves are the main support.
- **Alleged or argued (one side).** McGowan says no evidence and no witnesses on Charge Two. Biese says one accuser took part in the process (Tennessean). Harris says presbyters leaked trial information against BCO confidentiality, that the presbytery adopted the report in January 2025, and that the appeal stays the censure. The other side has not spoken publicly. Neither the accusers nor RGP have given an account.

## 3. Conflicts and open questions

- **Trial and verdict date.** The Roys Report says "On March 28" (2026-03-28 was a Saturday). McGowan posted on Thursday, 2026-05-28 (US time). Harris (2026-06-01) says the verdict came "last week." The verdict and the censure may have come on different days. The event is dated `../2026-05-28`.
- **Indictment date.** The scan is signed 2026-02-04. Harris says January 2026, and the presbytery may have voted to indict before the signing. Harris links the document as the "trial verdict," but it is the indictment.
- **What Charge One was.** The indictment charges false statements against the ninth commandment. The news calls it the charge "about slavery," and Protestia calls it "racial comments." The events follow the indictment.
- **Effect of an appeal.** Harris says the appeal stays the censure. A forum commenter cites a BCO rule that the lower court may vote to keep a suspension in force during an appeal. I did not check the BCO text, and no source says what RGP did. No event states the effect.
- **Appeal status.** No source confirms that an appeal or complaint was filed, and I found no SJC case. Check the SJC report in the 54th GA minutes (2027) and the RGP minutes.
- **Presbytery adoption of the report (January 2025).** Only Harris states it. Harris says that an X user published the report. That user's X post of 2025-01-29 says that "Various Elders" gave him access and that it was "public/non-executive." That suggests that the report was read in open session. Not confirmed.
- **Minority report.** Harris mentions it. It was not found online.
- **RPR citation.** byFaith (2026-05-29) says that RPR cited RGP to appear in 2027. It does not tie this to the Garris case. Harris links the two; I did not.
