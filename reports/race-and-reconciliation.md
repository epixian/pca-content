# Race and racial reconciliation in the PCA: research pass

Written 2026-11-20. All new events have `status: draft` and `visibility: public`. The last strict build (`tools/build.py --strict`) passed with no errors (365 events, 132 actors). During the pass, other passes had short-lived build errors in their own files (see "Build note" at the end). Each fact about a church action rests on the GA minutes, on the 1973 Advisory Convention minutes, or on a session statement. Opinion pieces are used only as responses.

## Scope

This pass covers how the PCA has spoken about race, racism and racial reconciliation:

- **Founding era.** The 1973 Advisory Convention, the 1977 NAPARC conference on race relations, and the 1984 answer to an RPCES overture on ethnic ministries.
- **2002–2004.** The 30th General Assembly statement on racial reconciliation, the 31st General Assembly request for a pastoral letter, and the 32nd General Assembly pastoral letter "The Gospel and Race".
- **2015–2016.** The personal resolution on Civil Rights remembrance, its referral, the presbytery overtures, Overture 43, the study committee, and the PCA Unity Fund.
- **2017–2019.** The work of the Ad Interim Committee on Racial and Ethnic Reconciliation, the Morton Smith memorials, and the published papers. The 2018 report itself is already in the graph.
- **2021–2025.** The overtures on white supremacy and critical race theory, the statement on anti-Asian racism, the 2023 follow-up survey, and the 2025 action on ethnicity data. The 2025 condemnation of racial superiority teaching is already in the graph.

Local church acts appear only where a session published its own statement (First Presbyterian Church, Jackson, 2016). Church court cases about race are not in this pass.

## Record, position, study and opinion

**Record.** All Assembly actions come from the GA minutes. The 1973 resolution comes from the printed minutes of the Advisory Convention. The First Presbyterian Church, Jackson, statement is the session's own text, but the copy is a reprint in The Aquila Report.

**Position.** These are the 1973 Advisory Convention resolution, the 2002 statement and the 2002 personal resolution, the 2004 pastoral letter, the 2016 Overture 43 resolutions, the 2021 statement on anti-Asian racism, and the 2025 condemnation (existing event). Note that the Advisory Convention was not yet a church court.

**Study.** The 2018 report was "received" and distributed. The 46th GA did not adopt its text as a statement. But Recommendation 2 told CDM to publish the actions of the 30th, 32nd and 44th Assemblies "as the PCA's statements on the Gospel and racial reconciliation." The 50th GA "commend[ed] again" the report. The 2021 and 2023 Assemblies used the report as a ground to decline new overtures.

**Opinion.** Hutchinson (2015), Nelson (2016), Tisby (2016), Shaw (2021), and the Baird floor speech (2015). The speech transcript was made by a commissioner, TE Tim LeCroy, and the PCA Historical Center keeps it. The minutes do not record the speech. I gave it `kind: opinion`.

**Dates of Aquila Report pieces.** I used the date printed on each Aquila Report page. The dates in the Aquila sitemap survey differ by one to three days, and for the Tisby reprint by four years (the survey lists 2020-12-10; the page shows June 28, 2016).

**Balance of opinion.** Two pieces support the actions (Tisby; Hutchinson, who supports the referral and the process). Two pieces are critical or cautious (Shaw against the 2021 overture; Nelson, who warns about "confessing the sins of others" but accepts covenantal repentance). All four opinion pieces come from The Aquila Report, which reprinted three of them from blogs. No piece from byFaith, TGC, WORLD or Christianity Today is used.

### Points where the record and later accounts differ

These are noted, not resolved:

1. **The 2002 wording.** The adopted statement (M30GA p. 262) confesses "our involvement in these sins" and says "We will strive." Nashville's original Overture 20 (M30GA pp. 265–266) confessed "our covenantal involvement in these national sins" and said "we pledge to work hard." The 2015 personal resolution (M43GA p. 17) describes the 2002 action as a confession of "covenantal, generational, heinous sins." The 2018 report (M46GA p. 596) quotes the adopted text but leaves out "minority" from "urban and minority congregations." The event quotes only the adopted text.
2. **The 2003 action.** Overture 17 asked for an ad interim committee (M31GA p. 193). The Assembly's amended answer gave the task to MNA (p. 194). The 2004 letter (M32GA p. 428) confirms this. The event does not say that the 31st GA erected a committee.
3. **The 2003 "spirituality of the church."** The minority report uses the doctrine only as an example of a reason to disagree that is not racism. It does not argue from the doctrine. The event says this.
4. **The 2018 report and the spirituality of the church.** I searched M46GA pp. 596–628 for "spirituality", "intermeddle", "civil affairs" and "Thornwell" and found no use of the doctrine. I did not tag the 2018 report with `spirituality-of-the-church`. The 2016 use is North Florida's Overture 50. Mississippi Valley's pastoral letter (Overture 55, Attachment 1, M44GA pp. 673–694) also lists works on the doctrine in its resources (pp. 689–690).
5. **"39 presbyteries."** The 44th GA answered 39 overtures by reference to Overture 43 (M44GA pp. 70–71, my count), and also Overture 60 from a session. The Administrative Committee's struck grounds at the 52nd GA (M52GA p. 76) speak of "the Overtures of 39 different presbyteries." The two counts measure different things. The event gives the count of overtures.
6. **Struck text at the 52nd GA.** The extracted text of M52GA pp. 76–78 contains both the Administrative Committee's answer and the Committee of Commissioners' amendment, with no strike marks. Only the page images (PDF pp. 78–79) show "NEGATIVE", "Age or", "age or" and the Administrative Committee's grounds struck. The event follows the page images and quotes only unstruck text for the adopted answer. It quotes the AC's grounds from the AC's own report (M52GA pp. 147–148), where they are not struck.
7. **The 44th GA votes.** At the 44th GA and the 48th GA several votes were on procedure (to recommit, to call the question). The events give only the vote on the main recommendation. For the 48th GA answers to Overtures 36 and 47, the minutes give no count.
8. **"Repent" in Overture 43.** Potomac Presbytery's text (M44GA p. 647) asked the Assembly to "recognize, confess, and condemn these past and continuing racial sins." The Overtures Committee's amendment added "and repent of," the words about the Civil Rights era, and the list of sins (M44GA p. 76, page image PDF p. 78). David Coffin, of Potomac Presbytery, dissented from exactly this word. Later accounts (for example the AC grounds at the 52nd GA, and the Tisby piece) quote the amended text. The events follow the minutes.
9. **The NAPARC statement (1977).** M5GA records only that the PCA sent two representatives. I found no PCA Assembly action that adopted the statement. The RPCES printed it (M155GS p. 143) and adopted "a paper" from the conference in 1978 (M156GS p. 139). The 30th GA's note (M30GA p. 262) refers to "the resulting statement adopted," which may mean adopted by the conference.

## Gaps and open questions

- **The founding era.** The minutes of the first ten Assemblies have no statement on race. The 2018 report (M46GA p. 598) cites an address by O. Palmer Robertson at the first GA, in *Addresses Delivered During the First General Assembly* (1973). That book is not a source. I did not search the Presbyterian Journal or the Steering Committee papers.
- **RPCES statements.** The RPCES adopted a "Report on Racial Questions" (144th General Synod, 1966) and a statement on interracial marriage (1968). The 2003 and 2016 overtures cite them. The PCA Historical Center has both pages (topical/race/1966_racial_questions.html, 1968_interracial_marriage.html), and The Aquila Report reprinted the 1966 report (2015-06-17). I did not make them sources. M154GS p. 153 also refers to an RPCES "resolution on racial issues."
- **The 1964 EPC statement** ("Biblical Principles of Racial Reconciliation") is on the same Historical Center page. It is not used.
- **1985–2001.** I found nothing at the Assembly level. The hits for "racial reconciliation" in M22GA–M28GA are NAE reports and Covenant College reports.
- **1986 church case (M14GA pp. 180–181).** A protest in a judicial case says that some members were "falsely accused of not accepting an inter-racially married couple." The case names private persons. I wrote no event.
- **The PCA Unity Fund after 2016.** I did not trace how MNA set up the fund or how much it raised.
- **The 2017 initial report and the Moderator's appointment date.** The appointment date is not in the minutes I read. The event about the 45th GA mentions the appointment without a date.
- **Kevin Smith and other committee members.** They have no actor records. They appear in the event text only.
- **The 2018 report's later uses.** The 2021 statement (M48GA p. 118), the 2023 grounds (M50GA p. 100) and the 2025 AC grounds (M52GA p. 77) cite or quote the report. The existing event `ga46-receives-race-report-rejecting-kinism` has no `responds_to` link to the 44th GA committee event.
- **M53GA (2026).** The 2026 minutes are not yet a source. The GA53 debate on Christian nationalism mentions racism (`ga53-receives-christian-nationalism-report`), but I found no separate race action.
- **The body of the 2018 report.** I read the introduction (M46GA pp. 596–599), the recommendations (p. 628) and the pages that my searches hit (pp. 598, 606–612, 619–625). I did not read pp. 600–627 in full. The existing event covers the report; a separate event on its findings (the LifeWay survey results, its suggestions to presbyteries) may be useful.
- **Opinion sources not fetched.** Lucas, *For a Continuing Church* (2015); Kenneth Taylor's article on the spirituality of the church and segregation; the Presbyterian Journal. No byFaith or TGC piece was fetched.
- **Exact dates.** The First Presbyterian Church, Jackson, session acted at its May 2016 meeting; the day is not given. The CDM book date is "../2019-06" because M47GA reports it as done. The 30th GA action on Overture 19 has month precision only.
