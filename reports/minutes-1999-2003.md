The amendment's exact words, as M30GA p. 72 prints the enacted 12-5.e:

> To exercise in accordance with the Directory for Worship, authority over the
> time and place of the preaching of the Word and the administration of the
> Sacraments, over all other religious services, over the music in the services,
> and over the uses to which the church building and associated properties may be
> put; to take the oversight of the singing in the public worship of God; to
> ensure that the Word of God is preached only by such men as are sufficiently
> qualified (BCO 4-4, 53-2, 1 Timothy 2:11-12); to assemble the people for
> worship when there is no minister; to determine the best measures for promoting

M29GA p. 223 prints "Adopted" under the committee's negative answer to
Overture 27 and "Adopted as amended" under the minority substitute. The journal
(29-47, 29-50) says only that recommendation 18 and the minority report were
discussed. The front-matter list of amendments sent down ("BCO 12-5e see 29-44,
III, 18, p. 223") and M30GA Item 6 show that the minority text was sent down.

M29GA p. 224 says "the portion amended by addition is in bold". The text
extraction loses bold, so the events quote the clause and do not say which words
were new.

## Corrections to the brief and to earlier leads

- **BCO 12-5.e dates.** The brief and `complementarianism-pca.md` placed the
  proposal at the 30th GA and the enactment at the 31st. The minutes show the
  proposal at the **29th GA (2001-06-21)**, the presbytery vote 42-9, and the
  enactment at the **30th GA (2002-06-19)**. M31GA has no action on 12-5.e. This
  agrees with the M38GA concurring opinion ("amended in 2002").
- **A failed precursor.** The 28th GA (2000) sent down a BCO 12-5.d clause
  "prohibiting women from expounding and preaching the Word of God as an
  ordinance of public worship". The presbyteries defeated it 31-23 (M29GA p. 50).
- **The PCA vote on the suspension** was at the 26th GA (1998-07-01), not in
  1999-2002. No GA vote on the CRC was taken in 1999 or 2001. The 2001 IRC
  committee of commissioners only discussed "the status of PCA efforts to remove
  the CRC" (M29GA p. 60).

## Discrepancies noted, not changed

- **Session date misprints.** M29GA p. 47 prints "Wednesday Morning June 18,
  2001", but the Assembly met June 19-22 and p. 68 prints "Wednesday Afternoon
  June 20, 2001". M30GA p. 98 prints "June 20, 2001" for the 2002 Wednesday
  afternoon session. M26GA p. 99 prints "June 11, 1997". I dated by weekday and
  the title-page range: 29-12 is 2001-06-20.
- **`naparc-suspends-crc`.** M27GA p. 355 dates the NAPARC meeting to
  November 17-18, 1998 (Sioux Falls) and says the KAPC **and the CRC** "have not
  voted". The event text (from `urns-naparc-1998`) says the CRC synod voted
  against. M26GA p. 75 lists the CRC as having "Opposed the proposed suspension".
  I did not change the event. A reviewer may refine the date to
  1998-11-17/1998-11-18.
- **`naparc-terminates-crc`.** M31GA p. 433 speaks of "the recommendation of the
  2002 meeting" and says "The 2003 council approved the final action". M30GA
  p. 541 places the recommendation at the 2001 fall meeting, and M31GA was
  written before the November 2003 meeting. I kept the date 2002.
- **Cedar Springs' departure.** M29GA p. 49 gives 04/08/01 and M30GA p. 60 gives
  07/10/01. The event uses `2001` and cites both.
- **28th GA, 28-72.** The Bills and Overtures text is printed under the Friday
  session (2000-06-23), but recommendation 1 (Louisiana Ov. 22) was voted on
  Thursday evening (28-63, 2000-06-22). The Wood event uses the span.
