Written 2026-11-20. Scope: the judicial process against TE Rick Appleton, associate pastor of
Providence Presbyterian Church, Robinson Township, PA, in Pittsburgh Presbytery (PCA), 2026.

  Robinson Township, in Pittsburgh Presbytery: M50GA (2023) p. 760, M51GA (2024) p. 1033, M52GA (2025)
  p. 1146 (with TE Ray Heiple Jr.). M46GA (2018) p. 469 lists a Rick Appleton at Old Peachtree, Duluth, GA.

- The case is not the RPCNA case of Samuel Ketcham (Presbytery of the Alleghenies, January 2026). That
  case is background: Appleton attended the Ketcham trial, and Charge 1 cites it.
- Eli McGowan (@elimcgowan), who posted on the case, is not Sean McGowan (actor `sean-mcgowan`).

**The charges (indictment).**
- Charge 1: violating BCO 21-5 (failing "to adorn the profession of the Gospel"). (A) 24 social media
  posts, 2018-2026; (B) "public associations": a 2023 post of a Michael Spangler article, and his
  presence and naming at the Ketcham trial, a slavery post, and a "like"; (C) "obfuscating the truth":
  that he concealed his attendance at the Ketcham trial from the senior pastor and session, and that he
  wrote that he was "only vaguely familiar" with kinism. Standards cited include WLC 113, 136, 144 and
  the ninth commandment.
- Charge 2: violating the third commandment (WLC 113) "in his teaching on the nature and calling of
  women." Specification 1: "ontological inferiority of women." Specification 2: headship teaching that
  "denigrate women," 12 items (for example, husbands as "prophet" and "priest"; a wife to submit to
  an unbelieving husband "even if he doesn't let you go to church"). Specification 3: error on the
  "nature, role, and calling of women," 6 items (for example, childbearing as a woman's calling, and
  Eve's "shame" on "womankind"). Additional specifications under BCO 34-5 and 57-5 (p. 8).

## 3. Conflicts and open questions

- **Appeal.** Harris (2026-07-24) notes the 30-day period under BCO 42-4. I found no report of an
  appeal or complaint, and no SJC docket entry. The SJC report in the M54GA minutes (2027) will settle
  this. No event states the appeal status. Check before publishing.
- **Written decision.** Nelson (2026-07-21) said it would "likely be published next month." It was not
  found by 2026-11-20.
- **Vote.** Only Nelson gives a tally, and only for count 1 (41-0, 1 abstention). Harris and Iron Ink
  apply it to the whole verdict. The count 2 tally and the vote on the censure are unknown. The events
  give it for count 1 only.
- **Trial date.** Harris: "the weekend of July 18-19." McGowan (2026-07-20, 05:57 UTC, so 01:57 Monday
  Eastern time) says "this weekend" and "all-day," which suggests one day, likely Saturday 2026-07-18. The
  event uses the span 2026-07-18/2026-07-19.
- **Referral date.** McGowan (July) says the session referred Appleton "in March." The indictment
  gives February 5 (session meeting) and February 9 (email to presbytery), and McGowan's own first
  post is dated March 4. The event follows the indictment.
- **Race and kinism in the trial.** McGowan reports a claim that race and kinism were not a major part
  of the trial. Harris describes the charges as social media posts plus teaching on women, and does not
  mention specifications B and C. But the indictment's Charge 1 B and C deal with the Ketcham trial,
  kinism, "race realism," and a post on slavery as a "positive good." What the court weighed is unknown
  without the decision.
- **Suspension pending trial.** Only one X post (three unnamed sources) reports it. It says "after a
  specially called session"; it is not clear whether the session or the presbytery met. I do not know
  the date or the BCO basis. The event is `visibility: private` because its only
  source is social media.
- **"Deposed."** No source says whether the censure was deposition alone or also suspension from the
  sacraments or excommunication.
- **Providence website.** The leadership page still shows "Pastor Appleton" (in the deacons list, with
  no first name). It may be stale.
- **McGowan's typo.** His July post says "Philadelphia Presbytery"; he corrected it to Pittsburgh in a
  reply. No link to `philadelphia-presbytery` was made.
