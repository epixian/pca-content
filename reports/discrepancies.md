- Item 4: the research says Liberti commissioned its diaconate "in 2007". The source says the act is in the PP minutes of November 11, 2006 (M35GA p. 209; M36GA p. 261; M38GA p. 109). The event date is `../2006-11-11`.
- Item 4: M35GA p. 209 does not name Liberti. It says "Diaconate of new church". M36GA p. 205 (Overture 9) names Liberti Church as the church in this exception.
- Item 4: NCP's citation is on M35GA p. 198 (section 43), not p. 209. It cites the NCP minutes of March 3-4, 2006, for "2 Deaconesses". It has a second exception: deacons commissioned without election, ordination, and installation.

The NCP response defends the organizing pastor's approved exception. RPR's rationale for NCP differs from the PP rationale: it says BCO 9-7 covers people the session appoints, and the practice denies qualified men their right to the office. The 38th GA found it satisfactory.

PP adopted Overture 9 on January 26, 2008 (M36GA p. 206). So Liberti's overture came before PP's 2008-2009 response in M38GA.
- Item 6: the research says Hsu was "at City Church". M37GA pp. 176-177 do not name City Church; it calls him "a ministerial candidate". The event does not name City Church as a subject.
- Item 6: the research says PP allowed the licensure as "technically" an exception to church government. M37GA pp. 177-178 say the Leadership Development Team made that classification during the later ordination examination (after November 10, 2007), not at licensure (September 12, 2007).
- Item 6: the research cites M36GA p. 205 for the CCC complaint. That page has only a summary in Overture 9. M37GA p. 177 gives the date: October 11, 2007.
