The Reformed Church in the United States (RCUS) had applied for membership in the North American Presbyterian and Reformed Council (NAPARC). Mid-America Presbytery overtured the 21st General Assembly (Overture 31). It asked the Assembly to instruct the PCA delegates to NAPARC to vote for the reception of the RCUS, to establish ecclesiastical correspondence with the RCUS, and to apologize to the RCUS for unkind remarks by PCA delegates. The overture said that PCA delegates had reportedly made derogatory comments about the size of the RCUS. Louisiana Presbytery sent a concurring overture (Overture 36), which it adopted in April 1993.
