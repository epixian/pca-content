The seven-member ad interim committee (four teaching elders and three ruling elders, chaired by TE Paul Fowler) reported on election and covenant, justification and union with Christ, and perseverance and assurance. It "unanimously" made nine declarations. Each declaration named a view, for example one that rejects the "bi-covenantal structure of Scripture," one that makes a person "elect" by membership in the visible church, or one that bases justification "in any way" on works, and called it contrary to the Westminster Standards. Presenting the declarations, TE Sean Lucas said that justification by faith alone and union with Christ were "not an area in which we should tolerate breadth."
