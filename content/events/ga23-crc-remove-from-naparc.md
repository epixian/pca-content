After the 1995 Synod of the Christian Reformed Church (CRC) permitted women to hold the offices of minister and elder, the 23rd General Assembly told the Interchurch Relations Committee (IRC) to use due process to remove the CRC from NAPARC if the 1996 Synod did not rescind the action. It sent the CRC a letter that said the action contradicted Scripture and the Reformed confessions. The Assembly also did not accept the IRC's reply to an earlier exception about its vote to ask the Evangelical Presbyterian Church to reapply to NAPARC. Its grounds cited the 1989 decision about that church's ordination of women.
