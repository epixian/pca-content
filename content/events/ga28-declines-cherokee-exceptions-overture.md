The session of Cherokee Presbyterian Church sent Overture 6 to the 28th General Assembly after North Georgia Presbytery voted against it. The overture asked to amend BCO 16-4 so that a court could direct a candidate, by majority vote, not to teach an exception. The Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said that the Constitution already defined the presbytery's powers on this issue.
