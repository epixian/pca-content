The 48th General Assembly answered in the negative Chesapeake Presbytery's Overture 36 (a study committee on white supremacy) and its Overture 47 (a study committee on critical race theory). The minutes record the votes to close debate but not the counts on the answers. The Overtures Committee's grounds were the same for both: there had been "a proliferation of Study Committees," study committees fit doctrinal questions or complex practical ones, and "there are books available on the subjects."
