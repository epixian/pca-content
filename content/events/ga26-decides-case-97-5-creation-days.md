The session of Mt. Carmel Church complained against New Jersey Presbytery's 1996 "Affirmations and Denials Regarding the Interpretation of Genesis One." The Standing Judicial Commission denied the complaint. Nine members filed a minority report, so the case went to the 26th General Assembly under BCO 15-5. The Assembly defeated the minority report and adopted the majority report. Several commissioners recorded their votes.
