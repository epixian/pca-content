The 21st General Assembly answered Potomac Presbytery's Overture 16 in the affirmative as amended and adopted a Declaration of Conscience addressed to the President of the United States on homosexuality. The declaration said that the Assembly does not regularly address matters before the civil government, quoted WCF 31.4, and said that the Assembly found "a case extraordinary."

The Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures also recommended that North Georgia Presbytery's Communication 4, a resolution of April 20, 1993 against lifting the ban on homosexuality in the military, be answered by reference to Overture 16. The Minutes note that this recommendation was not reported to the Assembly.
