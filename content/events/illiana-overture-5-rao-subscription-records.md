Illiana, Eastern Canada, North Florida and Blue Ridge Presbyteries sent Overture 5 to the 31st General Assembly. It asked to amend RAO 14-3 and 14-8 so that presbytery minutes record the stated differences with the Standards that a presbytery approves as exceptions.
