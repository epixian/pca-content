Northern California Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 5-9.e, 9-2 and 9-7 so that a session may administer diaconal ministry itself, with assistants such as a "diaconal mercy team," when a church has no deacons. The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
