At its January 2009 stated meeting, Rocky Mountain Presbytery examined Dan Breed for ordination. Breed held that the office of elder is only for men and that 1 Timothy 2:11-12 forbids a woman to "teach authoritatively", that is, to preach. But he would let a woman teach Scripture to men and women under the session's authority, for example a Sunday school class on Romans. No presbyter moved to challenge this view, and the presbytery sustained his theological exam and approved him for ordination. TE James Urish and others filed a complaint on February 20, 2009. The presbytery denied it in April 2009, and the complainants took it to the General Assembly.
