An investigative committee of Rio Grande Presbytery reported on the complaints against Garris. Its majority report concluded that his statements on slavery and his manner on X "present us with a strong presumption of guilt" of violating the PCA's standards, especially WLC 142, 144 and 145 and the sixth and seventh ordination vows. Jon Harris wrote that the presbytery adopted the report in January 2025.
