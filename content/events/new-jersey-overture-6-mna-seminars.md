The Presbytery of New Jersey sent Overture 6, "Concern About Women Leaders in MNA Seminars," to the 25th General Assembly. It told its clerk to communicate the presbytery's deep concern about the use of women as teachers and worship leaders in seminars sponsored by Mission to North America, and to ask the Assembly to instruct MNA to stop the practice. The overture gives the meeting (the 79th stated meeting) but not its date.
