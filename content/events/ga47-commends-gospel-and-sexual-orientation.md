Fellowship Presbytery's Overture 11 asked the 47th General Assembly to commend and distribute the RPCNA report "Contemporary Perspectives on Sexual Orientation." The committees of commissioners on the Administrative Committee, Discipleship Ministries and Reformed University Fellowship recommended a negative answer; the CDM committee said that the report was under copyright. The Overtures Committee instead amended the overture to commend the published book, The Gospel & Sexual Orientation, as "a work consistent with our standards." The Assembly adopted the Overtures Committee's recommendation by voice vote.
