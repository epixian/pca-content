Overture 33 to the 29th General Assembly asked the Assembly to receive a definition of "Covenantal Patriarchy" and to call the PCA to repent for abandoning it. The definition said that God has placed men, under Christ, as the heads of households, and it rejected an "administrative egalitarianism" between men and women. Central Carolina Presbytery had rejected the overture on April 28, 2001, and a teaching elder of that presbytery sent it to the Assembly himself under the Rules of Assembly Operations. The Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said the Assembly affirmed biblical concepts of the covenant and the family but did not agree with all of the overture's reasoning and definitions, and that the overture gave no evidence for its accusation against the PCA.
