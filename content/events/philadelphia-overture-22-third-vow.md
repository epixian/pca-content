Philadelphia Presbytery adopted Overture 22, asking the 42nd General Assembly to establish a study committee on the third ordination vow in BCO 21-5, the vow to approve the government and discipline of the PCA. The overture said that some candidates for ordination or transfer "understand scripture to allow women to be ordained to the office of elder, contrary to BCO 7-2".
