Grace Presbytery adopted Overture 43 to the 12th General Assembly on March 13, 1984. It said judicial cases took much time and money at the Assembly. It asked the Committee on Administration and the Committee on Judicial Business to plan for hearing cases between Assemblies, for example by regional commissions. The 12th General Assembly answered it in the negative, together with Covenant Presbytery's Overture 35, because the "present system is sufficient."
