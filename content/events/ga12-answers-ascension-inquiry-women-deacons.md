Ascension Presbytery sent Constitutional Inquiry 12 to the 12th General Assembly after the 11th Assembly's judgment in Case 2. It asked whether a court may ordain or receive a man who believes Scripture permits women deacons but agrees to submit in practice to the standards. The Assembly adopted the Committee on Judicial Business's answer. The answer said that such a belief, with agreement to abide by BCO 7-2 and 9-3, "is not sufficient reason by itself to deny ordination or reception." It also said that the Assembly should not decide in the abstract what disqualifies a man from office. A separate inquiry from TE Bruce W. Howes and others asked whether an elder who believes the Bible permits women deacons keeps his third ordination vow. The Assembly answered it by reference to its answer to Constitutional Inquiry 13.
