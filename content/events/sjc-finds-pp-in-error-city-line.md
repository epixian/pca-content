In Case 2011-14 the Standing Judicial Commission held that Philadelphia Presbytery
erred when it declared the Reese and Bech complaint out of order, and it sustained
the complaint. It did not grant the requested amends and listed four
constitutional means of redress. The vote was 18 concurring, 1 dissenting, and 5
absent.
