On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations, the 30th General Assembly approved the termination of the CRC's membership in NAPARC. The grounds said that the CRC had not reversed its 1995 decision, which its 2000 Synod reaffirmed, and that by declaring the word "male" in its Church Order inoperative it had acted against Scripture and the Belgic Confession and circumvented its own constitution. The Assembly also commended the IRC for the rationale on which NAPARC's action was based.
