On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Administration, the 14th General Assembly received the Church/State Subcommittee's report as information and for study. The report covered church/state relations, incorporation, taxation, parental responsibility for education, and civil disobedience in the abortion controversy. The Assembly sent the report to the churches and asked for questions, suggestions or objections, with a final report to follow.
