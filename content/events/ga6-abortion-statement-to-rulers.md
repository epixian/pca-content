The 6th General Assembly adopted the report of its Ad Interim Committee on Abortion. It directed the Stated Clerk to send the report to the President, the leaders of Congress and the Chief Justice with a statement that began, "God declares in Sacred Scripture that civil government, no less than the Church, is a divine institution." Four commissioners recorded negative votes on that recommendation.
