North Georgia Presbytery sent Overture 11 to the 5th General Assembly. It called homosexuality a sin. It asked the Assembly to encourage Christians to petition the civil authorities so that people who practice, approve or condone it would not have authority to teach in schools. The Minutes do not print the date on which the presbytery adopted the overture.
