The 47th General Assembly answered Calvary Presbytery's Overture 4 in the affirmative (803-541), declaring the Nashville Statement a biblically faithful declaration. A minority report that would have sent the overture back to the next year's Overtures Committee failed (572-792). The minority had argued that the Nashville Statement should be studied by a committee and that the overture did not make it "an official document of the PCA."
