Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 12 to the 2nd General Assembly on July 20, 1974. It asked the Assembly to tell the Committee of Mission to the World to end all ties and cooperation with any agency of the World Council of Churches. The overture was a single sentence and stated no grounds. Warrior Presbytery's related overtures (Overtures 15-18) on the National and World Councils of Churches gave the reasons: the church had often said in its publications that it did not belong to or support the councils, and the 1st General Assembly had told its Committee on Mission to the United States not to do joint work with any denomination that was a member of them. The 3rd General Assembly answered these overtures.
