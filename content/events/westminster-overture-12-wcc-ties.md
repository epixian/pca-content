Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 12 to the 2nd General Assembly on July 20, 1974. It asked the Assembly to tell the Committee of Mission to the World to end all ties and cooperation with any agency of the World Council of Churches. Warrior Presbytery sent related overtures (Overtures 15-18) on the National and World Councils of Churches. The 3rd General Assembly answered these overtures.
