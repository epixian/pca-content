Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 12 to the 2nd General Assembly on July 20, 1974. It asked the Assembly to tell the Committee of Mission to the World to end all ties and cooperation with any agency of the World Council of Churches. The overture was a single sentence. Related overtures gave the objections. North Georgia Presbytery's Overture 14 said that the World and National Councils of Churches "expressly support certain policies of Communism." Warrior Presbytery's overtures (Overtures 15-18) pointed to the church's repeated statements that it did not belong to or support the councils, and to the 1st General Assembly's instruction that its Committee on Mission to the United States do no joint work with member denominations. In the PCUS, conservative opposition to membership in the World Council had centered on its 1970 grant to groups that used violence against racism, the appointment of a Buddhist official at its Geneva headquarters, and its program to support American draft resisters in Canada. The 3rd General Assembly answered these overtures.
