Calvary Presbytery (Overture 11) and Westminster Presbytery (Overture 34) sent the same overture, "Call for Repentance and Reconciliation," to the 44th General Assembly. It rejected race-based slavery and legalized segregation as wicked. It called on all churches and members of the PCA who had promoted segregation, or who were indifferent to racial injustice, to repent. The 44th General Assembly answered both overtures by reference to its action on Potomac's Overture 43.
