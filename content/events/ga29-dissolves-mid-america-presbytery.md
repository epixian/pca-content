The 29th General Assembly answered an overture in the affirmative. North Texas Presbytery received the four churches of Mid-America Presbytery and all of its remaining Oklahoma counties on July 1, 2001. This dissolved Mid-America Presbytery. Two years before, the 27th General Assembly had moved seven Oklahoma counties along Interstate 35 to North Texas.
