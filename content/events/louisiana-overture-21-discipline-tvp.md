Louisiana Presbytery adopted Overture 21 to the 28th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to censure Tennessee Valley Presbytery and, if that presbytery did not act by the 29th Assembly, to take further steps under BCO 40-5 and BCO 40-6, including a trial on the floor of the Assembly. The Committee on Constitutional Business advised that the overture as worded was not consistent with the Constitution, because it pre-determined the guilt of a presbytery and prescribed censures. The overture index of the Minutes refers it only to the Committee on Constitutional Business and lists no Assembly vote on it.
