Following the grounds of the 41st General Assembly, North Texas Presbytery (Overture 2) and Tennessee Valley Presbytery (Overture 9) asked the 43rd General Assembly to erect a committee to consider changes to WCF 21.8, WLC 117 and 119, and WSC 60 and 61. Both overtures included possible revised wording. The Assembly defeated a motion to recommit and answered Overture 2 in the negative, 662-248-9. It answered Overture 9 by reference. The grounds said that the Standards are clear on the Sabbath and that men can already state their differences on recreation on the Sabbath.
