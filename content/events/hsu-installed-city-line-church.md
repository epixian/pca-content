City Line Church voted to call TE Jason Hsu, and Philadelphia Presbytery took up
the call at a called meeting. The Moderator ruled out of order questions about
the diaconal practices of City Church and City Line Church and about Hsu's views,
and the presbytery sustained the rulings. The presbytery approved the call, and
Hsu was later installed as pastor of City Line Church.
