Ohio Valley Presbytery's Overture 10, "Specify Good Faith Subscription - Version 2," asked the 30th General Assembly to amend BCO 21-4. A candidate would state the specific instances in which he differed with the Confession and Catechisms, and the court would judge each difference. Overtures 3, 11, 12, 24, 29, 31 and 32 from many other presbyteries asked for similar changes.
