The session of West End Presbyterian Church (Hopewell, Virginia) sent Overture 15 directly to the 51st General Assembly after James River Presbytery rejected it (RAO 11-10). It asked that BCO 7-2 say church offices are open to "biological men only." On the Overtures Committee's recommendation, the Assembly answered the overture in the negative. The committee's grounds said the Standards are already clear and that further definition of "men" would "accept the current cultural confusion on the issue."
