Pittsburgh Presbytery's Overture 29 asked the 46th General Assembly to form a committee of four teaching and four ruling elders to study the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America's report "Contemporary Perspectives on Sexual Orientation" and recommend it for adoption by the 47th General Assembly. The Overtures Committee said that the overture, as amended in committee, would have commended the report to the church, and that commissioners had likely not read it. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative (957-193-20).
