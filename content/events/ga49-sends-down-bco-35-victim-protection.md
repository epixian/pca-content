The 49th General Assembly answered Tennessee Valley Presbytery's Overture 2021-40 in the affirmative as amended, which sent amendments to BCO 35 on victim protection to the presbyteries. The chairman of the Ad Interim Committee on Domestic Abuse spoke in support, and the Assembly adopted the recommendation by unanimous voice vote. It referred the presbytery's companion overture on witness eligibility (2021-41) to the 50th General Assembly, 1794-49.
