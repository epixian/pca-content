In April 2008 Siouxlands Presbytery declined a motion by TEs Brian Carpenter and Wes White for a BCO 31-2 investigation of TE Greg Lawrence, and in September 2008 it denied their complaint. In Case 2008-14, White v. Siouxlands, the Standing Judicial Commission sustained the complaint 22-1. It sent the matter back with instructions to investigate whether TE Lawrence held or taught views on the covenant of works or other Federal Vision doctrines contrary to the Standards. A panel had proposed the decision in February 2009; the full commission adopted it in October 2009. By then the presbytery had already formed an investigative committee.
