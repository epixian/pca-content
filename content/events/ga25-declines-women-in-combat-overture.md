Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 13, "Opposition to Women in Combat," asked the 25th General Assembly to receive a rationale that only men should be military warriors and to urge national leaders to repent of the policy. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative. Its grounds said that the situation did not justify intervention in a political matter, that the biblical arguments were not entirely persuasive, and that questions remained, such as whether church courts could discipline women in combat.
