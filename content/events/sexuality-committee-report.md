The Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality published its report to the 48th General Assembly in May 2020. TE Bryan Chapell chaired the seven-member committee, whose other members were TE Kevin DeYoung, RE Derek Halvorson, RE Kyle Keating, TE Tim Keller, RE Jim Pocta and TE Jim Weidenaar. The report contained twelve statements and sections on temptation and sin, pastoral care and terminology, apologetics and a bibliography. It made no formal recommendations.
