Westminster Presbytery's Overture 11 asked the 12th General Assembly to declare the newly united Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.) apostate. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative. It said that specific cases of alleged apostasy should be decided by the appropriate lower court, and it referred to an RPCES study on apostasy.
