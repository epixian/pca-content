Philadelphia Presbytery and Rocky Mountain Presbytery sent Overture 19, "Endorse Marriage Amendment to US Constitution," to the 31st General Assembly. The overture said the PCA was reticent to address the civil magistrate (WCF 31-4) but had done so in extraordinary cases. It asked the Assembly to declare its support for a proposed constitutional amendment that defined marriage in the United States as the union of a man and a woman, to declare that support to the President, Congress and the state legislatures, and to encourage members to work as citizens for its adoption. The Minutes do not print the presbyteries' adoption dates.
