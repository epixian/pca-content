The 39th General Assembly adopted the amendments that the 38th General Assembly had deferred. One of them was the BCO 13-6 amendment from Ascension Presbytery's 2009 overture. The presbytery vote on this item was 73 for and none against. The amendment requires ordained ministers who come from other denominations to state their differences with the Confession of Faith and Catechisms.
