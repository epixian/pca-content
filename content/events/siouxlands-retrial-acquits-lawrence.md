Siouxlands Presbytery conducted the new trial itself, with 16 qualified judges, at its stated meeting of September 25, 2015. TE Greg Lawrence acted as his own counsel and testified. The court found him not guilty on each of the five charges. TE Art Sartorius, the prosecutor, complained; the presbytery denied the complaint in September 2016, and he carried it to the Standing Judicial Commission (Case 2016-16).
