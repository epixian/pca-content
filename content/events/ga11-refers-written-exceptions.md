The 11th General Assembly considered written exceptions to the Westminster Standards. It answered in the negative a proposed amendment that ordinands state their exceptions or reservations in writing, with a three-fourths vote of presbytery needed to accept them (Recommendation 4). It answered Grace Presbytery's Overture 11 for licentiates in the affirmative, with "scruple" changed to "exception or reservation" (Recommendation 44). It then answered St. Louis Presbytery's Overture 37 in the affirmative as amended, which sent both questions back to the Sub-committee on Judicial Business for one recommendation to the 12th General Assembly.
