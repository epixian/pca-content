The PCA Interchurch Relations Committee reported to the 22nd General Assembly on a meeting of the North American Presbyterian and Reformed Council (NAPARC) in Grand Rapids. NAPARC sent a letter that called on the Christian Reformed Church (CRC) to reverse the 1993 Synod action toward opening the offices of minister and ruling elder to women. It also formed a study committee on the CRC's grounds and offered a pastoral committee to help the CRC.
