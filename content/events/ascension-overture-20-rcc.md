Ascension Presbytery's Overture 20 asked the 26th General Assembly to declare that the Roman Catholic Church is not a true biblical Christian church. It also asked the Assembly to call members of that church who profess the biblical faith to leave it and join the PCA or a similar church. The Minutes do not print an adoption date for the overture.
