Committees of the PCA, the RPCES, and the Orthodox Presbyterian Church approved a joint statement on "joining and receiving," which was reported to the 9th General Assembly of the PCA in 1981. The statement said that, in receiving these denominations, the PCA recognized their history as part of her total history.
