Savannah River Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 9-2 so that the duties of deacons devolve upon the ruling elders only when a church lacks spiritually qualified men (BCO 9-3), "and not upon unordained persons within the church." The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
