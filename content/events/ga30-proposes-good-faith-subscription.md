The 30th General Assembly answered Ohio Valley Presbytery's Overture 10 in the affirmative as amended. It sent the proposed amendment of BCO 21-4 to the presbyteries. It answered Overtures 3, 11, 12, 24, 29, 31 and 32 by reference to this action. A minority report that asked for an ad interim committee instead was defeated, and many commissioners recorded negative votes. The Assembly answered Ohio Valley's Overture 4 in the negative.
