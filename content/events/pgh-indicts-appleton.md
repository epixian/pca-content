According to a copy posted online by a party that could not be identified, Pittsburgh Presbytery indicted TE Rick Appleton in a document dated June 13, 2026, with lines for the moderator and the stated clerk. Charge 1 charged a failure to adorn the gospel (BCO 21-5) in three groups of specifications: 24 social media posts from 2018 to 2026, public associations (including his presence at the RPCNA trial of Samuel Ketcham), and "obfuscating the truth" about that trial and about kinism. Charge 2 charged a violation of the third commandment in his teaching on women, in three specifications. These quoted his sermons, Sunday school lessons, a video, posts, and a meeting, from 2020 to 2026. The second specification dealt with his teaching on headship in marriage.
