Ascension Presbytery's Overture 39 asked the 22nd General Assembly to affirm that subscription to the Westminster Standards "is full and sincere," to declare "new revelation" an error that bars a man from office, and to repudiate drama and liturgical dance in worship. Overture 44, from the session of South Hills RPCA after Pittsburgh Presbytery rejected it, asked the same and named the 1993 response to the CoMission protest. The Assembly answered that the Constitution was sufficient testimony, and that those who found it insufficient should seek to amend it. It suggested that elders of differing views draft a new section of the BCO Preface, "Of the Church's Confession and Subscription."
