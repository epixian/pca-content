Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 20, "Opposition to Women in Combat," asked the 24th General Assembly to protest the national policy that let women serve in combat. Some members of the presbytery filed a dissent from the overture, which the minutes print with it. The Assembly answered that, without meddling in civil affairs, it had grave concern about sending women into combat, and it asked Philadelphia Presbytery to bring a more detailed recommendation to the 25th General Assembly.
