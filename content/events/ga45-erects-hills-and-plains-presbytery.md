The 45th General Assembly adopted overtures from Covenant and North Texas presbyteries to form a new presbytery, later named Hills and Plains. It took all of Oklahoma from North Texas, five northwest Arkansas counties from Covenant, and nineteen southwest Missouri counties. It took effect on July 1, 2017.
