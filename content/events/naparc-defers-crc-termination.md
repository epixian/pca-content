At NAPARC's 2000 meeting, after the CRC Synod of 2000 reaffirmed its decision on women in office, the PCA delegation moved that the CRC be expelled from NAPARC. The council adopted a substitute motion that delayed the vote until 2001 and asked the PCA to formulate grounds for the action, according to the PCA Interchurch Relations Committee's reports.
