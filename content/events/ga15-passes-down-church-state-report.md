The 15th General Assembly did not vote on the Church/State Subcommittee's report. A substitute motion to receive it and pass it down to the presbyteries for their consideration was adopted on June 15, 1987. A later amendment added the report's summary positions, to be studied by the presbyteries and submitted for adoption or rejection by the 16th General Assembly. A protest against passing the report down was received and spread on the Minutes.
