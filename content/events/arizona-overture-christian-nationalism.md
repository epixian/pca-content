Arizona Presbytery adopted an overture to the 52nd General Assembly to erect a study committee on Christian nationalism and "Theonomic Reconstructionism." It noted that the PCA's 1788 American revision of the Confession "represents a substantial change from the Historic Text (1646)" on church and magistrate, and it pledged a $2,500 gift.
