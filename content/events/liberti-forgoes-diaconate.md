In a response to the 37th General Assembly, Philadelphia Presbytery said its
organizing commission had chosen not to ordain Liberti's one elected man as a
deacon. It gave two reasons: ordained deacons are not required to organize a
church, and one ordained deacon did not make an organized diaconate. It
apologized for calling the commissioned people a "diaconate"; the 38th General
Assembly found the response satisfactory.
