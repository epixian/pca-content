The 16th General Assembly adopted, as amended, the summary positions of the Church/State Subcommittee, chaired by TE Douglas F. Kelly. The positions said that civil governments that "exceed" their power "and break the covenant" may be resisted and "in extreme cases, overthrown"; that the church should resist or pay under protest a tax that is "confiscatory"; and that a Christian who nonviolently intervenes to protect an unborn child is "worthy of the concerned and prayerful support" of other Christians. Commissioners recorded negative votes on two paragraphs and on the whole recommendation.
