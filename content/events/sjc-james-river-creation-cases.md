James River Presbytery sustained the ordination exam of a candidate who held that the six days of Genesis are "God's work days" and not necessarily 24-hour days. It later granted him an exception and instructed him not to teach or preach that view. In Case 98-1 the SJC denied a complaint against the exam, 15-6. In Case 98-5 it denied a complaint against the instruction not to teach, 13-8. Dissenting opinions were filed in both cases, and the decisions were reported to the 27th General Assembly.
