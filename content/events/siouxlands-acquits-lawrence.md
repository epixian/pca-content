The presbytery's judicial commission tried TE Greg Lawrence on September 12–14, 2011, and returned a unanimous verdict of not guilty on all five charges. He did not testify. On September 22, 2011, the presbytery adopted the judgments 24-6. A complaint against the verdict was denied in April 2012, and four teaching elders and three ruling elders carried it to the Standing Judicial Commission (Case 2012-08).
