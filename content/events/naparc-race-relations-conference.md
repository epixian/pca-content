The PCA's Committee on Interchurch Relations sent two representatives to a conference on race relations that NAPARC sponsored in Grand Rapids. The conference statement, which the RPCES printed in its 1977 synod minutes, said that the Reformed churches "have failed to speak and act boldly in the area of race relations." The PCA minutes of 1977 record only that the PCA sent representatives; in 2002 the 30th General Assembly printed parts of the statement with its own statement on racial reconciliation.
