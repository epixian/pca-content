The Overtures Committee recommended that Westminster Presbytery's Overture 15 be answered by reference to the new BCO 16-4. The 49th General Assembly instead adopted a minority report, first as the main motion (1094-1044) and then on final vote (1167-978). It sent to the presbyteries a new BCO 7-4: "Men who describe themselves as homosexual, even those who describe themselves as homosexual and claim to practice celibacy by refraining from homosexual conduct, are disqualified from holding office." Commissioners recorded their negative votes by name.
