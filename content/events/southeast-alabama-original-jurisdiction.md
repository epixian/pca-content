Southeast Alabama Presbytery adopted Overture 25 to the 48th General Assembly. It joined Savannah River and Central Georgia presbyteries in asking the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction over TE Greg Johnson, and it attached the BCO 31-2 report it had sent to Missouri Presbytery in August 2019.
