On the recommendation of the Interchurch Relations Committee of Commissioners, the 31st General Assembly answered Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 25 on church union conversations in the affirmative, and Ascension Presbytery's Overture 24 with reference to it. It asked the permanent Interchurch Relations Committee to bring to the 32nd Assembly a statement on church union, including a definition of "organic" union.
