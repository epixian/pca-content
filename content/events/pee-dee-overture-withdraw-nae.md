Pee Dee Presbytery adopted Overture 3 to the 49th General Assembly, asking it to withdraw the PCA from the National Association of Evangelicals "at the soonest possible date." Citing WCF 31 and BCO 3-3, it said that the NAE had "frequently intermeddled in civil affairs," and it named the NAE's 2018 "Fairness for All" motion on sexual orientation, gender identity and religious freedom. It also noted that no other NAPARC church was a member of the NAE.
