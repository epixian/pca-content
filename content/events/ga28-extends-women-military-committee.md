The committee, now chaired by TE Peter Lillback, gave an interim report to the 28th General Assembly. It said that it had not finished its work, in part because of significant discussion from divergent viewpoints among its members, and it listed the historical, exegetical, ecclesiological, and ethical questions under study. At the committee's request, the Assembly extended it for another year.
