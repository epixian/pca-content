In its review of presbytery minutes, the 9th General Assembly's Committee of Commissioners on Review and Control of Presbyteries cited Evangel Presbytery for postponing candidates' theology examinations over theonomy. It cited Gulf Coast Presbytery because "Theonomy" was "made an issue and test of orthodoxy" in a licensure transfer examination on January 10, 1981, "contrary to Seventh General Assembly pronouncement."
