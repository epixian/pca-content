Houston Metro Presbytery's Overture 8 asked the 49th General Assembly to amend BCO 33-1 and 34-1. As amended by the Overtures Committee, BCO 34-1 would require the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction over a minister when his presbytery does not indict in a doctrinal case or a case of public scandal and at least ten percent of presbyteries request it. Calvary Presbytery's Overture 9 asked for a similar change and was answered by reference to Overture 8. The Committee on Constitutional Business advised that the overture conflicted with the right of each court to resolve questions of doctrine. A minority report asked that the overture be referred back to the presbytery. The Assembly adopted the committee's recommendation (1245-521) and sent the amendment to the presbyteries.
