Chicago Metro Presbytery adopted Overture 42 to the 47th General Assembly, "Establish Study Committee on Sexuality." Its grounds said that the PCA was "not unified in how it should respond to present-day controversies surrounding human sexuality." Several other presbyteries sent overtures asking for a similar committee.
