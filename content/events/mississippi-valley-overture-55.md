Mississippi Valley Presbytery adopted Overture 55, "Confession of the Sin of Racism, and Commitment to Christian Unity," after discussing the matter at three meetings. With it the presbytery sent a pastoral letter to its churches, which said that "racism remains a current and ongoing pastoral issue." The 44th General Assembly later commended this letter to the churches as an example.
