Southwest Florida Presbytery adopted Overture 1 to the 54th General Assembly, "Amend BCO 7-3 & 7-4 regarding Titles, Offices, and Unordained Roles." It proposed that unordained people not be given the titles of the ordained offices, including their feminized forms. Its rationale said that some churches were causing confusion regarding church offices, cited savethepca.com as one public effort to document such practices, and said that the 53rd General Assembly had answered the related overtures by reference to Overture 37.
