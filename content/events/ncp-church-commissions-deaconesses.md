Northern California Presbytery had approved an organizing pastor's exception
about the diaconate when he transferred into the presbytery. He intended to elect
and install deacons without ordination, and to elect and install unordained
deaconesses. When his church was organized, its newly installed session
commissioned unordained men and women to a body that the presbytery's minutes of
March 3-4, 2006 called the diaconate.
