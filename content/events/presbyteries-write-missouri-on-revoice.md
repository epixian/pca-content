Between October 2018 and August 2019, five presbyteries wrote to Missouri Presbytery about Memorial's hosting of Revoice and about TE Greg Johnson: Calvary (October 25, 2018), Southwest Florida (November 13, 2018), Savannah River (January 26, 2019), Westminster (August 10, 2019) and Southeast Alabama (August 2019). Sessions in other presbyteries also wrote. Southeast Alabama's letter and Westminster's were among the four requests for a BCO 31-2 investigation of TE Johnson.
