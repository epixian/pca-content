Presbyterian Churchmen United (PCU) published the Declaration of Commitment, addressed to the members of the PCUS. The PCA Historical Center gives the date of publication as October 4, 1969, and says that more than 500 ministers signed it. The signers affirmed the Scriptures as "the infallible Word of God" and their vows to the Westminster Standards. They said they would oppose changes to the church's doctrine and polity and any move into the union planned by COCU, and that they would judge any proposed union by these principles. PCU held its first rally in Atlanta, and Christianity Today reported in January 1970 that PCU was then a chartered organization.
