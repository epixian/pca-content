After a panel heard testimony at Cedar Springs on September 16, 2000, the Standing Judicial Commission found no strong presumption of guilt against TE John Wood, so judicial process was not instituted. It concluded that what the woman said at the August 1998 evening services "crossed the line," but that the session had not intended her to preach and that this did not require process against Wood. It also found no clear evidence that his stated view, which excludes women from ordination and preaching but lets them do what unordained men may do, was outside the bounds of the Standards; the report went to the 29th General Assembly in June 2001.
