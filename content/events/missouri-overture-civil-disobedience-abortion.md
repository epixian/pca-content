Missouri Presbytery adopted Overture 16 to the 13th General Assembly. It cited Roe v. Wade and the duty to submit to governing authorities, and it said that Christians "may disobey civil authorities when necessary so that we may obey God." It asked the Assembly to promote pro-life work in the churches, to erect a committee to write a study paper on the history and theology of civil disobedience as it applies to abortion, and to counsel the churches on whether members should engage in civil disobedience and whether churches should discipline those who do.
