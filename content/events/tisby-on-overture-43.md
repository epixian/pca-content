In a piece from the Reformed African American Network that The Aquila Report reprinted, Jemar Tisby wrote as a black member of the PCA that Overture 43 was "different because it deals with the Civil Rights era." He wrote that it was not "accurate or charitable" to call the 123 elders who voted against it racists, and that the overture "will not abruptly change the culture of an entire denomination," though the Unity Fund and the study committee might bring change.
