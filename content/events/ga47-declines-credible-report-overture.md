Savannah River Presbytery's Overture 36 asked the 47th General Assembly to amend BCO 40-4 and 40-5 to say what counts as a "credible report" that lets a higher court cite a lower court. It noted that some read the term to cover any credible source, and others only a referral from a lower court. The Overtures Committee said BCO 40-5 needed clarification beyond the overture, and that the overture limited what a credible report could be. The Assembly answered it in the negative.
