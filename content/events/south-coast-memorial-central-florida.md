South Coast Presbytery sent a memorial (Overture 26) to the 28th General Assembly. It protested that Central Florida Presbytery had received, on April 18, 2000, a minister against whom South Coast charges were still outstanding, and it asked the Assembly to reverse that reception. The Committee on Constitutional Business agreed with the Stated Clerk to refer the memorial to the Standing Judicial Commission. It became Case 00-3, which was later withdrawn.
