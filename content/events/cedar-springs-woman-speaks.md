With the session's approval, the director of Women's Ministries at Cedar Springs Presbyterian Church spoke at its Sunday evening services on August 16 and 23, 1998, to explain her views and the church's women's ministries. At the first service, on August 16, there was no sermon by an elder. Western Carolina Presbytery later alleged that the church had had women preachers fill its pulpit.
