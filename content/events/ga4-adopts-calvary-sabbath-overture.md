Calvary Presbytery's Overture 5 said that the Sabbath had fallen into widespread profanation and disuse in American society, through athletic, commercial and social activities. The 4th General Assembly answered it in the affirmative with amendments. It reaffirmed its stand against all unnecessary encroachments upon the Lord's Day and exhorted all citizens to worship God and keep the day holy. It also exhorted each pastor and session to study how to bring the congregation's practice of Sabbath observance into greater conformity with the Standards.
