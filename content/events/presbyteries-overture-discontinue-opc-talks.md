Six presbyteries sent overtures to the 18th General Assembly with the title "Discontinue OPC Talks Until...": Palmetto (Overture 4), Southeast Alabama (Overture 5), Grace (Overture 9), Southern Florida (Overture 20), Evangel (Overture 24) and Central Georgia (Overture 25). They asked the PCA to stop negotiations on union with the Orthodox Presbyterian Church until the OPC asked to be received by joining and receiving under BCO 14-6. Southeast Alabama's overture, which Southern Florida and Evangel also adopted, said that a negotiated merger under a new constitution could take decades and disturb the peace of the church.
