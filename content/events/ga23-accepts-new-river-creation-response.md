New River Presbytery answered a 22nd General Assembly exception about a minister's exam by stating his views on creation, baptism and the diaconate. The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records recommended that the response be found not satisfactory. It also recommended that the presbytery re-examine him on six-day creation and on the office of deacon being limited to men. The 23rd General Assembly instead adopted a substitute, 315 to 229, that found the response satisfactory.
