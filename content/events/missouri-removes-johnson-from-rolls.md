At a called meeting, Missouri Presbytery dismissed Memorial and granted the requests of TE Greg Johnson and two other Memorial teaching elders to have their names removed from its rolls under BCO 38-3a. The presbytery had recently authorized a new BCO 31-2 investigation of TE Johnson, but it decided "not to conduct the case." Its public letter named, among the sources of tension, statements TE Johnson had made since the end of SJC Case 2020-12.
