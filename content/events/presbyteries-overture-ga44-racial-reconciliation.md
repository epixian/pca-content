About forty overtures on racial reconciliation came to the 44th General Assembly (2016). Four kinds of text stood out:

- Heartland Presbytery's Overture 1, "Steps Toward Racial Reconciliation," the first of them, asked the Assembly to exhort all members, especially officers, to examine themselves for "unconfessed and unrepentant sins of partiality, favoritism, or prejudice." It cited the 30th General Assembly's statement, the paper "The Gospel and Race," and a statement of the RPCES General Synod.
- Missouri Presbytery's Overture 4, "Confession of Sin and Commitment to Racial and Ethnic Diversity," asked the Assembly to "recognize and confess our church’s covenantal and generational involvement in and complicity with racial injustice inside and outside of our churches during the Civil Rights period". Many presbyteries sent the same text or a close version, and several sent overtures "In Support of" Overture 4. Heritage Presbytery's version (Overture 18) also rejected any teaching that a race bears "the curse of Cain".
- Calvary Presbytery (Overture 11) and Westminster Presbytery (Overture 34) sent the same "Call for Repentance and Reconciliation." It rejected race-based slavery and legalized segregation as wicked, and called those who had promoted segregation to repent.
- Northwest Georgia Presbytery's Overture 53, "Leading by Example in Repentance First," asked that the overtures calling for confession "be returned to their courts of original jurisdiction," under BCO 31-2 and 38-1.

The Assembly answered them by reference to its action on Potomac's Overture 43.
