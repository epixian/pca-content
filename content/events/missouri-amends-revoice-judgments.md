At a called meeting, Missouri Presbytery reconsidered the nine theological judgments of its May 2019 report. It adopted amended statements for eight of them and sent the ninth (on the Revoice lecture "Queer Treasure") to a new committee. The amended statements both affirmed and criticized parts of Revoice. TE Ryan Speck complained against these actions in January 2020.
