Savannah River Presbytery adopted Overture 25, "Address the Unordained Diaconate," on January 20, 2018. It cited Recommendation 4 of the 45th General Assembly and asked that presbyteries require congregations without a board of ordained deacons to report why. The 46th General Assembly adopted the Overtures Committee recommendation to answer the overture in the negative.
