Potomac Presbytery unanimously adopted Overture 43 to the 44th General Assembly. It said that during the Civil Rights period "there were founding denominational leaders and churches who not only failed to pursue racial reconciliation but also actively worked against it," and it asked the Assembly to "recognize, confess, and condemn these past and continuing racial sins." The overture did not use the word "repent" in its first resolution. At the same meeting the presbytery adopted Overtures 44 (a PCA Unity Fund) and 45 (a study committee).
