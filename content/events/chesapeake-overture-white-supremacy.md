Chesapeake Presbytery approved Overture 36 to the 48th General Assembly, which asked the Moderator to appoint a study committee on white supremacy. It said that "racism, more generally, and white supremacy, more particularly, are forms of the sin of partiality." In May 2021 the same presbytery adopted Overture 47, which asked for a study committee on critical race theory.
