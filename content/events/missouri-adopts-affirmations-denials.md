At a called meeting, Missouri Presbytery adopted 49 affirmations and denials from its committee (TEs Dan Doriani, Mark Dalbey and Ryan Laughlin and RE Sean Maney). They were in two parts: a short biblical theology of sexuality with reference to homosexuality, and homosexuality and identity in the current debate.
