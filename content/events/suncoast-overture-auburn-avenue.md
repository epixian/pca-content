Suncoast Florida Presbytery adopted Overture 22. It asked the 34th General Assembly to acknowledge "the heterodoxy" of the Auburn Avenue session and Louisiana Presbytery's error in declaring TE Steve Wilkins faithful to the Standards, and to establish a commission to discipline him and the session. It quoted the church's Summary Statement as teaching a covenant of grace made "with the elect and reprobate alike." With the advice of the Committee on Constitutional Business, the overture went to the Standing Judicial Commission.
