Nashville Presbytery's Overture 8 asked that BCO 24-1 require sessions, like presbyteries under BCO 21-4, to have ruling elder and deacon nominees state the specific instances in which they differ with the Confession of Faith and Catechisms, and to grant exceptions only for differences not out of accord with the fundamentals of the system of doctrine. The Overtures Committee recommended an amended version (97-31-0), and the 52nd General Assembly adopted it (1243-507), sending it to the presbyteries. The BCO 24-1 amendment listed among the nine amendments given final approval by the 53rd General Assembly is a different one, on a session's role in examining officer nominees (Overture 24).
