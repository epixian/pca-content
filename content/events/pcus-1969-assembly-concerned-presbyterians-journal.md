The 109th General Assembly of the PCUS adopted a statement that told Concerned Presbyterians to "speak the truth in love," after the group was accused of not promoting the "peace, unity, edification and purity" of the church. The Assembly also rejected an overture from the Presbytery of Northeast Texas to investigate the Presbyterian Journal, saying that it did not have "original jurisdiction over the accused parties."
