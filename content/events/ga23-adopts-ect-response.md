The 23rd General Assembly answered Pacific Northwest Presbytery's Overture 19 in the affirmative as amended. It adopted the statement on justification by faith alone (WCF 11). The statement said that the PCA's understanding of justification "is not compatible with the teaching of the official Roman Catholic Church." The Assembly sent it to the signers of "Evangelicals and Catholics Together."
