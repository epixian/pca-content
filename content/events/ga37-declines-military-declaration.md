Eastern Pennsylvania Presbytery adopted Overture 18, "A Declaration Concerning Homosexuals in the Military," on April 25, 2009. It asked the Assembly to send a letter to the President against a change in the policy on homosexuals in the armed forces. The 37th General Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said that the case was not extraordinary and that no civil magistrate had asked for such advice, so the overture did not meet the standard of Confession of Faith 31.4.
