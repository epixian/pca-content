New Jersey Presbytery (Overture 44) and Pittsburgh Presbytery (Overture 37) asked the 40th General Assembly to mark the 30th anniversary of the 1982 Joining and Receiving of the RPCES and to encourage church unity. The Assembly answered Overture 44 in the affirmative and answered Overture 37 by reference to it. During the session, a teaching elder gave thanks for the joining and receiving of the RPCES thirty years before.
