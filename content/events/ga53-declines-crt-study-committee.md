Calvary Presbytery's Overture 80 asked the 53rd General Assembly to erect an ad interim committee to study critical race theory from a Reformed exegetical, confessional and theological perspective, and to give pastoral guidance on it. The Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer (105-25-2), and the Assembly answered the overture in the negative.
