The 109th General Assembly of the PCUS proposed a joint committee of twelve members from each church to discuss differences with the United Presbyterian Church in the U.S.A. and, in the end, to negotiate union. It also changed its constitution so that PCUS presbyteries could join with neighboring United Presbyterian presbyteries, declined to withdraw from the Consultation on Church Union (COCU), and authorized a committee of ten, to be named by the moderator, to study a new Confession of Faith "together with a Book of Confessions."
