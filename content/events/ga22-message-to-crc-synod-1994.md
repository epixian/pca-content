The 22nd General Assembly adopted a substitute motion by a vote of 549 to 329. It stopped the Assembly's investigation of the Christian Reformed Church (CRC), except for its official acts. It told the Interchurch Relations Committee to recommend on fraternal relations if the CRC completed the process to open the offices of elder and minister to women. It also sent the CRC Synod of 1994 a message that asked it to reverse these steps.
