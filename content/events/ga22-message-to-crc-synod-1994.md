The 22nd General Assembly adopted a substitute motion by a vote of 549 to 329. It stopped the Assembly's investigation of the Christian Reformed Church (CRC), except for its official acts. If the CRC completed the process to open the offices of elder and minister to women, the Interchurch Relations Committee was to bring the next Assembly a recommendation on whether fraternal relations with the CRC should continue. It also sent the CRC Synod of 1994 a message that asked it to reverse these steps. Under this action, three overtures on the Interchurch Relations Committee's handling of the CRC (Westminster's Overture 29, Eastern Canada's Overture 15 and South Texas's Overture 43) were not recorded.
