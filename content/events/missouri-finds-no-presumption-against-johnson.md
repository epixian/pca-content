Missouri Presbytery adopted the recommendations of the committee that had investigated TE Greg Johnson under BCO 31-2. On each of four allegations it found no strong presumption of guilt and no warrant for a trial; the votes ranged from 44-1-4 to 41-6-4. It exhorted TE Johnson to take more care in how he expressed his views and declared him a member in good standing. At the same meeting it found fault with the Revoice lecture "Queer Treasure."
