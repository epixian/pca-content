On the recommendation of the Interchurch Relations Committee, the 28th General Assembly established two levels of relations with other denominations. Fraternal relations, with NAPARC voting members and other Presbyterian and Reformed churches, involve the exchange of fraternal delegates and minutes. Corresponding relations, with other evangelical churches, involve the exchange of greetings, letters and observers.
