The 27th General Assembly answered Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 27 with a substitute, adopted as amended. The Assembly reaffirmed "with united voice the sanctity of human life under the protection of the Sixth Commandment." It asked the Stated Clerk to remind the churches of the 6th General Assembly's report, to repeat several of that report's recommendations in his letter to the churches, and to appoint a day of prayer and fasting for the ending of abortion in 2000. It urged each presbytery to form a pro-life committee. The substitute did not include the overture's request for a new communication to government officials. Three teaching elders registered negative votes.
