Eastern Carolina Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to add a new chapter, BCO 17A, on commissioning: a public act by which a session or presbytery sets apart a member for a ministry role that does not involve the authority of the ordained offices. The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
