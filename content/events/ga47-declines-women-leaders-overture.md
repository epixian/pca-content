Northwest Georgia Presbytery's Overture 40 asked the 47th General Assembly to authorize churches to appoint the officers of their women's ministry "with the laying on of hands," to assist the deacons. It said that the change needed no constitutional amendment. The Overtures Committee said the overture was vague and "would create confusion around the doctrine of ordination." The Assembly answered it in the negative by common consent.
