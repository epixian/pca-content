Tennessee Valley Presbytery adopted Overture 40 to the 48th General Assembly, to amend BCO 32-13 and 35-5. In cases of alleged child abuse, domestic abuse, sexual abuse, or sexual assault, a court could make reasonable accommodations, such as written or videoconference testimony, to shield accusers from face-to-face contact with the accused, while it gave a path for cross-examination. The presbytery also sent Overture 41, to widen who may be a witness under BCO 35-1. The 48th General Assembly committed both overtures to the next Overtures Committee and referred them to the ad interim committee on abuse for advice.
