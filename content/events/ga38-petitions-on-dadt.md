The 38th General Assembly answered Overtures 12, 17 and 22 in the affirmative. It adopted the request of the Presbyterian and Reformed Joint Commission on Chaplains and Military Personnel and directed the Stated Clerk to petition the Secretary of Defense, the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, the service chiefs and the President with a letter. The letter said that the PCA "is gravely concerned over the potential repeal of the current law governing homosexual individuals in the military." The Assembly adopted this recommendation as a substitute and then as the main motion. At the 39th General Assembly (2011), the Stated Clerk reported on his communication with the President.
