On the first day of the 10th General Assembly, the Assembly heard that the General Synod of the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod had approved the joining and receiving by 322 to 90. The PCA presbyteries had voted 25 to 0 for it. The certified RPCES delegates were then declared members of the General Assembly.
