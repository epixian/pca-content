The Stated Clerk referred Westminster Presbytery's Overture 3 to the Ad Interim Committee on Women Serving in the Ministry of the Church. The committee's Recommendation 1 was to answer the overture in the negative. Before the report was presented, a point of order said that the report violated a polity principle because the committee had no ruling elders; the Moderator ruled it not well taken, and the Assembly sustained the ruling (781-299). The 45th General Assembly then adopted Recommendation 1 (941-307).
