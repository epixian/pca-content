In Case 2012-08, Sartorius et al. v. Siouxlands, the Standing Judicial Commission sustained the complaint against TE Greg Lawrence's acquittal, 18-1. It held that the presbytery's judicial commission had received what was in effect his testimony in writing while he declined to testify and to be cross-examined. It ordered a new trial on the same indictment, at which such written statements could be used only if he testified. The decision also asked the presbyteries to study how a minister's right not to testify fits his duty to explain his views.
