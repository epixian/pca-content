Earlier Assemblies had asked the Committee on Judicial Business for wording by which candidates would record scruples, in answer to overtures from Missouri and Grace presbyteries. The 13th General Assembly directed the committee to develop "unified language" for licentiates and ordinands to record scruples, and it answered Missouri Presbytery's Overture 3 by reference to that action. In 1986 the 14th General Assembly dismissed the committee from the assignment.
