TE Mike Sloan brought a personal resolution on child sexual abuse to the 41st General Assembly, which received it by a two-thirds vote. The Overtures Committee proposed a substantially redrafted "Statement on Child Sexual Abuse" for adoption and distribution to presbyteries and sessions. After TE Sloan spoke in favor of recommitting it, the Assembly recommitted the statement to the Overtures Committee of the 42nd General Assembly.
