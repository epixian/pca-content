Tennessee Valley Presbytery adopted Overture 16, "Affirm Unordained Deaconesses."
It noted that the RPCES had allowed congregations to appoint women as deaconesses
and that some PCA churches had continued the practice since Joining and Receiving.
It asked the Assembly to affirm that unordained deaconesses may serve the church.
