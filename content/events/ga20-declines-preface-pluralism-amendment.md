Westminster Presbytery's Overture 10 to the 19th General Assembly asked to rewrite the last sentence of BCO Preface II(1). The overture said the current text "seems to support pluralism by equating all religious constitutions." At the 19th General Assembly, the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures recommended the affirmative, as amended. The 19th General Assembly instead referred the overture to the Committee on Constitutional Business and deferred it to the 20th General Assembly.

At the 20th General Assembly, as Overture 31, the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures recommended the affirmative, citing WCF 23. A substitute motion to answer the overture in the negative passed. The Minutes print no adoption line for the overture.
