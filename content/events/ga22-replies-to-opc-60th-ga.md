The 60th General Assembly of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church (1993) wrote that it "deeply regrets" the PCA's 1992 decision to consider no method of union other than joining and receiving, and that the decision cut off further efforts toward union for the present. The 22nd General Assembly answered by a vote of 412 to 308. It referred the OPC to the 1992 action, which asked the OPC to take its constitutional steps to be received into the PCA, and it noted the PCA's experience of joining and receiving with the RPCES in 1982. Four teaching elders recorded negative votes.
