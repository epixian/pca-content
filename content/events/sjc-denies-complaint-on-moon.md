TE Joshua Moon had led the September 2009 motions that cleared TE Greg Lawrence and had defended them in a paper. Foothills Community Church asked Siouxlands Presbytery to investigate him. In October 2009, after he denied the views alleged and answered questions, the presbytery found no strong presumption of guilt. In Case 2010-04, Sartorius et al. v. Siouxlands, the Standing Judicial Commission denied the complaint 19-1. It held that the presbytery had been diligent and that, without clear error, the commission "must defer" to its judgment of his credibility. The minutes print no date for the decision. The commission met on June 29 and October 21, 2010, and in the spring of 2011 (the report misprints "March 3, 2010").
