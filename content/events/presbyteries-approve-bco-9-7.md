The presbyteries voted 71 for and 2 against the amendment to BCO 9-7 (Item 5).
Philadelphia Presbytery voted for it 19-0-4. Metro Atlanta Presbytery (24-43-4)
and Chicago Metro Presbytery (8-15-0) voted against it.
