On the first evening of the 43rd General Assembly, TE Ligon Duncan and TE Sean Michael Lucas brought a "Personal Resolution on Civil Rights Remembrance." It said that the 2002 statement and the 2004 pastoral letter had not dealt with the Civil Rights era, and it asked the Assembly to "recognize and confess our church's covenantal and generational involvement in and complicity with racial injustice" in that period. By a two-thirds vote the Assembly received it and referred it to the Overtures Committee.
