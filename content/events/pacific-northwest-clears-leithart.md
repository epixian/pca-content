After the 35th General Assembly, TE Peter Leithart wrote to Pacific Northwest Presbytery to set out his views on the nine declarations. At his request and the request of another presbyter, the presbytery appointed a study committee in October 2007 to examine his fitness to continue as a PCA teaching elder. In October 2008 the presbytery adopted the committee's report that his views were "not out of accord with the fundamentals of our system of doctrine," and not the minority report, which found them out of accord. A complaint against this action was denied, and the presbytery confirmed the denial in April 2009.
