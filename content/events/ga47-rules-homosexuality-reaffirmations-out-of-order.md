Savannah River Presbytery's Overture 35 asked the 47th General Assembly to reaffirm the 5th General Assembly's 1977 statement on homosexuality and other past actions. Southern New England Presbytery's Overture 39 asked it to re-commend the PCA's earlier statements and study reports, including the RPCES report "Pastoral Care for the Repentant Homosexual." On the Overtures Committee's recommendation, the Moderator ruled both overtures out of order, because a motion to reaffirm a position previously taken "serves no useful purpose" while the original action stands.
