The Ad Interim Committee on Domestic Abuse and Sexual Assault presented its report to the 49th General Assembly. Its chairman said that the report was not a denominational assessment but a theological, biblical, and pastoral study of abuse, with pastoral and practical applications. The Assembly dismissed the committee with thanks.
