Pacific Presbytery's Overture 37 asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 9-3 so that sessions could decide whether qualified women may be ordained deacons. The Overtures Committee recommended answering it in the negative (115-14-1), and it recommended answering eight related overtures, including Southwest Florida's Overture 14, by reference to Overture 37 (110-23). The Assembly adopted the recommendation on Overture 37, and four teaching elders filed a dissent. Southwest Florida Presbytery later wrote that the related overtures were answered by reference to Overture 37.
