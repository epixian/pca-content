Thomas Rickard, a PCA teaching elder, published "A Brotherly Plea to Cease Using the Title 'Deaconess'" on the Presbyterian Polity website. He gave several arguments why churches that commission non-ordained deaconesses should reconsider the practice, and he referred to the recent change to BCO 7-3.
