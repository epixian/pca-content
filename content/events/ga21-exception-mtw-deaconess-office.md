On review of the minutes of the Committee on Mission to the World, the 21st General Assembly took exception to approved team planning documents. The documents identified "deaconess" as a church office, which the Assembly said was contrary to BCO 7-2. In its response to the 22nd General Assembly, the committee said that the field had amended the documents.
