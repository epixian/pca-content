Fellowship Presbytery's Overture 32 asked the 42nd General Assembly to warn the church against teachers who promote evolution, undermine the historicity of Genesis 1-3, or deny the special, immediate creation of Adam and Eve. It also asked sessions and presbyteries to ask about the historical Adam and Eve in ordination exams. After debate, the Assembly answered the overture in the negative. The grounds said that an in thesi statement might be read as an amendment to the constitution, and that the 28th General Assembly had already adopted a statement on these issues.
