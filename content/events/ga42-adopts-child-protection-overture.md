The 42nd General Assembly answered Georgia Foothills Presbytery's Overture 6 in the affirmative as amended, on the Overtures Committee's recommendation (committee vote 89-0-1). The adopted text exhorted church leaders to screen and train staff and volunteers, said that child sexual abuse must be reported to the civil authorities in accordance with local laws, and directed the permanent committees and agencies to review their child protection policies and report to the 43rd General Assembly. The concurring overtures were answered with reference to Overture 6. TE Mike Sloan then led the Assembly in prayer for child protection efforts.
