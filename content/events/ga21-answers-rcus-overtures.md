The 21st General Assembly answered in the negative Overture 31 from Mid-America Presbytery and Overture 36 from Louisiana Presbytery. Both overtures asked the PCA delegates to NAPARC to vote for the reception of the Reformed Church in the United States (RCUS). The Assembly also adopted a statement that its answer was not to be interpreted as making or countenancing any negative remarks about the RCUS.
