A Tennessee Valley Presbytery committee recommended an exception of substance against a session whose minutes noted a youth group Super Bowl party on a Sunday, under WLC 117-118 and WCF 21.8. The presbytery instead approved the minutes without exception and denied a complaint against that action. The SJC denied the complaint, 18-1, because the record gave no grounds for the presbytery's action and so did not show error. A concurring opinion said that the decision did not address the underlying question of Sabbath observance. The decision is printed in the Minutes of the 51st General Assembly.
