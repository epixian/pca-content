At a called congregational meeting, Memorial Presbyterian Church in St. Louis voted to withdraw from the PCA. Missouri Presbytery later described the departure as a "mutually agreed separation" after years of tension over Memorial's outreach to people who experience same-sex attraction and its involvement with Revoice.
