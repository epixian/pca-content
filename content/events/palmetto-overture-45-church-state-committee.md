Palmetto Presbytery adopted Overture 45 to the 12th General Assembly on January 26, 1984. It cited the Nebraska church cases, the Bob Jones University decision and the new Social Security tax on churches. It asked the Assembly to appoint a special committee to inform the churches on church/state issues and to build a network of reporters in the states. The 12th General Assembly answered it in the negative on the ground that "such information is available."
