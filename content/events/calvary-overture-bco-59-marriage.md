Calvary Presbytery adopted Overture 2 to the 45th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to give BCO 59, "The Solemnization of Marriage," full constitutional authority. Its whereas clauses noted that the United States and Canada had made same-sex marriage legal, and said that the BCO had no statement with constitutional authority that prohibited same-sex marriage. The Overtures Committee recommended the overture in the affirmative as amended, but the 45th General Assembly recommitted it to the Overtures Committee of the 46th General Assembly (591-246-17).
