Northern California Presbytery adopted Overture 10 to the 38th General Assembly.
It proposed amendments to BCO 1-4, 4-2, 5-10, 7-2, 9-2, and 9-7 and a new BCO 9-8.
The new section would let a session without ordained deacons appoint an
unordained body of men and women to carry out diaconal ministry.
