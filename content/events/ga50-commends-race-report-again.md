As the 46th General Assembly had directed, Mission to North America worked with LifeWay Research to repeat the 2017 survey of elders. In 2022, 87% (81% in 2017) said the PCA needs a determined commitment to address racial problems, but 53% (65% in 2017) said PCA leaders very much need to work toward greater reconciliation. On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Mission to North America, the 50th General Assembly commended the 2018 report and its recommendations to the church again.
