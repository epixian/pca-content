The 40th General Assembly adopted overtures that dissolved Louisiana Presbytery and divided its area among four presbyteries. North Texas received nine northwest parishes. Mississippi Valley received the northeast parishes, and Southeast Louisiana received the rest. Covenant Presbytery received the four Arkansas counties that Louisiana Presbytery had held, so that it included the whole state of Arkansas.
