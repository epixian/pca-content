Chesapeake Presbytery asked for a study committee on ministry amid ethnic diversity. Instead of a committee, the 30th General Assembly asked the staff of Mission to North America to explore the challenges of "the increasing ethnic diversity of our nation," to suggest ways to meet them, and to offer a statement of biblical values, with a report to the 31st General Assembly. In 2003 the 31st General Assembly adopted MNA's response. It urged presbyteries and churches to give "intentionally high priority" to culturally sensitive ministries among all people groups, and it commended an MNA working paper, "Ministering Among the People Groups of North America," to the churches.
