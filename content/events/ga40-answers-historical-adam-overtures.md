Potomac Presbytery's Overture 26 asked the 40th General Assembly to answer all overtures that sought an in thesi statement on evolution and Adam by reference to earlier Assemblies that declined abstract declarations. The Assembly answered Overture 26 in the affirmative. Its grounds said that Scripture and the Westminster Standards are already clear that Adam and Eve are real, historical human beings directly created by God. It answered Overtures 10 (Rocky Mountain) and 29 (Savannah River) by reference to this action. A minority report to adopt Overture 10 instead was defeated 312-475.
