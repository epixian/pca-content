James River Presbytery (Overture 5) and Susquehanna Valley Presbytery (Overture 10) asked the 37th General Assembly to appoint a study committee on the role of women in the church. James River's overture said that "many PCA churches are uncertain about how to use appropriately God’s gifts among the many capable women" in them.
