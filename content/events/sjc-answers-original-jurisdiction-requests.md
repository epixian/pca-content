The Standing Judicial Commission had held the three 2020 requests for original jurisdiction until Missouri Presbytery's own process ended, because BCO 34-1 applies only when a presbytery "refuses to act." In Case 2020-02 it answered them by reference to its decisions in the two Speck cases. RE Mel Duncan recorded his negative vote.
