The Committee on Women in Church Office reported to the 55th General Assembly of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church (OPC). The committee concluded that women may not be ordained as deacons, and a minority report by Robert Strimple concluded that they may. The Assembly asked presbyteries and sessions to study both reports and dissolved the committee.
