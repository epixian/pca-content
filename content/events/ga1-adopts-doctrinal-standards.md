The first General Assembly of the National Presbyterian Church (later the PCA) adopted as its doctrinal standards "the Westminster Confession of Faith as proposed by the Steering Committee" and "the unamended Westminster Larger and Shorter Catechisms." The text of the Confession printed in the minutes has the American form of chapter 23.3, under which civil magistrates protect the church "without giving the preference to any denomination of Christians above the rest."
