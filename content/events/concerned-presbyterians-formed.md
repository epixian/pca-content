Laymen in the PCUS formed Concerned Presbyterians, Inc. as a non-profit corporation in the early 1960s. The source for this is a 1993 personal resolution by its president, Kenneth S. Keyes, and its secretary, W. Jack Williamson, which the 21st PCA General Assembly ordered spread upon its minutes. The resolution called the group "a major contributor to the eventual formation of the PCA."
