Laymen in the PCUS formed Concerned Presbyterians, Inc. in the early 1960s as a non-profit corporation, with ministerial advisors, to combat liberalism in the denomination. In a 1993 personal resolution, its president, Kenneth S. Keyes, and its secretary, W. Jack Williamson, called the group "a major contributor to the eventual formation of the PCA." Williamson had moderated the 1st PCA General Assembly in 1973. The 21st General Assembly ordered the resolution spread upon its minutes.
