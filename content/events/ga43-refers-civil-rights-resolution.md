On the Overtures Committee's recommendation, the 43rd General Assembly referred the resolution to the 44th General Assembly (684-46-16). The committee's grounds, written after consultation with the African-American Presbyterian Fellowship, asked for time to perfect the resolution and said that "matters of corporate repentance ought to come through lower courts of the Church rather than by personal resolutions." A motion to recommit the resolution to the Overtures Committee failed (214-541-9), and a motion to suspend the rules to admit a new personal resolution failed (299-455-8). After the vote the Assembly held an extended season of prayer, and a protest signed by over 200 commissioners confessed the church's complicity "with racial injustice inside and outside our churches during the Civil Rights period."
