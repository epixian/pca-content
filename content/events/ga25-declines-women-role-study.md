South Coast Presbytery adopted an overture on April 26, 1997, that asked for a church-wide study committee to write a statement on the role of women in the church. Its reasons included egalitarian views in evangelical churches and practices that, it said, might one day divide the PCA. The 25th General Assembly answered the overture in the negative, on the ground that the Book of Church Order should continue to give guidance on the role of women.
