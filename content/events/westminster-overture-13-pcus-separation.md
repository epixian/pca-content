Westminster Presbytery sent Overture 13 to the 5th General Assembly. It said there was "unmistakable apostasy" in the Presbyterian Church in the United States. It asked the Assembly to declare that re-affiliation by a PCA member with the PCUS, or with any member body of the National or World Council of Churches, violated the doctrine of separation from apostasy. Sessions and presbyteries could then not transfer members to such bodies. The Minutes do not print the date on which the presbytery adopted the overture.
