The presbyteries voted on the amendments that the 49th General Assembly had sent down. The new BCO 16-4 on officer qualifications passed with 79 presbyteries for and 1 against, and the revised BCO 21-4 and 24-1 examination amendment passed 76-4. The BCO 7-4 amendment, which would have disqualified men who describe themselves as homosexual, received 48 for and 32 against, short of the 59 required. These are the corrected totals printed in the 51st Assembly's minutes; the 50th Assembly's minutes had printed earlier counts (for 7-4, 39-29).
