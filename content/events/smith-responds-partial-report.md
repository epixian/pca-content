In Ad Fontes, Miles Smith called the partial report "undoubtedly the most serious document on the subject written in the 21 st century." He wrote that it conflates the magistrate's concern for piety with establishmentarianism, and that "Not every conversation can be, or should be, about race and antisemitism."
