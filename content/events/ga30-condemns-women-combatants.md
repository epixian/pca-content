The 30th General Assembly adopted the committee majority's four final recommendations as pastoral counsel. They declared that any policy that places as combatants women who are or might be carrying a child violates God's moral law, declared it the biblical duty of man to defend woman, condemned the use of women as military combatants and any conscription of women, and supported members who object to these on grounds of conscience. A minority report, which said the grounds were plausible but not binding on all members, was offered as a substitute and defeated; the minutes list 77 commissioners who recorded negative votes. On June 21 a motion to send the report to the President of the United States failed.
