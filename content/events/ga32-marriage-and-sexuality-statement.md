The 32nd General Assembly answered Missouri Presbytery's Overture 16 in the affirmative as amended. It answered Overtures 12 to 15, which asked it to support the proposed Marriage Amendment to the US Constitution, and two personal resolutions by reference to that answer. As adopted, the answer called on the civil governments of Canada, the United States and all nations to ensure that marriage is legally defined as between one man and one woman. The printed page shows that the Assembly struck the words "(even constitutional amendment, if deemed necessary)" and the word "federal" from the overture's call. On the floor the Assembly added a resolve that called on members, as citizens, to protect and defend the biblical teaching on marriage. The Stated Clerk later reported that he sent the actions to those who had inquired, including a senator and a member of Congress.
