Calvary Presbytery sent two overtures against collecting data on age or ethnicity. On June 24, Overture 43, an amendment to RAO 4-11, did not reach the required two-thirds vote (1005-623). On Overture 42 the Administrative Committee had recommended a negative answer (Appendix A), and its grounds cited the 2018 report's call for a longitudinal study and said that "forbidding all such data collection is counter to the instruction of previous Assemblies." The Committee of Commissioners struck that answer and the words "age or," and the Assembly adopted its version (1184-361): the Office of the Stated Clerk is not to collect statistical data on ethnicity in the annual statistical reports. The Administrative Committee's grounds say that the Stated Clerk had only told churches that such data would be requested in the future.
