The 30th General Assembly answered in the affirmative Personal Resolution 2, brought by TE Henry Lewis Smith and six other commissioners. The resolution called all under the Assembly's care "to repent of and renounce any racism and/or class-consciousness." It encouraged local churches to make known that their worship and fellowship are open "to warmly welcome all persons without regard to race, class or national origin."
