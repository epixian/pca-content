Messengers to the 2023 SBC annual meeting upheld the Executive Committee's decision that Saddleback Church and Fern Creek Baptist Church were not in friendly cooperation with the convention. In both cases, the reason was the church's women pastors, which the committee judged contrary to the Baptist Faith and Message 2000. The vote on Saddleback was 88.46 percent in favor.
