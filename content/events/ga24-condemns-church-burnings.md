A personal resolution (Personal Resolution 2), "Response to Recent Church Burnings," came to the 24th General Assembly. It noted that numerous African-American churches had been burned in recent years, and it called racism a grievous sin. It asked the Assembly to issue a public statement that decried the burnings. It also asked the presbyteries where the burnings occurred to help the victims. The Assembly answered the resolution with a statement that condemned the burning of all church buildings. The statement encouraged congregations and presbyteries to express concern for the churches that had suffered these losses.
