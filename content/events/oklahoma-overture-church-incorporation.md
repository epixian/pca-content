Oklahoma Presbytery adopted Overture 24 to the 13th General Assembly. It noted that the PCA was registered as a corporation in Delaware, that a corporation is under the jurisdiction of a state, and that "the church of Jesus Christ is under no jurisdiction of human government." It asked for a committee to consider the theological implications of the PCA remaining incorporated.
