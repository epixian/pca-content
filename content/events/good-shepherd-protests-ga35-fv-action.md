The session of Good Shepherd Presbyterian Church in Minnetonka, Minnesota, wrote to the Stated Clerk to protest the Assembly's action of June 13, 2007. It argued that the report did not address the biblical arguments of Federal Vision proponents and did not allow for teaching "on the basis of Scripture that go beyond the Westminster standards." The Stated Clerk sent the letter to the 36th General Assembly as information (Communication 1).
