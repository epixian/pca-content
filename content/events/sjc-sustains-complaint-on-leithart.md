In Case 2009-6, Bordwine et al. v. Pacific Northwest Presbytery, the Standing Judicial Commission sustained the complaint 17-2. It held that without judicial process the presbytery could not declare TE Peter Leithart's views either in or out of accord with the Standards. It found that his views on baptism, the bi-covenantal structure of Scripture and imputation "suggest a strong presumption of guilt." The presbytery could counsel him to recant or to move to "some other branch of the visible church"; otherwise it was to act under BCO 31-2 before its fall meeting. The minutes print no date for the decision; the commission met in October 2009, January 2010 and March 2010.
