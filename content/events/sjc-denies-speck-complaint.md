TE Ryan Speck complained against Missouri Presbytery's July 2020 decision not to indict TE Greg Johnson; the presbytery denied the complaint 43-6-1, and he carried it to the Standing Judicial Commission. The full commission heard the case on March 25, 2021, and then sent TE Johnson 25 written questions to complete the record. On October 21, 2021, it denied the complaint 16-7. It held that it "was not unreasonable" for the presbytery to judge his explanations satisfactory. RE Jay Neikirk and TE Guy Waters wrote a concurring opinion; RE Samuel J. Duncan and RE Steve Dowling wrote dissenting opinions.
