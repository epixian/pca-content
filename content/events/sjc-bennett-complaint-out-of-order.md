TE M. Jay Bennett complained to Missouri Presbytery on April 16, 2012, after the Meyers trial, and carried the complaint to the Standing Judicial Commission on August 16, 2012 (Case 2012-09). The commission found the case administratively out of order. Bennett had been received by the Orthodox Presbyterian Church on April 28, 2012, so he had no standing to bring a complaint to the commission. The commission did not reach the merits.
