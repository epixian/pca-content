Northwest Georgia Presbytery adopted Overture 26 on April 4, 2023, to amend BCO 7-3 on the titles of unordained people. The Overtures Committee amended it to say that unordained people shall not be referred to as, or given the titles of, the ordained offices of pastor, elder, or deacon. The 50th General Assembly adopted the committee's recommendation to answer the overture in the affirmative as amended (1427-481-20).
