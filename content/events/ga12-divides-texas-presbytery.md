The 12th General Assembly granted a Texas Presbytery overture to divide the presbytery along a line of named counties, from Schleicher in the west to Shelby in the east. The named counties stayed in the northern presbytery. The division took effect on January 1, 1985. North Texas Presbytery became the successor of Texas Presbytery, and South Texas Presbytery was the new body.
