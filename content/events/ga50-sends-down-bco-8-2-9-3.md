The 50th General Assembly answered Mississippi Valley Presbytery's Overture 23 in the affirmative as amended (1673-223-15) and sent it to the presbyteries. It added to the qualifications for elders (BCO 8-2) that an elder "should conform to the biblical requirement of chastity and sexual purity in his descriptions of himself, and in his convictions, character, and conduct," and similar words for deacons (BCO 9-3). Four other overtures, including Arizona Presbytery's on BCO 7, were answered by reference to it.
