Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 3 to the 45th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to acknowledge that the 44th General Assembly had erred in forming the ad interim committee on women in the ministry of the church, not to receive the committee's report, and to dismiss the committee "with apology." Its whereas clauses said that the proposal had not come from a lower court, and objected that the committee had women as members. The Stated Clerk referred the overture to the ad interim committee.
