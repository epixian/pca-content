The PCA Interchurch Relations Committee reported that NAPARC had postponed the Evangelical Presbyterian Church's application since 1988 over three questions, one of them the role of women in the ruling and teaching offices. At its 1995 meeting NAPARC heard that the EPC had not substantially changed its position on women in office, and it voted 4 to 3 to postpone the application again until 1996.
