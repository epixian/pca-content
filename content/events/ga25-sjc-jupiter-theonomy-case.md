In 1994 Southern Florida Presbytery did not sustain the theology exam of a teaching elder who sought to transfer from Great Lakes Presbytery. The question was his view of Old Testament judicial law under WCF 19-4. The Jupiter church session complained, and in Case 95-5 the Standing Judicial Commission found procedural errors in the presbytery's handling of the complaint and returned the case for a new hearing. The opinion did not address the substantive issue. A concurring opinion noted that the presbytery had declined to state that theonomic views were the basis of its vote. The 25th General Assembly approved the judgment.
