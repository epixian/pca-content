Potomac Presbytery's Overture 26 asked the 49th General Assembly to condemn political violence and intimidation, "especially that which is illicitly done in the name of Christ." Its whereas clauses said that Christian symbols had appeared at some violent events, causing an "unhelpful conflation of the Kingdom of Christ with unlawful and violent actions." The Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer; its grounds said that the matter was better dealt with locally and, citing BCO 3-3, that the overture "seems unwise and unnecessary." A minority report to answer it in the affirmative as amended failed (489-1047), and the Assembly answered the overture in the negative (1108-371). The Moderator then asked a commissioner to pray about political violence in the country.
