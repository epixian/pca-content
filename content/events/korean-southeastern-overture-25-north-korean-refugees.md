Korean Southeastern Presbytery adopted Overture 25, "Support North Korean Refugees," for the 28th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to write official letters to the United Nations and to the President of the United States asking for protection and refugee camps for people escaping from North Korea. It also asked for a time of prayer at the Assembly, a prayer sign-up sheet for commissioners, and a letter to each local church asking for prayer.
