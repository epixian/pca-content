The 49th General Assembly answered Pittsburgh Presbytery's Overture 29 in the affirmative as amended (1922-200) and sent a new BCO 16-4 to the presbyteries. It said that, to be qualified for office, officers "must affirm the sinfulness of fallen desires, the reality and hope of progressive sanctification," and be committed to pursuing victory over sinful temptations. A motion to recommit failed 96-2023. In an in gross vote (2062-33) the Assembly also sent down Pittsburgh's Overture 31, a revised amendment to BCO 21-4 and 24-1 on examinations, and answered several similar overtures by reference to Overture 29.
