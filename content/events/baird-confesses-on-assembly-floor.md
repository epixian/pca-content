In debate on the Civil Rights resolution, TE Jim Baird of Mississippi Valley Presbytery said that he was one of twelve men elected in 1971 to form the new denomination. He said, "I confess that I did not raise a finger for civil rights," and asked that the matter go to every session and presbytery. The transcript was made by a commissioner and is kept by the PCA Historical Center; the minutes do not record the speech.
