Missouri Presbytery had formed a study committee in July 2004 on "the federalist vision, the new perspective, new thinking on the sacraments" and related issues. On January 18, 2006, it adopted the committee's report, with affirmations, denials and suggested examination questions on covenant and election, justification, union with Christ, and the sacraments. The report called itself "an instructional tool and guide" for the presbytery. The presbytery then asked the 34th General Assembly to commend it (Overture 26).
