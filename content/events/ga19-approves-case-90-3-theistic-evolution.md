A church member complained that the session of Grace Covenant Presbyterian Church, Blacksburg, VA, let a member teach youth while he held views on creation and theistic evolution. New River Presbytery sustained the complaint and said that theistic evolution could not be allowed as an exception for teaching or ordination. On the church's complaint, the SJC panel found that the presbytery did not err in its application of the standards. It did not send the case back to the session, but advised the church that the teacher should not be given authority to teach while he held such exceptions. The 19th General Assembly approved the judgment.
