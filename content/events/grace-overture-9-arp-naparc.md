Grace Presbytery adopted Overture 9 to the 5th General Assembly on July 8, 1977. It noted that the Associate Reformed Presbyterian Church had a fraternal relation with the United Presbyterian Church in the U.S.A. It asked the Assembly to direct its interchurch delegates to vote against the ARP's application for membership in NAPARC.
