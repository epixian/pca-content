The presbyteries voted on the amendment to BCO 7-3 that the 50th General Assembly had sent down. It says that unordained people shall not be called by, or given, the titles of the ordained offices of pastor/elder or deacon. Of 88 presbyteries, 79 reported: 65 voted for it and 14 against, more than the two-thirds needed.
