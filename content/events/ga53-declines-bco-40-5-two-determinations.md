Potomac Presbytery's Overture 67 asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 40-5 so that a higher court, on a credible report about a lower court, must first decide whether the matter, if true, would be an important delinquency or grossly unconstitutional proceeding, and whether a credible source gives sufficient evidence, before citing the lower court. The Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer (101-28-3), and the Assembly answered the overture in the negative.
