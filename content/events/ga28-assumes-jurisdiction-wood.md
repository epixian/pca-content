The Standing Judicial Commission had judged the presbyteries' request not properly before the Assembly, because Tennessee Valley Presbytery had acted on the matter. In answer to Louisiana Presbytery's Overture 22, the 28th General Assembly on June 22 resolved that the presbyteries' resolutions be treated as overtures, over a minority report. It then answered them in the affirmative, assumed original jurisdiction of TE John Wood on the allegations, and referred the matter to the SJC for investigation under BCO 31-2. James River Presbytery's Overture 23 made a similar request. The Assembly also directed the SJC to draft procedures for future matters under BCO 34-1.
