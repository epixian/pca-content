Westminster Presbytery adopted a memorial by "Concerned Presbyterian Ruling and Teaching Elders" and sent it to the 21st General Assembly as Overture 29; the same memorial with its signatures came as Communication 3. It said that the PCA had turned away from the 1973 "Message to All Churches." Its areas of concern included "new" revelation, the Standing Judicial Commission, "loose practice" in subscription, committees acting as boards, congregationalism, and the regulative principle of worship.
