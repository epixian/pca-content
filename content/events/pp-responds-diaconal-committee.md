Philadelphia Presbytery asked the 22nd General Assembly to reconsider the
exception. It said the committee only planned conferences of an advisory
character, and it required the committee to report to the presbytery at least
annually. It described the women members as women appointed by their sessions to
assist the deacons, and it stopped using the term "deaconess". The 22nd General
Assembly approved the response as satisfactory.
