The 11th General Assembly re-issued the 9th Assembly's invitation to the Orthodox Presbyterian Church to join the PCA, under the terms of "The Revised Joint Statement." The vote was 441 to 220. It sent a plan for the reception of the OPC to the presbyteries and answered Overtures 24 and 35 by reference to this action. In the same report the Assembly voted to continue fraternal relations with the Christian Reformed Church.
