The 8th General Assembly adopted Recommendation 5 of its Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations by a vote of 525 to 38. The recommendation sent a letter, "An invitation to effect one Church," to the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod, the Orthodox Presbyterian Church and the Reformed Presbyterian Church of North America. The Assembly answered Overtures 6, 18, 19, 28 and 33 by reference to this action.
