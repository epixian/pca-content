Ascension Presbytery's Overture 17 asked that officers with granted exceptions preach and teach them only if their presbyteries permit. The 34th General Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said that it could require a man not to teach, against Preliminary Principle 1 and WCF 20. A substitute was defeated.
