Northeast Presbytery overtured the 17th General Assembly (Overture 42) about the funding of ethnic ministries. The overture said that ethnic and cross-cultural ministries are an essential part of reaching all nations with the Gospel. It asked the Assembly to sustain current funding levels for ethnic ministries from the General Fund. It also asked the Assembly to direct the Mission to North America Committee to report on its funding policies.
