The Standing Judicial Commission decided TE Ryan Speck's complaint against Missouri Presbytery's December 2019 actions on its Revoice judgments. By a vote of 22-2 it sustained the complaint in part and denied it in part. It sent the matter back to the presbytery for a new hearing on what it must do to make clear to the wider church the errors it had found in some Revoice teachings. It encouraged the presbytery to interact with the Assembly's human sexuality report, while noting that the report "does not have Constitutional status."
