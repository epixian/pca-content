The 44th General Assembly answered Potomac Presbytery's Overture 43 in the affirmative as amended (861-123-23), and it answered 39 other overtures on racial reconciliation by reference to it. The Assembly resolved that it "does recognize, confess, condemn and repent of corporate and historical sins, including those committed during the Civil Rights era," and named sins such as the segregation of worshipers, the exclusion of persons, churches and elders on the basis of race, the teaching that the Bible sanctions segregation, and "the participation in and defense of white supremacist organizations." The Overtures Committee had amended Potomac's text, which asked the Assembly to "recognize, confess, and condemn these past and continuing racial sins": the amendment added "and repent of," the words about the Civil Rights era, and the list of sins. It commended Mississippi Valley Presbytery's pastoral letter as an example, and it reminded the churches that BCO 31-2 and 38-1 provide means of discipline for these sins.
