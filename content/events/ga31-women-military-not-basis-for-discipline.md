Potomac Presbytery's Overture 1 asked the 31st General Assembly to clarify the status of the 30th Assembly's statement on women in the military, and Chesapeake Presbytery's Overture 3 asked it to say that women's military service is not necessarily a basis for discipline. The Assembly answered Overture 1 with a statement that nothing the previous Assembly did compels any court of original jurisdiction to exercise discipline on the issues of the committee's report. It answered Overture 3 by reference to that action.
