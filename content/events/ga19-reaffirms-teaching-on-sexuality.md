In 1991 the Presbyterian Church (USA) considered a special committee report on human sexuality. Central Georgia Presbytery overtured the 19th General Assembly to reaffirm the 5th General Assembly's 1977 statement on homosexuality and to make it known to the news media. The 1977 statement said that a practicing homosexual continuing in his sin would not be a fit candidate for ordination or membership. The Assembly adopted a press release, proposed by its Ad Interim Committee on Divorce and Remarriage, that reaffirmed the Bible's teaching on sex and marriage. It answered Overture 28 by reference to that action.
