After debate, the 40th General Assembly answered Central Carolina Presbytery's Overture 2011-12 in the negative, so the PCA stayed in the National Association of Evangelicals. The Assembly also directed the Interchurch Relations Committee to watch for any action or position of the NAE and to report it to the General Assembly.
