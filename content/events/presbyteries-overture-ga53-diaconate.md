Six presbyteries sent related overtures on the diaconate to the 53rd General Assembly (2026):

- Savannah River Presbytery (Overture 34) asked to amend BCO 9-2, so that when a church has no deacons, their duties fall on the ruling elders "and not upon unordained persons within the church."
- Catawba Valley Presbytery (Overture 36) asked to add BCO 8-11 and 9-8, so that the duties and authority of elders and deacons "shall be exercised only by ordained elders" and ordained deacons.
- Northwest Georgia Presbytery (Overture 48) asked to amend BCO 7-2 and 7-3, so that no court may commission or appoint "a woman as a deaconess, shepherdess, or any other pretended office."
- Mississippi Valley Presbytery (Overture 52) asked to add BCO 7-4, because "the church is not free to invent offices."
- Northern California Presbytery (Overture 71) asked to amend BCO 5-9.e, 9-2 and 9-7, so that a session may administer diaconal ministry directly when a church has no deacons.
- Eastern Carolina Presbytery (Overture 74) asked to add a new chapter, BCO 17A, on commissioning, which "does not confer ecclesiastical office."

The Assembly answered all six, with Overtures 14 and 38, with reference to its answer to [Overture 37](/events/ga53-answers-overture-37/), Pacific Presbytery's request to allow women to serve as ordained deacons. The Assembly voted against Overture 37.
