The 12th General Assembly answered Grace Presbytery's Overture 11 to the 11th General Assembly in the negative. The overture asked that candidates for licensure file a written statement of their subscription and exceptions. The grounds were that it was "seen as lowering of our standards" and that changes in confessional standards should come by amending the documents. The clerk noted that the matter in effect went back to the Judicial Business Committee.
