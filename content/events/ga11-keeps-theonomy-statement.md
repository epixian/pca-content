Illiana Presbytery asked the 11th General Assembly to rescind the 7th Assembly's statement on the judicial law, and Gulf Coast Presbytery asked it to set aside the whole 1979 action. Both overtures argued that the statement made WCF 19.4 "inoperative" in judicial matters. The Assembly answered both in the negative.
