According to the later indictment, the session of Providence Presbyterian Church questioned its associate pastor, TE Rick Appleton, on February 5, 2026. The questions were about his presence at the RPCNA trial of Samuel Ketcham on January 24 and his views on kinism and "race realism." On February 9 the senior pastor emailed Pittsburgh Presbytery to ask that Appleton be examined. On February 27 Appleton sent the presbytery a letter of explanation.
