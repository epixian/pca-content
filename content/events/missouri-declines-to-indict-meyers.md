In March 2010, 29 PCA elders sent Missouri Presbytery a letter of concern that TE Jeffrey Meyers taught Federal Vision theology. Among other things, it said he denied the bi-covenantal structure of the Standards and the imputation of Christ's merits. The presbytery formed an investigative committee in April 2010. On January 8, 2011, it determined that there was not enough evidence for a strong presumption of guilt. It denied a complaint against that decision on April 19, 2011, and ten elders carried the complaint to the General Assembly (Case 2011-06).
