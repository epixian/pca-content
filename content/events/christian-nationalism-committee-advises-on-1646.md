In its constitutional guidance, the committee advised that an officer who advocates Christian civic engagement needs "no stated exception," and that the "nursing father" view "would not normally require" one. It advised that an officer who holds the establishment principle of the 1646 Confession "is out of accord with the PCA’s Standards and must declare this difference" to his examining body, and that some such views "may therefore be acceptable to Presbyteries," whose courts decide. It advised the same for an officer who holds that Old Testament civil law binds modern states "in its specific provisions." It read the 1979 and 1983 Assembly actions as its basis and wrote that the PCA has not "condemned all theonomic positions as erroneous."
