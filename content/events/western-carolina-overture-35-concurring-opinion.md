Western Carolina Presbytery adopted Overture 35 to the 29th General Assembly. It asked that all concurring opinions be removed from the Standing Judicial Commission's report on the John Wood matter and not appear in the Minutes. It said that concurring opinions are provided for only in cases of judicial process and that these contained personal allegations against members of the Assembly.
