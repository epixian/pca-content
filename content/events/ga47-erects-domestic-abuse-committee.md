The 47th General Assembly answered Pacific Northwest Presbytery's Overture 7 in the affirmative as amended, by hand vote, and erected a study committee on domestic abuse and sexual assault. The amended text said that no practice, policy, or guideline would be proposed for adoption, and recommended that the Moderator appoint subject-matter experts and women as advisors. The Assembly answered the eight similar overtures with reference to Overture 7, and raised the committee's budget to $25,000 from designated gifts.
