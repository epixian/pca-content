The Overtures Committee recommended that the 42nd General Assembly answer Savannah River's Overture 43 in the negative. A minority report offered a substitute in which the Assembly "expresses its gratitude to the Lord for sustaining by His grace ministers of the gospel, chaplains, and Christians serving in the public sphere who are experiencing ostracism". The Assembly adopted the minority report as a substitute by 443-395.
