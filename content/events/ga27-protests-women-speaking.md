At the close of the 27th General Assembly, groups of commissioners entered two protests about women speaking. One protested that the Assembly had allowed three women to address the court, one of whom they said engaged in exhortation, and argued that women are not to address church courts. The other protested the vote to vindicate MNA staff without hearing a tape of the woman's address, and the Assembly declined to respond to either protest.
