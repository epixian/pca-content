Louisiana Presbytery examined TE Steve Wilkins orally for about three hours on December 9, 2006, with a transcript and his written answers. On January 20, 2007, it voted 13-8 that it found "no strong presumption of guilt" in the memorial's charges and would not institute process. TE James Jones complained, and on April 21, 2007, the presbytery denied the complaint 10-8. TE Jones and six other presbyters then carried the complaint to the General Assembly (Case 2007-8).
