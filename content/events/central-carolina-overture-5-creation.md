Central Carolina Presbytery's Overture 5 asked the 26th General Assembly to erect a study committee on the interpretation of Genesis 1-3. The overture said that sessions and presbyteries needed clear criteria for allowable exceptions to the standards on these chapters. Westminster Presbytery's Overture 18 also asked for a study committee on creation. It said that any departure from creation in six 24-hour days struck at the fundamentals of the system of doctrine.
