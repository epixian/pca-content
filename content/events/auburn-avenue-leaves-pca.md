On January 27, 2008, the congregation of Auburn Avenue Presbyterian Church voted to withdraw from the PCA and to affiliate with the Confederation of Reformed Evangelical Churches. The same day TE Steve Wilkins told Louisiana Presbytery that he would withdraw, and the congregation received him as a member. At a called meeting on February 9, 2008, the presbytery noted the irregularity and removed his name from its roll under BCO 38-3(a).
