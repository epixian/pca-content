Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 25 asked the 31st General Assembly to direct the Interchurch Relations Committee to begin conversations with the matching committees of NAPARC churches with a view toward church union. Ascension Presbytery's Overture 24 to the same Assembly urged good-faith efforts toward organic union. The Minutes do not print adoption dates for these overtures.
