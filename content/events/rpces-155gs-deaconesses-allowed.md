At the 155th General Synod, the committee majority moved that women may be ordained to the office of deacon, and a minority position was moved in response. After debate and amendments, the Synod adopted the minority position. It limited the office of deacon to qualified men and affirmed the right of a local church to have a separate body of unordained women who may be called deaconesses.
