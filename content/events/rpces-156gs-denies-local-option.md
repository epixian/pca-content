Overture N from Michigan-Northern Indiana Presbytery asked the 156th General Synod to let each particular church decide whether its diaconate would include women, whether they would be ordained, and whether they would be called deacons or deaconesses. The Bills and Overtures Committee proposed a response that did not recommend this. After a substitute motion, the response was amended to say that churches may not ordain a woman as a deacon but are free to elect women as deaconesses and set them apart by prayer.
