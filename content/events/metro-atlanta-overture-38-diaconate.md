Metro Atlanta Presbytery adopted Overture 38 to the 53rd General Assembly, to amend BCO 7-3 and add a new BCO 9-8. The amendment would let each session choose one of three forms of the diaconate: only ordained men in the office of deacon; ordained men together with commissioned or appointed women; or only commissioned or appointed men and women who are not officers. In the second and third forms, the unordained members could bear the title of deacon. The overture said that the 2017 study report had found the practice of commissioning women not prohibited by the BCO, and that Overture 26 of the 51st General Assembly (2024) had made differing practice "more difficult". It stated that its aim was not to change the PCA's exegesis or conclusions on ordination and church offices. The 53rd General Assembly answered it with reference to its answer to [Overture 37](/events/ga53-answers-overture-37/), Pacific Presbytery's request to allow women to serve as ordained deacons. The Assembly voted against Overture 37.
