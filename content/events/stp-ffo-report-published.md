Save the PCA published "The Functional Female Officer Report" on its website on the evening of October 4, 2025; the site announced that Michael Foster would discuss it live on X at that time. From public church websites, live streams, and documents, the report said that many PCA churches had placed women in titled roles that functioned like the offices of elder or deacon. It asked presbyteries to adopt overtures like Savannah River Presbytery's Overture 34 and to press for a clear distinction between ordination and commissioning.
