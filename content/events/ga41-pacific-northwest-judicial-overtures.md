Pacific Northwest Presbytery sent two overtures that drew on the Federal Vision cases. Overture 15 proposed that when a higher court rules that a lower court erred by not indicting, it must accept a reference of the case; the rationale cited Bordwine v. Pacific Northwest and Sawyers v. Missouri, where a presbytery could be made to try "a man ... whom it does not believe should be put on trial." The Assembly answered it in the affirmative and sent it to the presbyteries. Overture 16 would have let 5% of presbyteries ask for original jurisdiction when a presbytery "declines to order an indictment." The 40th Assembly had referred the same text back in 2012, and the 41st answered it in the negative.
