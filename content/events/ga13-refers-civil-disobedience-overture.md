On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures, the 13th General Assembly referred Missouri Presbytery's Overture 16 on civil disobedience and abortion to the Church/State Subcommittee that the 12th General Assembly had established. It asked the subcommittee to report to the 14th General Assembly.
