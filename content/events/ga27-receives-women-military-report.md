The Ad Interim Committee on Women in the Military asked the 27th General Assembly to adopt its conclusions, which opposed the drafting of women under any circumstances and called the inclusion of women in combat roles contrary to the Word of God. The Assembly first defeated a motion to refer the report back, 310 to 442, but then adopted a motion to receive the report as information, to be used as advice, and to refer it back to the committee for the 28th General Assembly. It also voted to add three members to the committee.
