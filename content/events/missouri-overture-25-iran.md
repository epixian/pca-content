Missouri Presbytery sent Overture 25, "Justice & Peace Concerning U.S.-Iran Relations," to the 34th General Assembly. It said the church is called "at extraordinary times" to speak on particular public issues. It asked the Assembly to call on the federal government of the United States to seek a just and peaceful resolution of the crisis over Iran's nuclear program, to encourage prayer for the leaders of the United States and Iran, and to observe a moment of prayer. The Minutes do not print the presbytery's adoption date.
