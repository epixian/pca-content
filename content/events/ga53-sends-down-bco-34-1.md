Westminster Presbytery's Overture 27 asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 34-1, which lets the General Assembly assume original jurisdiction over a teaching elder when his presbytery refuses to act in doctrinal cases or cases of public scandal. Its text would let a presbytery that reported a teaching elder's false teaching or heinous sin to his presbytery ask the Assembly to act if that presbytery fails to indict, with the request of three presbyteries on the same matter. The Overtures Committee recommended an amended version (123-10), and the Assembly answered the overture in the affirmative as amended, which sends the amendment to the presbyteries.
