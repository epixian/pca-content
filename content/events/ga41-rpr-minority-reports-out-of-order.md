Minority reports of the Committee on Review of Presbytery Records asked the 41st General Assembly to take exceptions of substance to the minutes of Pacific Northwest and Missouri presbyteries. They said the presbyteries were delinquent "in condemning erroneous opinions" in the trials of TE Peter Leithart and TE Jeffrey Meyers and when they denied the complaints that followed. On points of order by RE Howie Donahoe, the Moderator ruled both reports out of order under BCO 40-3, because records review may not address a judicial case in which a complaint was filed. The Assembly sustained the rulings and approved the presbyteries' minutes as the committee recommended.
