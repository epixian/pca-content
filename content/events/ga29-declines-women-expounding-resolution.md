A personal resolution asked the 29th General Assembly to clarify the 28th Assembly's statement on MNA seminars by declaring that Scripture prohibits a woman, under any circumstance, from publicly expounding, exhorting, applying, or preaching the Word of God. The Assembly answered it in the negative. The ground said that the words "under any circumstance" would prohibit women from teaching other women and children.
