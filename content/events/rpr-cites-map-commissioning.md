The 38th General Assembly adopted RPR's recommendation to approve Metro Atlanta Presbytery's minutes with three exceptions of substance on practices #4, #5, and #6 of its [April 21, 2009 resolution on diaconal practices](/events/map-elects-women-diaconal/), which appears to follow the [six views](/events/ncp-adopts-six-diaconate-views/) that Northern California Presbytery had adopted six weeks earlier. RPR said that commissioning rather than ordaining "appears to be a distinction without a difference," and that practice #6 denied men their right to ordination as deacons.
