The 24th General Assembly answered Personal Resolution 3, a "Humble Petition Concerning Partial Birth Abortions," with a statement that condemned partial-birth abortion and "urgently and humbly" petitioned the President and Congress.

The same day, the Assembly answered Personal Resolution 5, "Postcard Campaign Against Partial Birth Abortion," which would have made commissioners aware of materials for a postcard campaign asking legislators to override the President's veto, in the negative. Its grounds quoted WCF 31.5 against intermeddling with civil affairs except by humble petition, and said that members and churches are free to act according to their conscience.
