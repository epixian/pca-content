In response to the 42nd General Assembly's child protection action, the Cooperative Ministries Committee reported that each committee and agency had or was working on a child protection policy. The 43rd General Assembly noted that the Administrative Committee had reviewed its policies. In the review of one agency's minutes, it recorded an exception because the board had not recorded its review of child protection policies as the 42nd Assembly had directed.
