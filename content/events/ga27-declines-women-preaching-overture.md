Western Carolina Presbytery's Overture 16, "Forbid Women to Preach/Teach at Worship Services," asked the 27th General Assembly to advise sessions and presbyteries not to allow women to preach or teach at worship services. The Assembly answered it in the negative. Its ground said that the Assembly stands against the preaching of women as referred to in 1 Timothy 2:12, but that violations of the Standards should first be addressed by the lower courts.
