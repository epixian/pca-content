Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 66 asked the 53rd General Assembly to issue a formal "Declaration of Thanksgiving" for the United States on the 250th anniversary of its founding. The Overtures Committee replaced the declaration with a shorter resolution (91-30-5) that encourages all PCA congregations to give thanks to the Lord for the United States as the anniversary approaches. The Assembly adopted Overture 66 as amended and answered similar overtures from Calvary (Overture 76) and Fellowship (Overture 89) presbyteries with reference to it.
