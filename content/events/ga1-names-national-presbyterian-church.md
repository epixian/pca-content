The Executive Committee proposed three names: The Presbyterian Church in America, The Presbyterian Church of America, and The National Presbyterian Church. On the afternoon of December 5 the Assembly chose "National Presbyterian Church." It also changed the name of the corporation from Continuing Presbyterian Church (A Corporation) to National Presbyterian Church (A Corporation).
