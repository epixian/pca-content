Pacific Presbytery adopted Overture 58 to the 12th General Assembly on April 28, 1984. It asked the Assembly to express its love for the wider body of Christ and pointed to the NAE doctrinal statement that earlier Assemblies had approved. The 12th General Assembly answered the overture in the affirmative. It asked the Permanent Sub-Committee on Inter-church Relations to study the NAE so that the 1985 Assembly could decide whether the PCA should seek membership.
