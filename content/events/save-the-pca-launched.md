Michael Foster and others started Save the PCA, an online campaign with a website at savethepca.com. Its About page said that its goal was to stop the PCA from "embracing left-wing practices that are not rooted in scripture," such as female ordination, by organizing PCA members and by "a guerilla awareness campaign."
