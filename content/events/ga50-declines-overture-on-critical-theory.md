Overture 18 came from the sessions of four churches in Georgia after Metro Atlanta Presbytery declined to send it. It asked the Assembly to affirm "Christ-centered racial reconciliation" and to reject an ideology that, it said, "attempts to define and explain race, racism, and gender in a manner contrary to a Biblical worldview." The 50th General Assembly answered it in the negative (1156-318-19), on the ground that these issues "have already been well addressed" by the 2018 report.
