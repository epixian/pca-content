The 45th General Assembly voted on each of the nine recommendations of the women study committee:

1. Answer Westminster Presbytery's Overture 3, which asked the Assembly to dismiss the committee, in the negative. Adopted (941-307).
2. Recognize that, from the founding of the PCA, there has been a variety of views and practices on how women may serve without ordination, and continue mutual respect for them. Adopted (880-349-12).
3. Develop and use the gifts of godly women in the local, regional, and national church, and consider overtures to let qualified women serve on appropriate committees and agencies. Adopted (908-279-6).
4. That sessions, if possible, establish a diaconate of qualified ordained men. The Assembly struck the second sentence, on churches that go without ordained deacons (642-473-14), and adopted it as amended (939-140-18).
5. That sessions consider how to include non-ordained men and women in worship. A substitute failed (391-796-8), and the recommendation was adopted (830-362-11).
6. That sessions and presbyteries appoint godly women and men to assist the ordained diaconate (BCO 9-7). Adopted as amended (838-289-32).
7. That presbyteries and the General Assembly consider an overture to establish the position of commissioned church worker for qualified unordained men and women. Adopted (649-501-6).
8. That the courts consider how to affirm and include underprivileged and underrepresented women in the PCA. Adopted (966-119-15).
9. That the committee be dismissed with thanks. Adopted (1056-38-17).

An amendment to add a Recommendation 10, to commend the report to the presbyteries and sessions for continued study, guidance, and use, was defeated (569-671).
