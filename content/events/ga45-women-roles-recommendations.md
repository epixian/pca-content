Besides its recommendations on the diaconate, the 45th General Assembly adopted three recommendations on women's roles from the women study committee. Recommendation 2 recognized that the PCA has had a variety of views and practices on how women may serve without ordination. Recommendation 3 asked the courts to use the gifts of godly women and to consider overtures to let qualified women serve on appropriate committees and agencies. Recommendation 5 asked sessions to consider how to include non-ordained men and women in worship.
