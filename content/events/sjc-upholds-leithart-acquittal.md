In Case 2012-05, Hedman v. Pacific Northwest Presbytery, the full Standing Judicial Commission heard the complaint on March 6, 2013, and denied it 15-2. It held that the complainant had not shown from the record that the presbytery violated the Constitution when it acquitted TE Peter Leithart. The decision said that nothing in it should be read as "addressing (or thereby endorsing)" his views in general. A concurring opinion said the case showed the need for "a more practical mechanism" for higher courts to take original jurisdiction.
