The Stated Clerk reported that Item 5, the amendment to BCO 9-7, had received the
required two-thirds vote of the presbyteries, and the 39th General Assembly
adopted it. BCO 9-7 now states that assistants to the deacons are not officers of
the church and are not subjects for ordination.
