Calvary Presbytery's Overture 54 asked the 44th General Assembly to thank Covenant College for forfeiting the women's tennis conference championship match of April 17, 2016, because it was scheduled on the Lord's Day. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Covenant College, the Assembly answered the overture in the affirmative as amended. The adopted text also reminded congregations of the 4th General Assembly's 1976 resolution against unnecessary encroachments upon the Lord's Day.
