At the same called meeting, Missouri Presbytery adopted Overture 45 to the 47th General Assembly. It asked for an ad interim committee to seek consensus on doctrinal boundaries and pastoral care in the debates about sexuality. Its committee on Memorial's hosting of Revoice had recommended this.
