Mississippi Valley Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to add BCO 7-4, which would forbid the creation of ecclesiastical offices beyond those Scripture gives, while still allowing men and women in un-ordained service. The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
