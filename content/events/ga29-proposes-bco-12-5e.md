Philadelphia Presbytery's Overture 27 asked the 29th General Assembly to amend BCO 12-5 so that sessions would prohibit women from preaching in public worship. The Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures recommended a negative answer, but a minority of the committee offered a substitute that answered the overture in the affirmative as amended. The Assembly sent down the minority's text, under which the session is "to ensure that the Word of God is preached only by such men as are sufficiently qualified (BCO 4-4, 53-2, 1 Timothy 2:11-12)."
