The Ad Interim Study Committee on Women in the Military presented a consensus report, a majority paper, "Man's Duty to Protect Woman," and a minority paper, "Recommendations for the Wise Counsel of the Church." The committee agreed on most points but divided on whether women's absence from offensive combat was a matter of duty or of wise counsel. The chairman moved to set the recommendations aside and extend the committee, but the 29th General Assembly instead took up the consensus recommendations. It adopted recommendations 1-4 and 8-11, which included a warning to women of the PCA about the dangers of military service, and sent the papers to the presbyteries for study. It recommitted recommendations 5-7 and 12, and a substitute declaring "the biblical duty of men to defend women," to the committee for the next Assembly.
