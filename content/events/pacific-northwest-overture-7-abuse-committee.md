Pacific Northwest Presbytery adopted Overture 7 to the 47th General Assembly, to form a study committee on domestic abuse and sexual assault. The committee would prepare an annotated bibliography, report on best practices for elders, sessions, presbyteries, and agencies, and could recommend statements and changes to the BCO Rules of Discipline. The overture cited the 42nd General Assembly's child protection action and named BCO 32-20, 32-13, and 35-4 as provisions that might need review. Eight other presbyteries sent similar overtures: Northern New England (10), Southern New England (13), Nashville (20), Tennessee Valley (26), Eastern Pennsylvania (31), Georgia Foothills (38), Metro Atlanta (43), and Missouri (47).
