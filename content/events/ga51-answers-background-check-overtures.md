In 2023 the 50th General Assembly referred back to South Texas Presbytery its overture to require criminal background checks of all minister and officer candidates (102-28-0), on the ground that presbyteries and churches were already free to do them. In 2024 five presbyteries sent similar overtures to the 51st General Assembly: Susquehanna Valley (Overture 6), Warrior (16), Ohio (17), Missouri (23), and South Texas (24). Some cited the report of the Ad Interim Committee on Domestic Abuse and Sexual Assault, which had recommended background checks. The Assembly answered Ohio Presbytery's Overture 17 in the affirmative as amended and the others with reference to it. The Overtures Committee's grounds said the amended language encouraged church courts to adopt policies and procedures without making specific mandates.
