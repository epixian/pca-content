The presbyteries approved the BCO 21-4 amendment by 45 votes to 19. The 31st General Assembly then enacted it, 816-545, and many commissioners recorded negative votes. The amended text lets a court grant an exception only if the declared difference is not out of accord with any fundamental of the system of doctrine.
