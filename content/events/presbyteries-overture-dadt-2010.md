Rocky Mountain (Overture 12), South Texas (Overture 17) and Savannah River (Overture 22) presbyteries asked the 38th General Assembly to petition the United States government to keep the "Don't Ask, Don't Tell" policy on homosexuals in the military. The 38th General Assembly answered the three overtures through the report of its Committee of Commissioners on Mission to North America.
