Save the PCA released an interactive tool that showed its figures by presbytery, and it revised the report's findings because of tips and refinements to the dataset. As revised, the report said that 9.5% of the church websites it surveyed had some form of functional female officer (4.0% with a functional female elder and 6.0% with a functional female deacon). An October 13 article had quoted the original figure as 7.3%.
