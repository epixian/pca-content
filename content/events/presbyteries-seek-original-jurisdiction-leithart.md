Gulf Coast (Overture 20), Calvary (Overture 21) and Mississippi Valley (Overture 22) presbyteries adopted identical overtures asking the 41st General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction under BCO 34-1 and to have the Standing Judicial Commission hear "Pacific Northwest Presbytery vs. Peter Leithart." They argued that the commission had not decided whether his views conflict with the Standards. They also said that the chief prosecutor, Jason Stellman, had since resigned from the PCA ministry and joined the Roman Catholic Church, which they called a conflict of interest. The Stated Clerk referred the overtures to the commission.
