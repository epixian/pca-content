In April 2009 Siouxlands Presbytery formed a committee to investigate the views of TE Greg Lawrence. In September 2009 the committee recommended, 4-2, a strong presumption that he taught contrary to the Standards on baptism. On motions by TE Joshua Moon for the committee minority, the presbytery rejected the report 25-13 and then found no strong presumption of guilt about any doctrines associated with the Federal Vision. The Standing Judicial Commission gives that second vote as 20-13 in one decision and as 20-17 in another. In October 2009, on a complaint by TE Wes White, the presbytery repented of "acting in haste" and gave the matter to a second committee.
