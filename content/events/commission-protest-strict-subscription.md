Twenty-eight commissioners, among them TE Morton H. Smith, protested the 21st General Assembly's refusal to take exception to Mission to the World's partnership agreement with CoMission. They said that the agreement's use of the Jesus Film broke the second commandment as the Larger Catechism (Q. 109) explains it. The protest said that "the PCA was founded as a strict subscription Church."
