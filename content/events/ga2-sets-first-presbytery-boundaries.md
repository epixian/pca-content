The 2nd General Assembly adopted, as amended, the report of its Presbytery Boundaries Committee. The report gave each presbytery its bounds, mostly by state; for example, Texas Presbytery received the state of Texas. The boundaries took effect on January 1, 1975. On the next day the Assembly passed a motion to reconsider the report. The Minutes show no later vote on it.
