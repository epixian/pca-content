Great Lakes Presbytery adopted Overture 47 to the 52nd General Assembly. It asked whether officers who hold the 1647 text of WCF 23.3 must take an exception to the 1788 text, noted that "some in the PCA have labeled the historic WCF’s views" on the magistrate as Christian nationalism, and noted that some describe their view "as Ethno-Nationalism, or label themselves as Heritage Americans."
