The 44th General Assembly answered Potomac Presbytery's Overture 45 in the affirmative as amended (814-87-15). It told the Moderator to appoint a study committee "comprised of multiple ethnicities, with ruling elder participation," to assess the PCA's situation, identify specific problems and suggest concrete steps, with a report to the 45th General Assembly. The Assembly also urged each presbytery and session to form a similar committee. The overture's grounds said that earlier actions had not increased ethnic diversity and participation in the denomination.
