The 29th General Assembly answered Overtures 7 (Calvary), 20 (New River) and 23 (Mississippi Valley) on exceptions about the length of the creation days in the negative. The grounds said that the lower courts decide if a man's view is an exception. A minority report substitute was defeated. The Assembly also answered North Georgia Presbytery's Overture 30 in the negative on similar grounds.
