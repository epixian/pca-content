Savannah River Presbytery adopted Overture 34 on April 15, 2025. It cited the BCO 7-3 amendment on titles and asked to amend RAO 16 so that each presbytery's directory reports why a church has no diaconate. The Overtures Committee recommended that the 52nd General Assembly answer it in the affirmative as amended, but the Assembly vote (929-730) failed because it did not reach the two-thirds vote required for an RAO amendment.
