The Stated Clerk reported that the amendments sent down by the 41st General Assembly, including the change to BCO 43-10, had received the consent of two-thirds of the presbyteries, and the 42nd General Assembly adopted them. The text sent down (Item 2) adds a sentence: when a higher court rules that a lower court erred by not indicting someone and the lower court refers the matter back, the higher court "shall accept the Reference if it is a doctrinal case or case of public scandal."
