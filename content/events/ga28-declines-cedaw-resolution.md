A personal resolution from a teaching elder asked the 28th General Assembly to go on record against the United Nations Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women, and to have the Stated Clerk tell the President of the Senate and the Senate Majority Leader. The resolution said the convention would legalize prostitution and same-sex marriage. The Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said treaties and legislation were not proper subjects for the Assembly (WCF 31-5), that the convention's text was not before the Assembly, and that the Assembly had already stated the Bible's teaching on these moral issues. The Assembly encouraged members to act as "active and discerning citizens."
