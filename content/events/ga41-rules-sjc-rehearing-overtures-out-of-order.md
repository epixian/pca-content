Illiana Presbytery's Overture 19 asked the Assembly to have the Standing Judicial Commission rehear the Leithart case (2012-05). Great Lakes Presbytery's Overture 23 asked it to direct the commission to hear the complaint about TE Jeffrey Meyers (2012-09). The Overtures Committee asked the Moderator to rule both out of order, because presbyteries "are not constitutionally permitted, in any way, to try to reopen SJC cases." The Moderator so ruled, and the Assembly sustained him on appeal both times. The Stated Clerk, as parliamentarian, explained that the minority report on Overture 19 fell with the overture. Those minority reports said that the 2007 Assembly had "officially opposed the Federal Vision."
