Westminster Presbytery's Overture 4 to the 21st General Assembly asked to amend the second sentence of BCO 3-4. The amendment added that the human reason and providential events that determine the constitution of the state "are to be ordered by Christian prudence according to the general rules of the Word." One whereas clause cited the "general equity" of the judicial law (WCF 19.4).
