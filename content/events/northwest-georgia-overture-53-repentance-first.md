Northwest Georgia Presbytery adopted Overture 53, "Leading by Example in Repentance First." It asked that all overtures to the 44th General Assembly that called for confession and repentance be returned to their courts of original jurisdiction, to be handled under BCO 31-2 and 38-1. The 44th General Assembly answered it by reference to its action on Potomac's Overture 43.
