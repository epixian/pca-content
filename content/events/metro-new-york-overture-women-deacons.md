Metropolitan New York Presbytery sent Overture 29 to the 47th General Assembly. It proposed amendments to several sections of the BCO so that local sessions could decide whether women may serve as deacons. The Committee on Constitutional Business advised that it did not conflict with other parts of the Constitution. The presbytery withdrew the overture before the Assembly acted, and the Minutes do not print its text.
