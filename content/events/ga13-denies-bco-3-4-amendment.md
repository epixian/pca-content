On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business, the 13th General Assembly denied Oklahoma Presbytery's Overture 2 to amend BCO 3-4. The grounds said that the overture cited no biblical rationale for its claim, that the current language was historically significant, and that the real need was more education on its historical background.
