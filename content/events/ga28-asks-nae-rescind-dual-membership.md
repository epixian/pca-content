The National Association of Evangelicals (NAE) had changed its Bylaw B-7 so that denominations could be members of both the NAE and bodies such as the National Council of Churches. On the recommendation of the Interchurch Relations Committee, the 28th General Assembly told the committee to inform the NAE that the PCA strongly disapproved of the change and to ask that it be rescinded. The PCA Stated Clerk made this request in writing and moved it at the NAE board in March 2001. The committee reported to the 31st Assembly that the NAE had dropped the dual-membership proposal.
