The Stated Clerk judged Overture 15 out of order on three grounds: it differed
from the overture put to the presbytery, it dealt with the issue just decided in
SJC 2008-1 and 2008-10, and a motion to reaffirm is not in order. The Committee
on Constitutional Business concurred, and the Moderator ruled the overture out of
order. The Overtures Committee concurred by a vote of 75-0-0.
