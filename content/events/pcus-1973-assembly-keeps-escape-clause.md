At the 113th General Assembly of the PCUS, Christianity Today reported that about eighty congregations had already left the denomination. The Assembly voted to keep the escape clause in the draft plan of union with the United Presbyterians. It formed a seven-member committee to state the causes of the division and recommend solutions, and adopted a resolution that called for prayer, self-examination and repentance. It also voted down a motion that asked the Montreat trustees to cancel meetings of the Presbyterian Evangelistic Fellowship.
