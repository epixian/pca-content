The 9th General Assembly acted on its committee's plans to receive the Reformed Presbyterian Church, Evangelical Synod (Recommendation J) and the Orthodox Presbyterian Church (Recommendation K). Recommendation J asked the Assembly to take the first formal vote under BCO 14-6, which needs the consent of three-fourths of the presbyteries. A substitute motion to defer action on the OPC until "the current controversy" was resolved failed, 192 to 347. The Assembly adopted Recommendation K by 422 to 187. The Assembly continued its ad interim committee to the 1982 Assembly for "the proposed reception of the RPCES and the OPC." The presbyteries later approved the RPCES plan (see the 10th General Assembly), but the OPC plan received 18 presbytery votes for and 7 against, short of the 19 that it needed.
