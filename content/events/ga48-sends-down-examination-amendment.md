The 48th General Assembly answered Eastern Pennsylvania Presbytery's Overture 37 in the affirmative as amended and sent it to the presbyteries. The amendment told presbyteries (BCO 21-4) and sessions (BCO 24-1) to give specific attention in examinations to "potentially notorious concerns," including sexual immorality and homosexuality. A candidate "must not be known by reputation or self-profession according to his remaining sinfulness." A minority report, which would have brought its wording into line with the new BCO 16-4, failed 617-1209.
