Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 48 to the 14th General Assembly. It said that affiliation with the National Association of Evangelicals would reduce the Reformed faith "to an undefined evangelicalism," and that the NAE spoke to political and moral issues through its Office of Public Affairs in Washington, D.C. It said that such controversies should be addressed through church courts. The overture asked the Assembly to stop considering affiliation with the NAE.
