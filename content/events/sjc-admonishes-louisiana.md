In Case 2007-14, PCA v. Louisiana Presbytery, the Standing Judicial Commission tried the first specification on March 6, 2008, and dismissed it. On the second, to which the presbytery had pleaded guilty, it imposed the censure of admonition: Louisiana Presbytery was "derelict in its duty" when it failed to find a strong presumption of guilt about some of TE Steve Wilkins' views. The vote was 16-1, with 2 disqualified and 5 absent. The minutes do not print the day of the vote; the commission met on March 6–7, 2008. In a concurring opinion RE Howard Donahoe wrote that the BCO 34-1 phrase "refuses to act" is "poorly-worded" and set the bar for original jurisdiction too high.
