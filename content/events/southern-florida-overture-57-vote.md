Southern Florida Presbytery adopted Overture 57 by unanimous consent on April 17, 1984. It said that Christians had been lax in their right and responsibility to vote. It asked the General Assembly to urge all PCA members to register and vote in the November 6, 1984 election. The 12th General Assembly answered the overture in the affirmative.
