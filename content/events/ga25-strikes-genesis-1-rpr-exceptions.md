The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records proposed exceptions against two presbyteries on views of Genesis 1. One cited James River Presbytery for licensing a candidate who called Genesis 1 a "poetic account." The other cited New Jersey Presbytery's "Affirmations and Denials" on Genesis One. The 25th General Assembly struck the James River exception because it had not determined that only one interpretation of Genesis 1 was possible. The Moderator ruled the New Jersey exception out of order on similar grounds.
