The RPCES Rocky Mountain Presbytery had asked the RPCES synod in 1982 to make ministry to "the various ethnic peoples of our continent" a priority, and the overture came to the PCA after joining and receiving. The 12th General Assembly answered it by reference to the report of the Committee on Mission to North America. The report agreed with the overture and named, among the difficulties, "our own ethnocentricity and cultural isolation as a denomination." The Assembly encouraged churches to reach out to ethnic minorities near them.
