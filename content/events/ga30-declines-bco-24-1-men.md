Rocky Mountain Presbytery's Overtures 14 and 15 asked the 30th General Assembly to amend BCO 24-1 so that churches would elect "men" rather than "persons" as ruling elders and deacons, and so that each prospective officer "shall" rather than "should" be an active male member. Overture 15 noted some disagreement in the PCA about women as deacons. The Assembly answered both in the negative, on the grounds that "persons" in that context refers to men under BCO 7-2 and that "should" there expresses duty.
