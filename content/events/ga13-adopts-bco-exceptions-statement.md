On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business, the 13th General Assembly adopted Missouri Presbytery's Overture 17. It printed a two-paragraph statement in its Minutes as its position. The first paragraph repeated the 1984 rule: an exception to the BCO on a matter that the Westminster Confession and Catechisms address is handled as an exception to those standards. The second paragraph added the overture's rule for exceptions on the "general principles of Biblical polity."
