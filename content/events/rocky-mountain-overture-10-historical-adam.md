Rocky Mountain Presbytery's Overture 10 asked the 40th General Assembly to adopt the declaration of the PCUS General Assemblies of 1886, 1888 and 1924 on the historical Adam. That declaration said that Adam and Eve were created body and soul by immediate acts of God, and that Adam's body had no animal parentage. The overture cited a 2011 Christianity Today article on the historical Adam, the BioLogos Forum, and the SJC's words in Case 90-3. Savannah River Presbytery's Overture 29 asked for the same declaration.
