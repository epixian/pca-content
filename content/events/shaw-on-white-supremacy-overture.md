In The Aquila Report, TE Jim Shaw II wrote that Chesapeake Presbytery asked the Assembly "to study something that exists only in their minds." He rejected the claim that the PCA was founded on white supremacy ("Our forefathers in the PCA ... were not white supremacists") and said that the overture's thinking derives from critical race theory.
