On the report of the Committee on Administration, the 12th General Assembly answered Westminster Presbytery's Overture 50 and Oklahoma Presbytery's Overture 55 in the negative. It also answered a personal resolution on the tax issue in the negative. In the same report the Assembly asked for a study committee on church/state relations and taxation (see the related event).
