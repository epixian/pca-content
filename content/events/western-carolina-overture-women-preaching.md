Western Carolina Presbytery adopted Overture 16, "Forbid Women to Preach/Teach at Worship Services," on March 20, 1999. It said that there were reports of sessions and presbyteries that let women preach or teach the Scriptures at worship services. It recalled that the PCUS ordination of women was in part a cause of the separation of the PCA, and that the 25th General Assembly had ended fraternal relations with the CRC mainly because the CRC opened the office of elder to women. The overture said that such practices cause tension and "could eventuate in a division of the Church". It asked the 27th General Assembly to speak to the issue and to advise sessions and presbyteries not to allow the practice to continue.
