A teaching elder's overture, which his presbytery had declined to adopt, asked the 27th General Assembly to amend BCO 50-2 so that Scripture in public worship would be read by "the minister or some other man approved by the Session." It cited the 26th Assembly's response to Southern Florida Presbytery. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative because it did not provide a sufficient exegetical basis.
