On the recommendation of the Committee on Review of Presbytery Records, the 30th General Assembly took an exception of substance to Korean Southern Presbytery's minutes of April 9, 2001. The exception said that the ordination of women as deaconesses is not provided for in the PCA Constitution, citing BCO 9-3 and 7-2.
