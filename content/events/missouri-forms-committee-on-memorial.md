Missouri Presbytery created an ad hoc Committee to Investigate Memorial (CIM). It was to investigate TE Greg Johnson and the Memorial session under BCO 31-2 and 41, after concerns about the church's hosting of Revoice. TE Ron Lutjens chaired it; TE Ryan Speck was a member.
