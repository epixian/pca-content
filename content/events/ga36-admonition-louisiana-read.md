During the Standing Judicial Commission's report on June 11, 2008, the Moderator read the admonition to Louisiana Presbytery before the 36th General Assembly. The presbytery's clerk acknowledged that the presbytery "has duly received the Admonition." The commission's report included an objection from RE Samuel J. Duncan, the disqualified prosecutor, who argued that the Assembly should have voted to approve or disapprove the decision.
