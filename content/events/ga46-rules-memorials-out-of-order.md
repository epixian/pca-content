The Overtures Committee asked that all six memorial overtures, three of them for Morton Smith, be ruled out of order. Among its reasons it said that the rules do not "specify how to deal with assertions regarding a deceased person's deficiencies that an overture may attribute to him." The Moderator ruled the point of order well taken, so the Assembly adopted none of the memorials.
