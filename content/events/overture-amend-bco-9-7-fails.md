Grace Presbytery adopted Overture 9 on January 9, 2018. It responded to the 45th General Assembly's actions on the women study report. It asked the 46th General Assembly to add three paragraphs to BCO 9-7: the congregation would make no vows with deaconesses or deacon assistants like those with ordained deacons; they would not be called members of a "diaconate," a "board of deacons" or a deacon "team"; and they could not vote in the diaconate or hold its offices. It also asked for a new BCO 24-11, which would make the requirement that elders and deacons be men "a solemn covenant whereby the Church as a whole promises never to attempt to ordain females to any such office." The Committee on Constitutional Business gave the opinion that the overture was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution, and the Assembly adopted the Overtures Committee recommendation to answer it in the negative.
