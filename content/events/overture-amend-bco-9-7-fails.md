Grace Presbytery adopted Overture 9 on January 9, 2018. It asked the 46th General Assembly to add paragraphs to BCO 9-7 about deaconesses and deacon assistants, and to add a new BCO 24-11 about women officers. The Committee on Constitutional Business gave the opinion that the overture was in conflict with other parts of the Constitution, and the Assembly adopted the Overtures Committee recommendation to answer it in the negative.
