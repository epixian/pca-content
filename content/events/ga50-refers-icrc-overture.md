Covenant Presbytery's Overture 2 asked the 50th General Assembly to have the PCA apply to join the International Conference of Reformed Churches, noting the withdrawal from the National Association of Evangelicals the year before. The Interchurch Relations Committee proposed to postpone the matter until it had sent representatives to the ICRC's 2026 meeting, because ICRC rules require a church to attend a meeting before it applies. The Assembly instead adopted its committee of commissioners' substitute, which referred the overture back to the Interchurch Relations Committee (1183-16-59).
