Gulf Coast Presbytery asked the 11th General Assembly five questions after the 1982 decision. The Assembly ratified advice stating that the 7th Assembly's minutes had omitted a qualifier ("in so far as this view is in accord with paragraph a"), that no view of the judicial law contrary to WCF 19.4 is acceptable, and that all views not contrary to it are acceptable. It also interpreted Deuteronomy 13 as legislation for Israel's theocratic era that "should not be enforced by the state in the present era"; 29 commissioners recorded negative votes, and the Assembly added that this declaration "shall not be used by the courts of the Church to bind the conscience of elders."
