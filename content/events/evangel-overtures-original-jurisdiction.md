Evangel Presbytery adopted Overtures 9 and 11 to the 29th General Assembly. They proposed new wording for BCO 33-1 and BCO 34-1, with procedures for a higher court to assume original jurisdiction, including a preliminary hearing on whether the presbytery had already investigated under BCO 31-2. The rationale said that the John Wood matter had shown that the existing BCO 34-1 did not answer several questions, such as what "refuses to act" means.
