Answering Calvary Presbytery's Overture 13, the 51st General Assembly commended as "biblically faithful" a letter by the 50th Assembly's Moderator's Commission "humbly petitioning leaders of the United States Government" about medical interventions for gender reassignment in minors. It left it to each court whether to share the letter with civil magistrates.
