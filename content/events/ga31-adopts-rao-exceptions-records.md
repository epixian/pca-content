The 31st General Assembly answered Overture 5 in the affirmative as amended, by a two-thirds majority. It answered New Jersey Presbytery's Overture 6 (a BCO 21-4 procedure for exceptions) and Westminster Presbytery's Overture 20 (a report of all exceptions to the Assembly) by reference to that action. It answered Covenant Presbytery's Overture 18, another RAO 14-3.e.5 amendment on subscription, in the negative.
