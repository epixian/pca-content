According to a copy posted online by a party other than the court, the presbytery's prosecutor signed an indictment of TE Zachary Garris, attested by the moderator, on February 4, 2026. Charge One charged a violation of the ninth commandment (Exodus 20:16); its six specifications said that posts on X about slavery from 2023 and 2024 were false. Charge Two charged a violation of Ephesians 4:29; its two specifications were posts of October 8 and 9, 2023, in an exchange with a professor.
