In 1987 the 15th General Assembly sent down amendments from its Ad Interim Committee to Study and Make Recommendations as to Structure and Procedure. One amendment to BCO 40-5 said that a higher court may "act on" a case or "act against" a lower court, but may not "act for" it. A related amendment rewrote BCO 11-4. Commissioners led by TE Edmund Clowney protested that the amendments would impair the unity of the church in its government. In 1988 the 16th General Assembly reported that the presbyteries had rejected the BCO 40-5 and BCO 11-4 amendments, 14 for and 28 against.
