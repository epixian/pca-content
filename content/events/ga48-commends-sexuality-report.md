The 48th General Assembly answered Calvary Presbytery's Overture 38 in the affirmative, after short debate. The overture asked the Assembly to declare the report of the Ad Interim Committee on Human Sexuality "a biblically faithful declaration" and to send it to the Committee on Discipleship Ministries for teaching materials. The Overtures Committee had recommended it 124-3-0.
