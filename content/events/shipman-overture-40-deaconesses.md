TE Herbert Shipman of [Northeast Presbytery](/actors/northeast-presbytery/) wrote an overture asking the General Assembly to declare three practices out of accord with the BCO: the congregational election of deaconesses (BCO 9-7), deaconesses sitting on a board and voting with the deacons (BCO 9-4), and the setting apart of anyone other than elders and deacons with prayer and the laying on of hands (BCO 17-2). It cited reports of attempts to undermine the Constitution on the role of women. He presented it to the presbytery's May 10–11, 1991 meeting, which did not act on it, so he sent it directly to the 19th General Assembly as Overture 40. His cover letter hoped that a clear declaration would correct the practice and prevent division.
