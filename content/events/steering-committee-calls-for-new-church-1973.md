In February 1973 the Steering Committee for a Continuing Presbyterian Church called for "a formal ecclesiastical entity to be formed in 1973." The Advisory Convention minutes say that the Steering Committee decided to ask sessions to call a Convocation of Sessions; they do not give the date of that decision.
