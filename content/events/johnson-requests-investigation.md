After the Revoice conference, several individuals, sessions and presbyteries sent concerns to Memorial Presbyterian Church and to Missouri Presbytery. On October 10, 2018, TE Greg Johnson asked the presbytery for a BCO 31-2 investigation of the allegations against him. The Memorial session asked the presbytery to accept, as a BCO 41 reference, an investigation of the session's hosting of the conference. (The facts here come from the Standing Judicial Commission's summary in a later case.)
