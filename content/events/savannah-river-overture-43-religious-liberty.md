Savannah River Presbytery adopted Overture 43, "Expression of Support for Sanctity of Life and Marriage." It asked the 42nd General Assembly to express support for Christians in private business who faced fines, penalties and ostracism for declining, out of religious conviction, to provide services for same-sex wedding ceremonies.
