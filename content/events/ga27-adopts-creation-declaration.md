A personal resolution referred to the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures noted that the PCA was divided over the nature of the "days" in Genesis 1. On the committee's recommendation, the 27th General Assembly adopted an amended declaration of ten points. It said that Genesis 1 and 2 is a historic and true account of creation in six days and not a myth. It also said that God made Adam directly from the dust and not from a lower animal form, made Eve from Adam, and that all mankind except Christ descended from them. The last point denied macroevolution. Overtures to the 40th General Assembly quoted this declaration.
