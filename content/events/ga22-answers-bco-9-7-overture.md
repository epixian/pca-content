Southeast Alabama Presbytery overtured the 22nd General Assembly to encourage all PCA churches to use the ministry of "godly men and women" appointed to assist the deacons under BCO 9-7. The Assembly answered that the Constitutional Standards were a sufficient testimony on the matter.
