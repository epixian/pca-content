Grace Presbytery's Overture 7 asked the 44th General Assembly to petition government leaders to protect the religious liberty of ministers and congregations after the 2015 Supreme Court ruling on same-sex marriage. The overture quoted Westminster Confession 23.3 on the duty of civil magistrates to protect the church. It included a draft petition to the executive and legislative branches of the State of Mississippi, and it was adopted by Grace Presbytery on January 12, 2016. The Assembly referred the overture to the Administrative Committee, with instructions to report back to the 45th General Assembly.
