The 52nd General Assembly answered Great Lakes Presbytery's Overture 47 in the affirmative as amended (1008-333), after the Overtures Committee had recommended it 73-50-0. The committee was to study "the relationship between Christian Nationalism, Ethno-Nationalism, and related teachings," advise on their conformity to the Westminster Standards, and give pastoral guidance, funded by designated gifts of up to $10,000 a year. Earlier that day the Assembly had adopted in gross (1708-28) recommendations that answered the Arizona and South Texas overtures by reference to Overture 47.
