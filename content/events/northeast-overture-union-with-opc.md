Northeast Presbytery adopted Overture 5 to the 24th General Assembly, "Instruct IRC to Discuss Union With OPC." It said that the Orthodox Presbyterian Church was the church with the greatest affinity to the PCA, that the OPC had difficulty with joining and receiving, and that it was "time to break the impasse." It asked the Assembly to instruct the Interchurch Relations Committee to discuss the OPC's concerns candidly, with a view to union.
