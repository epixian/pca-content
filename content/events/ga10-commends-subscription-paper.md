The 10th General Assembly received the paper "Confessional Subscription" from its Sub-committee on Judicial Business and commended it to the presbyteries, sessions and boards of deacons for study. It also told the sub-committee to prepare a BCO amendment. Under the amendment, ordinands would give written declarations of any scruples about the Westminster Confession and Catechisms, and the presbytery would record its evaluation of each scruple.
