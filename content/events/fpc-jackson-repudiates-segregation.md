After the 43rd General Assembly asked sessions to consider sins of racial prejudice, the session of First Presbyterian Church in Jackson, Mississippi, formed an ad hoc committee. At its May 2016 meeting the session rescinded and repudiated actions in its minutes from the 1950s and 1960s that excluded African Americans from worship, and it repudiated "the 1954 actions of our Session affirming segregation of the races." At a town hall meeting for the officers, TE Ligon Duncan and TE Jim Baird spoke about the church's past. The Aquila Report published the session's statement.
