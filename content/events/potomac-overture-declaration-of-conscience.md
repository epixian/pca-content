Potomac Presbytery's Overture 16 asked the 21st General Assembly to adopt a Declaration of Conscience on homosexuality and send it to the President of the United States, with copies to congressional leaders and state governors. The overture cited WCF 31.4 as the basis for such an affirmation.

The adoption line prints "February 13, 1992." The overture printed just before it in the Minutes, also from Potomac Presbytery, gives February 13, 1993, so this date is marked uncertain.
