Northwest Georgia Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to amend BCO 7-2 and 7-3 so that no court may commission or appoint, and no congregation elect or approve, "a woman as a deaconess, shepherdess, or any other pretended office." The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
