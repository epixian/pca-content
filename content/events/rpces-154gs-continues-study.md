The 154th General Synod approved affirmations (a) to (e) of the report. It recommitted affirmation (f) and the recommendation on women deacons, and it continued and enlarged the committee to include more divergent viewpoints. It also asked for a minority report and for a clarification of what ordination of elders and deacons means.
