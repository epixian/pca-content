Potomac Presbytery adopted Overture 15 to the 35th General Assembly, to amend BCO 21-4. The amendment concerned ministers who teach a view that their presbytery has approved as an exception to the doctrinal standards. It asked that any presentation of such a view "include an humble acknowledgment that the view is an exception to the doctrinal standards".
