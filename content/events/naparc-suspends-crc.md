At its 1998 meeting, NAPARC announced the member churches' votes on the proposal to suspend the CRC. The CRC synod voted against, the Korean American Presbyterian Church did not vote, and the other five member churches voted for suspension. NAPARC's list of member churches records the CRC as suspended in 1998.
