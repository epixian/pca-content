The 47th General Assembly answered Chicago Metro Presbytery's Overture 42 in the affirmative as amended, by voice vote. It created an ad interim committee to "study the topic of human sexuality with particular attention to the issues of homosexuality, same-sex attraction, and transgenderism," with seven members appointed by the Moderator and a budget of $25,000 a year from designated gifts. Overtures from Illiana, Ohio Valley and Missouri presbyteries were answered by reference to it.
