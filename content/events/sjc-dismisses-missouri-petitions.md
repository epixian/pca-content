The Standing Judicial Commission decided the 2022 requests from Southeast Alabama and Grace presbyteries. Because TE Greg Johnson's name had been removed from Missouri Presbytery's rolls, it held that "the PCA itself no longer has jurisdiction over TE Johnson" and that it could not assume original jurisdiction. The vote was 22-1.
