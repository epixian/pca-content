Gulfstream Presbytery's Overture 6 asked the 45th General Assembly to add Leviticus 18:22 and Romans 1:26-27 to the proof texts of Westminster Confession of Faith 24:4. It said that the current proof texts did not explicitly prohibit same-sex marriages, and it cited the Obergefell v. Hodges decision. The Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer (96-3-1), and the Assembly adopted it in gross.
