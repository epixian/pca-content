In June 2024 the Stated Clerk of Rio Grande Presbytery received two letters about posts by TE Zachary Garris on X about slavery, after an exchange with a Mission to the World staff member. One letter, dated June 6, had 21 signers; the other had 60 signers from many presbyteries. Both asked the presbytery to investigate "in keeping with BCO 31-2."
