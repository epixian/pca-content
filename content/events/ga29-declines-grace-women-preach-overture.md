Grace Presbytery's Overture 25, "Forbid Women to Preach or Teach in Corporate Worship," asked the 29th General Assembly to affirm that a woman may not preach or teach in the corporate worship of any church or church court, and to reaffirm that the offices of elder and deacon are open to men only. The Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said that the constitution (BCO 7-2) addressed the issues sufficiently and that the Assembly should not define constitutional principles by majority vote.
