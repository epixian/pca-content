North Florida Presbytery's Overture 50 to the 44th General Assembly accepted that "the power of the church is exclusively spiritual," but it said that "some of our churches have abused the spirituality of the church" by failing to promote, or by opposing, the equal dignity of all races. Among the sins it asked officers to examine was "abusing the doctrine of the spirituality of the church in order to avoiding speaking to injustice." The Assembly answered the overture by reference to Overture 43.
