The Interchurch Relations Committee recommended that the 49th General Assembly answer Pee Dee Presbytery's Overture 3 in the negative, and its committee of commissioners offered a substitute to answer it in the affirmative. After debate, the Assembly adopted the substitute (1030-699) and, on a revote, adopted it again (1059-681), withdrawing the PCA from the National Association of Evangelicals. TE Roy Taylor, chairman of the IRC Permanent Committee, filed a protest that others signed; it said that the withdrawal "casts doubt on the PCA's practice of catholicity."
