Southern Florida Presbytery, citing "confusion, uncertainty, inconsistency and discord" over "Theonomy" or "Christian Reconstructionism," asked for a study committee to "examine, define, and defend or refute" those teachings. The 24th General Assembly answered the overture in the negative, on the grounds that the Assembly had studied the matter before and that sufficient materials existed by Reformed scholars.
