The 111th General Assembly of the PCUS, at Massanetta Springs near Harrisonburg, Virginia, voted 217 to 207 to reduce its synods from fifteen to seven along new boundaries. It voted 213 to 189 to stay in the National Council of Churches (NCC) and 216 to 185 to stay in the World Council of Churches (WCC). It approved continuing union negotiations with the United Presbyterian Church in the U.S.A. by 260 to 119 and added to the union committee two members who were "not happy" with the present plan. Christianity Today reported that conservatives saw the synod plan as a way to weaken presbyteries opposed to union.
