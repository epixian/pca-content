The 17th General Assembly voted to continue to exchange observers with the Evangelical Presbyterian Church (EPC). It decided not to seek a relationship of ecclesiastical fellowship with the EPC because the EPC provided for the ordination of women as ruling and teaching elders. A substitute motion to enter into fraternal relations with the EPC was defeated, and eight commissioners recorded their votes for it.
