On the report of the Committee of Commissioners on Mission to North America, the 25th General Assembly answered the Presbytery of New Jersey's Overture 6 in the negative. The amended grounds said that seminars led by women on biblical and theological exposition would have women as the intended audience. They also said that only men are to be elders, that Lord's Day preaching is done by elders under the session, and that the ministry of women in the church, including MNA, must stay within the bounds of Scripture, including 1 Timothy 2:11-12. The 27th General Assembly later referred to these grounds.
