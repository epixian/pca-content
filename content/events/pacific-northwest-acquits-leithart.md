In October 2010, after the commission's decision in Case 2009-6, Pacific Northwest Presbytery appointed a prosecutor. Its nine-member judicial commission tried TE Peter Leithart on June 3–4, 2011, on five charges about baptism, the covenants, imputation, justification and union with Christ. On October 7, 2011, the presbytery adopted the commission's unanimous not-guilty judgments on all five charges, by votes from 33-4-3 to 30-5-2. A complaint against the verdict was denied on April 27, 2012, and was carried to the Standing Judicial Commission (Case 2012-05).
