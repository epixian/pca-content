The 30th General Assembly adopted the BCO 12-5.e amendment after it received the concurrence of more than two-thirds of the presbyteries. The amended paragraph gives the session authority over worship and adds that it is "to ensure that the Word of God is preached only by such men as are sufficiently qualified (BCO 4-4, 53-2, 1 Timothy 2:11-12)."
