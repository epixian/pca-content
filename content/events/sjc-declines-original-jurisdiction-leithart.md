The 41st General Assembly referred Overtures 20, 21 and 22 to the Standing Judicial Commission, which answered them in the negative, 15-1 with one abstention. It held that BCO 34-1 could not be used because Pacific Northwest Presbytery had not "refused to act": it had indicted TE Peter Leithart, tried him, and dealt with the complaint. It added that the protection against double jeopardy barred a second trial on the same case, though new teaching could give new grounds. A failure to declare a mistrial after a completed trial was not a refusal to act. The response appears in the commission's report to the 42nd General Assembly; the minutes print no date for the vote.
