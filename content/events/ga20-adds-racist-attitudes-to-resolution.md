The 20th General Assembly adopted an amended personal resolution (Personal Resolution 4) about attacks on Christian moral standards and family values. The resolution listed many results of anti-Christian activity, and the Assembly added "racist attitudes and activities" to that list. The resolution then stated the Assembly's support for biblical values. TE Eugene Case dissented from the addition of the words "racist attitudes and activities." He said that the media and others could define "racist" to include any attitude that did not agree with their own opinions or programs. Four other commissioners asked to add their names to the dissent.
