Evangel Presbytery (Overture 7), Central Carolina Presbytery (Overture 2, October
27, 2009), Eastern Carolina Presbytery (Overture 9, January 23, 2010), and
Westminster Presbytery (Overture 13, April 10, 2010) asked the 38th General
Assembly to amend BCO 9-7. Evangel's overture proposed that those who assist the
deacons "are not subjects for ordination"; the others sought to keep assistants to
the deacons from being treated as deaconesses or office bearers.
