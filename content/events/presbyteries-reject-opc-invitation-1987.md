The presbyteries voted on the invitation that the 14th General Assembly had reissued to the Orthodox Presbyterian Church. At the 15th General Assembly in 1987, the Stated Clerk reported that 24 presbyteries had voted for it and 16 against. The invitation failed because it did not have the votes of two-thirds of the presbyteries.
