The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records cited Pacific Northwest Presbytery with an exception of substance. At its January 2010 meeting the presbytery had granted an exception to a man who disagreed with Larger Catechism 177 and believed that young children may come to the Lord's Table. The committee judged this difference to be out of accord with the system of doctrine. A minority report argued that the presbytery is the proper court to judge a candidate's differences. The 39th General Assembly did not adopt the minority report and adopted the committee's recommendation.
