The 25th General Assembly had taken exception to Southern Florida Presbytery's minutes of April 16, 1996, because a service to organize a church and install its pastor included a woman who led in the opening prayer and another who read Scripture before the sermon. The presbytery replied by asking for the grounds of the exception. On the recommendation of the Committee on Review of Presbytery Records, the 26th General Assembly found the reply unsatisfactory and referred the presbytery to 1 Corinthians 14:34, 1 Timothy 2:11-15, and Larger Catechism questions 155-159.
