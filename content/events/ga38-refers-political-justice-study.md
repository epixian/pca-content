Ascension Presbytery's Overture 23 asked the 38th General Assembly to erect a study committee on political and economic justice. One of its questions was "What are the duties of the visible church relative to political and economic justice". The Assembly referred the overture to the Committee on Christian Education and Publications. In 2011 that committee reported that it was willing but, for lack of funds and personnel, was unable to take on the assignment.
