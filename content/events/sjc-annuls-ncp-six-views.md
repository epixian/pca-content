In Cases 2009-25/26 (TE David Brown and the session of Grace Presbyterian Church, Pleasanton, against Northern California Presbytery), the Standing Judicial Commission held that the presbytery erred when it adopted an abstract statement of the views on the diaconate that its ministers and sessions could hold. A church court binds only those under its jurisdiction in a proper case, such as the examination of a candidate or a judicial process. The Commission annulled the presbytery's action. It stated that its judgment gave no opinion on whether the six views were faithful to the Constitution. The vote was 20 concurring, 4 absent. A concurring opinion urged church courts to address specific cases rather than adopt broad statements.
