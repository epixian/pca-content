Ascension Presbytery's Overture 9 asked that an officer who preaches or teaches a granted exception candidly say that his view is exceptional. The 33rd General Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said the language restricted the judgment of presbyteries and sessions and might conflict with WCF 20.2.
