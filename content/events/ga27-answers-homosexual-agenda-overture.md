The 27th General Assembly answered Westminster Presbytery's Overture 22, "Warning Regarding Homosexual Agenda," by reference to the 5th General Assembly's 1977 actions, which it printed in full. One of them said that a practicing homosexual continuing in this sin would not be a fit candidate for ordination or membership. The Assembly added a statement that encouraged congregations to study the Scriptures, to pray, and to speak and act pastorally for their children, and it encouraged sessions to warn parents about what was promoted through government schools. It named Harvest USA as a resource.
