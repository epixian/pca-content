The Synod of 1996 of the Christian Reformed Church in North America (CRCNA) sent the PCA a reply, dated June 28, 1996. It said that its 1995 Synod had let classes authorize churches to ordain women as elders, ministers and evangelists, and that it respectfully disagreed with the PCA's call to repent and rescind. It also said that a move to remove the CRCNA from NAPARC would likely end the two churches' ecclesiastical fellowship.
