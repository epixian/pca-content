The 35th General Assembly adopted the RPR recommendation on the minutes of
Philadelphia Presbytery. It took an exception of substance to the minutes of
November 11, 2006, because the diaconate of a new church included four
commissioned deaconesses. A later overture names the church as Liberti Church.
