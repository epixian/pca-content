Calvary Presbytery's Overture 8 asked the 29th General Assembly to condemn the SJC's action in the John Wood matter. The Assembly answered it in the negative with reference to a communication from Western Carolina Presbytery, which had started the inquiry. In that communication Western Carolina said it accepted the SJC's decision and called the PCA to bring the matter to closure; some commissioners recorded negative votes.
