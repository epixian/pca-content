Ohio Valley Presbytery's Overture 8 asked the 47th General Assembly to amend BCO 14-1 so that non-ordained members could serve on the Assembly's permanent committees and agency boards. It cited the 2017 recommendation that courts consider overtures to let qualified women serve on committees and agencies. Overtures from Southern New England, Rocky Mountain, Nashville and Tennessee Valley presbyteries asked for similar changes. The Overtures Committee said that non-ordained persons, who have not taken a vow to uphold the Constitution, should not be part of the decision-making process. A motion to recommit failed (442-471), a minority report failed (274-639), and the Assembly answered Overture 8 in the negative (618-260). It answered the other four by reference to Overture 8.
