Tennessee Valley Presbytery asked the 17th General Assembly for a study of whether women could serve on the permanent committees and agencies of the General Assembly. The Bills and Overtures committee recommended denial, on the ground that such service would let women exercise ruling authority in the church, but the Assembly sent the question to the Committee on Judicial Business. On that committee's report, the Assembly answered that a non-ordained person may not vote on a General Assembly committee, and that no provision lets a woman serve on an ad interim committee, though women might serve in an advisory capacity. It answered the overture by reference to these answers.
