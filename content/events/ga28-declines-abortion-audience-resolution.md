A personal resolution from a ruling elder asked the 28th General Assembly to direct the Stated Clerk to request an audience with government officials who had not responded to the Assembly's earlier communication on abortion. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures, the Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said the Assembly's views were already expressed and communicated, and that it would be impractical for the Stated Clerk to speak further for the Assembly. One ruling elder recorded a negative vote.
