The 12th General Assembly answered Delmarva Presbytery's Overture 28 in the negative. It referred to 1 Timothy 3:8, 12, Acts 6:3 and BCO 9-3 and 9-7. A motion to add Romans 16:1 to the references failed. Many commissioners asked that their negative votes be recorded.
