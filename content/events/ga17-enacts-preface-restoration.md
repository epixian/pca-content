On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business, the 17th General Assembly answered Eastern Canada Presbytery's Overture 22 in the negative. The grounds said the language came from the 1788 Form of Government and had been removed by a clerical error. The Assembly then adopted the amendment to BCO Preface II(1) that restored the two sentences, after the presbytery vote that the Minutes record.
