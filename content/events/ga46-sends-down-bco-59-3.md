Four overtures to the 46th General Assembly asked it to give BCO 59 on marriage constitutional status: Calvary Presbytery's Overture 1 (its overture recommitted from the 45th Assembly) and Overture 5, Grace Presbytery's Overture 2, and Tennessee Valley Presbytery's Overture 24, which also revised the chapter. The Overtures Committee first recommended answering them in the negative, and the Assembly recommitted them to the committee. The committee then proposed to amend BCO 59-3 to say that marriage is "only to be between one man and one woman" and that ministers in the PCA "shall only solemnize marriages between one man and one woman," and to give BCO 59-3 alone full constitutional status. The Assembly adopted this answer to Overture 24 (764-12-7) and sent the amendment to the presbyteries.
