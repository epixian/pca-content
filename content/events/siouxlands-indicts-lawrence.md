In January 2010 the presbytery's second investigative committee recommended, 6-0, a strong presumption of guilt. At TE Greg Lawrence's request the presbytery postponed action and formed a committee to instruct him on its report and on the Assembly's nine declarations. In September 2010 a substitute motion that would have found no strong presumption failed, and the moderator announced that the effect was to find a strong presumption of guilt. The presbytery set up a judicial commission. The indictment, filed February 20, 2011, had five charges: on the covenant of works, baptism, the reprobate and baptism, perseverance, and assurance.
