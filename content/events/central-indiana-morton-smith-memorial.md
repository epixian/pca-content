TE Morton H. Smith, the PCA's first Stated Clerk, died on November 12, 2017. Calvary and Heritage presbyteries sent memorials for him to the 46th General Assembly. Central Indiana Presbytery's memorial (Overture 38) honored his service but also said that "his publicly stated views on race, segregation, and slavery have created challenges for many brothers and sisters in our denomination," and it quoted the 44th General Assembly's confession.
