The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records cited Chesapeake Presbytery because, at its May 15, 2012 meeting, it had given a session four years to come into compliance with BCO 7-2 and 9-3. The church was Loch Raven Presbyterian Church. When it was received into the PCA in 1986, a commitment had apparently been made to let women continue to serve as deaconesses. The presbytery answered that the church planned to move to a diaconate of only ordained men, and the 43rd General Assembly found this response unsatisfactory. In 2016 the committee noted that the church's new bylaws still let women be elected and commissioned to the Board of Deacons.
