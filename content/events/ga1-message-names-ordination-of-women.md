The first General Assembly of the new church, then called the National Presbyterian Church, adopted "A Message to All Churches of Jesus Christ Throughout the World" on December 6; the Message is dated December 7, 1973. The Message explained the founders' separation from the Presbyterian Church in the United States (PCUS). It named the ordination of women, with other practices, as a deviation from historic Presbyterian positions that it traced to a different view of Scripture.
