Oklahoma Presbytery adopted Overture 2, which reached the 13th General Assembly. It said that BCO 3-4 was "out of accord with Scripture," and it objected to the clause that "the constitution of the State must be determined by human reason and the course of providential events" rather than by divine revelation. It proposed new wording for BCO 3-4 that kept the denial of civil jurisdiction to church courts and added that "the Church does stand to call the civil government to its God-ordained purpose and function."
