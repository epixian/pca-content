The PCA's Interchurch Relations Committee prepared a rationale for terminating the CRC's membership and presented it to NAPARC's fall 2001 meeting. According to the IRC's report, NAPARC voted unanimously to recommend that the member churches' assemblies and synods terminate the CRC's membership.
