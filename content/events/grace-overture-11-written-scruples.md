Grace Presbytery sent Overture 11 to the 11th General Assembly. It referred to the 10th General Assembly's request for a BCO amendment on written scruples. It asked the Assembly to amend BCO 19-2 so that a candidate for licensure would read and file a written statement of his subscription to the Confession and Catechisms and of any scruple about them. The Minutes do not print the date on which the presbytery adopted the overture.
