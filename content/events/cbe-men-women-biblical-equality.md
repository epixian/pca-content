At its first conference, CBE presented its statement "Men, Women and Biblical Equality". The statement taught the full equality of men and women in creation and redemption, the use of spiritual gifts by women and men at all levels of church ministry, and mutual submission in marriage. A CBE board member said that the release of the Danvers Statement had made the work on the statement go faster.
