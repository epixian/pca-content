Evangel Presbytery adopted Overture 14 to the 48th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to direct the Committee on Mission to the World to add to the MTW Policy Manual: "All MTW leaders in line authority over church planting or church development ministry shall be ordained elders." It also asked that any change to this statement need the Assembly's approval. Its rationale said that MTW had already revised its guidelines after objections, but that without a policy statement the guidelines could be changed again.
