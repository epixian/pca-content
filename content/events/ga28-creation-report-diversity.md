The Creation Study Committee reported to the 28th General Assembly that it could not agree on the nature and duration of the creation days. The Assembly defeated the committee's recommendations as a unit. It then distributed the report, dismissed the committee, and added a recommendation. That recommendation affirmed that the diversity of views in the report was acceptable as long as the full historicity of the creation account was accepted.
