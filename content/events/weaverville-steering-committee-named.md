At a rally led by the Presbyterian Journal, the leaders of the Journal, Concerned Presbyterians, Presbyterian Churchmen United and the Presbyterian Evangelistic Fellowship said that they would form a new church, and named a steering committee to guide conservatives in the PCUS toward it. W. Jack Williamson, secretary of Concerned Presbyterians, said that congregations could use the escape clause in the proposed plan of union with the United Presbyterian Church to stay out of the union and keep their property. L. Nelson Bell resigned from the Journal's board because he opposed the plan, and the Covenant Fellowship of Presbyterians said it would oppose any withdrawal.
