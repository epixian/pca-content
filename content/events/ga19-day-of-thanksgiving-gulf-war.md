The 19th General Assembly answered Heritage Presbytery's Overture 41, "Calling a Day of Thanksgiving for War Victory," in the affirmative as amended. The Minutes print the original text in brackets and the amended text after it.

The original text gave thanks for an Allied "victory over the forces of Iraq," set the day for July 4, 1991, and told the Stated Clerk to send copies to the President, Congress and military leaders. The amended text gave thanks for the end of the war, called a day of thanksgiving on July 7, 1991, and sent the resolution only to the churches. The Minutes give no grounds for the amendment and print no presbytery adoption line.
