Missouri Presbytery sent Overture 16, "Marriage and Sexuality," to the 32nd General Assembly. It pointed to the 1978 statement against legalized abortion as a precedent for stating "the demands of biblical justice for the civil commonwealth" without endorsing particular legislative strategies. As sent, its main resolve asked the Assembly to call on the federal governments of Canada and the United States to ensure that marriage is legally defined as between one man and one woman, "(even constitutional amendment, if deemed necessary)." It also asked the Assembly to declare the biblical teaching on marriage and to call on the church to strengthen marriages and encourage chastity. The original wording is known from the strikethrough marks on m32ga p. 175; the Minutes print the overture only as amended. The Minutes do not print the presbytery's adoption date.
