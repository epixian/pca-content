Pee Dee Presbytery asked the 51st General Assembly to give BCO 53 ("The Preaching of the Word"), a chapter of the Directory for Worship, full constitutional status. Its proposed text replaced "person" with "qualified man" in the rule on who may be invited to preach or exhort with the session's consent.
