TE L. Roy Taylor, TE Paul D. Kooistra, TE R.C. Sproul and TE Woodrow Wilson Benton filed an answer to the CoMission protest, and more commissioners added their names. It said that the Assembly had never declared the PCA a "strict subscriptionist" church. It described the PCA's practice as sincere adoption of the system of doctrine, with "tolerable exceptions" made known to the court and judged by it. The Assembly received the answer and spread it on its Minutes as a personal response of the signers.
