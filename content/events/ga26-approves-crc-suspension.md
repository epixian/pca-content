NAPARC asked its member churches to vote on its November 1997 resolution to suspend the CRC, and the CRC's Interchurch Relations Committee wrote to the PCA to urge a vote against suspension. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations, the 26th General Assembly approved the proposal that the CRC be suspended from membership in NAPARC. The IRC, which the 25th Assembly had told to recommend a future relationship with the CRC, reported that it had no recommendation other than to continue discussions.
