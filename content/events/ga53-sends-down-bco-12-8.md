Ascension Presbytery's Overture 72 proposed a new BCO 12-8, citing the failure of Savannah River's 2025 overture on diaconate reporting. The Overtures Committee recommended it as amended (85-42); its amended text kept only the requirement that each Session record annually a list of the members of the Session and the Diaconate. The 53rd General Assembly adopted a BCO 12-8 amendment, which the Administrative Committee listed among the amendments sent to the presbyteries.
