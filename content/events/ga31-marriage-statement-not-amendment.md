The 31st General Assembly answered Overture 19 with a statement. The statement quoted WCF 24-1 and 24-2 on marriage between one man and one woman, and added a warning from Proverbs 14:34 about any nation that defies God's law on the family. The Assembly did not endorse the proposed amendment. The grounds said the church is spiritual in nature and, while it must speak to moral issues, it should ordinarily refrain from endorsing specific legislation (WCF 31-4, BCO 3-3, BCO 3-4).
