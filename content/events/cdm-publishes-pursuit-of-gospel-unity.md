As the 46th General Assembly had directed, the Committee on Discipleship Ministries published the 2018 report together with the actions of the 30th, 32nd and 44th General Assemblies "as the PCA's statements on the Gospel and racial reconciliation." The book was titled *The Pursuit of Gospel Unity: The PCA Papers on Racism and Racial Reconciliation*.
