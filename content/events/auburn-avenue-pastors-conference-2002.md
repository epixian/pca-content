Auburn Avenue Presbyterian Church in Monroe, Louisiana, a PCA church in Louisiana Presbytery, held a pastors' conference in 2002. The General Assembly's study committee later wrote that the church "has hosted conferences where their ideas have been promoted", and that "one of the issues raised early on in the 2002 Auburn Avenue Pastor's Conference was 'baptismal regeneration.'" In a lecture there, TE Steve Wilkins said that "we can speak of baptismal regeneration in this sense": "by the blessing of the Spirit, baptism unites us to Christ and his church." The committee noted that Wilkins and others later stopped using the term.

The views taught at these conferences were first called "Auburn Avenue Theology", after the church, and later "Federal Vision".
