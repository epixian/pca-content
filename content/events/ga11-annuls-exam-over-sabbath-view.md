North Georgia Presbytery sustained the exam of a licentiate who transferred his license from another presbytery. In the exam he said that he did not believe the first day of the week is the Christian Sabbath. On a complaint (Case 7), the judicial commission of the 11th General Assembly sustained the specification of error and annulled the presbytery's action. It directed the presbytery to instruct him in "this fundamental element of doctrine" and to re-examine him if his view came into conformity with the Standards. The commission's report was adopted as the Assembly's judgment, and several commissioners recorded negative votes.
