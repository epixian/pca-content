The Standing Judicial Commission decided SJC 2008-1 (the Crossroads session) and
SJC 2008-10 (RE Mark Grasso et al.) together. It held that Philadelphia Presbytery
did not err in approving Jason Hsu for licensure or for ordination, and it denied
the complaints by a vote of 10 concurring, 3 dissenting, and 10 absent.
