On its first evening, the Advisory Convention of the Continuing Presbyterian Church, with 306 voting representatives of 165 churches, unanimously adopted a resolution to call a General Assembly at Briarwood Presbyterian Church, Birmingham, Alabama, on December 4, 1973. The resolution said that more than 200 congregations had already withdrawn from the PCUS. Later in the convention, it asked the Steering Committee to keep an advisory role until the four organizations that formed it dissolved it.
