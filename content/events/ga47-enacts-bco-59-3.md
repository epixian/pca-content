The presbyteries approved the amendment to BCO 59-3 sent down by the 46th General Assembly, 77 to 3. The 47th General Assembly then adopted it in gross with two other amendments (1030-105). BCO 59-3 now says that ministers in the PCA "shall only solemnize marriages between one man and one woman," and it has full constitutional status.
