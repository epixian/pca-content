The 35th General Assembly adopted the Overtures Committee's recommendation to answer Potomac Presbytery's Overture 15 in the negative without prejudice. The Assembly commended the matter of teaching approved exceptions to the church for further study. The committee reported that it had debated the overture for several hours.
