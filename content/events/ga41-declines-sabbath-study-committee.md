North Texas Presbytery's Overture 7 said that many PCA officers take stated differences to the Standards' requirements for keeping the Sabbath, especially the prohibition of recreation, and that these differences are commonly allowed. It asked the 41st General Assembly to appoint a study committee to consider amendments to WCF 21.8, WLC 117 and 119, and WSC 60 and 61. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative. Its grounds said that presbyteries with concerns should first propose specific changes to the Standards.
