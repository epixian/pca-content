The Committee on Review of Presbytery Records proposed an amendment to RAO 14-3.e.5 at the 32nd General Assembly. The amendment was carried over and postponed twice. The 34th General Assembly adopted it as amended by a two-thirds vote. Presbytery minutes must now record how the court judged each stated difference, and whether the court let the candidate teach and preach it.
