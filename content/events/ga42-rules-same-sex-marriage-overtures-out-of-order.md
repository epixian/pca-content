Westminster Presbytery sent two overtures to the 42nd General Assembly: Overture 2, "Concerning Same-sex Marriage", and Overture 5, "Concerning Our Present Need". Both asked the Assembly to reaffirm the PCA's earlier position on homosexuality and to address civil legislation on same-sex marriage. On a point of order, the Moderator ruled the committee's recommendations on both overtures out of order, because they asked for reaffirmation of positions already adopted. The Assembly sustained the ruling on appeal.
