The 15th General Assembly answered in the negative three overtures that asked it to leave the National Association of Evangelicals: Overture 15 from Northeast Presbytery, Overture 18 from Warrior Presbytery, and Overture 16, which a session had sent and which three teaching elders presented after Grace Presbytery rejected it. The Assembly defeated a substitute that would have rescinded the 1986 decision and asked the Interchurch Relations Committee to explore a cooperative agreement with the NAE instead. Four teaching elders recorded negative votes.
