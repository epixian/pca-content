The 36th General Assembly adopted the RPR recommendation that Philadelphia
Presbytery's response was unsatisfactory. The rationale agreed that BCO 9-3 would
not apply to unordained women who were not members of the diaconate. It noted
that the record said "four deaconesses and one deacon were commissioned" to the
diaconate, and that only ordained and elected men can be members of a diaconate.
