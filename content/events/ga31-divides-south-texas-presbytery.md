The 31st General Assembly granted a South Texas Presbytery overture, amended to form two presbyteries. Houston Metro Presbytery took southeast Texas, inside a line of named counties from Leon to Sabine and from Leon to Matagorda. South Texas Presbytery kept the area south of a line of named counties from Val Verde to Calhoun. The division took effect on January 1, 2004.
