The 21st General Assembly answered a letter from the Christian Reformed Church (CRC) with a resolution. The resolution thanked God that the CRC Synod of 1992 did not ratify a change to its Church Order that would have opened the offices of minister, elder and evangelist to women. It also told the CRC that the PCA would continue to pray for it.
