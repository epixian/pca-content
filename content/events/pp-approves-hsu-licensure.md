At its stated meeting, Philadelphia Presbytery examined and approved ministerial
candidate Jason Hsu for licensure. He stated a difference with BCO 7-2: he
believed women could hold the office of deacon, but he affirmed that women should
not be ordained as deacons. In his later ordination examination, the presbytery's
Leadership Development Team classified this difference as "technically" an
exception to church government, not to the confessional standards.
