At Eastern Heights Presbyterian Church in Savannah, Georgia, six ministers and ten elders from congregations that had left the PCUS, or were trying to leave it, signed a resolution to form Vanguard Presbytery, a provisional presbytery. They elected Todd Allen moderator and agreed that he should accept an invitation to join the Steering Committee for a Continuing Presbyterian Church. L. Nelson Bell, moderator of the PCUS, issued a statement of regret.
