RE Dudley Reese and TE Niel Bech complained on July 2, 2011, that the presbytery
had not allowed questions to TE Hsu or City Line Church. In executive session,
Philadelphia Presbytery voted to declare the complaint out of order. The
complainants took the complaint to the General Assembly on October 21, 2011.
