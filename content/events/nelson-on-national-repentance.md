In The Aquila Report, TE Jared Nelson applied C. S. Lewis's essay "The Dangers of National Repentance" to the question before the PCA. He warned that in covenantal repentance one must not be "confessing the sins of others," but he concluded that "covenantal repentance is valid" if it is made with solemnness and "perhaps reluctance."
