The Minutes print the heading as "From the Presbytery of St. Louis (Missouri)." The presbytery adopted an overture, printed as Overture 3 to the 13th General Assembly, to add a paragraph to BCO 13-7. A minister who differed with part of the Confession of Faith and Catechisms would enter his differences with his subscription, and his standing would be subject to the judgment of his presbytery.
