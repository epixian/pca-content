Citing Recommendation 3 of the 2017 women study report, Nashville Presbytery asked the 46th General Assembly to amend BCO 14-1.11 and the corporate bylaws so that women could serve on the boards of agencies. Tennessee Valley Presbytery asked for a minority of seats on the Covenant College board to be open to non-ordained members. The Assembly ruled the bylaw changes out of order and answered both BCO overtures in the negative. The Overtures Committee's grounds quoted, as an answer of a previous General Assembly, the 1989 Bills and Overtures committee's statement that such service would let women exercise ruling authority in the church.
