When Liberti Church was organized as a particular church, the organizing
commission of Philadelphia Presbytery did not ordain the one man elected as a
deacon. Instead it commissioned him with the four women the congregation elected.
The record of the service called this the "Vows/Commissioning of Diaconate". The
presbytery's minutes of November 11, 2006 record it.
