In January 2019 the Committee on Mission to the World adopted a "Statement on Valuing Women in MTW," with guidelines that opened leadership roles to women while stating that only ordained elders exercise ecclesiastical authority over churches. Heritage Presbytery's Overture 41 asked the 47th General Assembly to direct MTW to amend its guidelines so that only ordained elders could serve as team leader, regional director, or international director. The Stated Clerk referred it to MTW. The committee of commissioners recommended a negative answer with grounds, and the Assembly recommitted the recommendation to MTW (712-526).
