Southeast Louisiana Presbytery's Overture 7 argued that section 16 of the commission's manual, its procedure for hearing a memorial (the procedure used for Central Carolina's memorial on Louisiana Presbytery), let the Assembly "censure a presbytery without a trial" and was unconstitutional. The commission proposed a new section 16, because BCO 40-5 no longer mentioned memorials after 2006; it now dealt with a "Report arising out of General Review and Control." On June 17, 2009, the 37th General Assembly approved the amendment and answered Overture 7 by reference to it.
