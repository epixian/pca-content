The 5th General Assembly answered North Georgia Presbytery's Overture 11 in the affirmative and adopted it as amended. The Assembly also adopted a resolution that stated three points: the act of homosexuality is a sin, churches should seek to lead the homosexual person to repentance, and a practicing homosexual who continues in this sin "would not be a fit candidate for ordination or membership" in the PCA.
