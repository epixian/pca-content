In Case 2007-8, TE James Jones et al. v. Louisiana Presbytery, the Standing Judicial Commission sustained the complaint 22-0, with one absent and one recused. It held that the presbytery had applied the wrong constitutional standard to TE Steve Wilkins' differences with the Standards, and that the record supported a probable finding that it erred when it found no strong presumption of guilt. Because the case turned on interpretation of the Constitution, the commission said it owed the presbytery less deference (BCO 39-3). It annulled the presbytery's denial of the complaint, and the memorial stayed before the commission.
