The 53rd General Assembly voted to make the partial report available to sessions, presbyteries and interested parties, and it extended the committee to report to the 54th General Assembly. The Christian Post reported that the chairman, TE David Strain, told the Assembly, "We wish to be clear about the unqualified wickedness of racism and kinism, antisemitism and all forms of racial prejudice."
