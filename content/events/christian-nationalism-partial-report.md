The committee released a 35-page partial report to the 53rd General Assembly with a pastoral letter, affirmations and denials, and constitutional guidance. Its pastoral letter said the church is not called to "direct the affairs of the state," and it warned against "intemperate and unclean speech" and against forms of Christian nationalism "that embrace forms of antisemitism," race realism and kinism. The committee stated that it lacks authority to declare any view constitutional or unconstitutional.
