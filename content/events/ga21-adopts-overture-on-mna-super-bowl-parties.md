In November 1992, the Committee on Mission to North America (MNA) promoted Super Bowl parties, held on the Lord's Day, as a means of evangelism. Eastern Canada Presbytery's Overture 21 asked the 21st General Assembly to commend MNA's zeal but rebuke it for promoting an activity that did not conform to the Standards, and to warn the church against pragmatism. The Assembly adopted the overture, with a statement from MNA that thanked the presbytery and said, "We have heard the message."
