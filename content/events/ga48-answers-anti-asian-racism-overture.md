Korean Capital Presbytery's Overture 48, printed in English and Korean, asked the Assembly to repudiate anti-Asian racism. The 48th General Assembly (1374-107) answered it with a statement that quotes the 2018 racial reconciliation report on the image of God in all people. The statement expressed grief "together with our AAPI brothers and sisters" over the pain and violence of the past year and assured them of the Assembly's desire to walk together in "the commitments of the Racial Reconciliation Report." Metro Atlanta's Overture 45 on Asian American flourishing was answered by reference to this action, with a time of corporate prayer led by an Asian American commissioner; Overture 46 was answered by reference to Overture 45.
