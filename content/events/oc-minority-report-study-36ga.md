A minority of the Overtures Committee, with 27 signers, recommended an ad interim
study committee on women in diaconal ministry. It named TE Jimmy Agan, TE Ligon
Duncan, TE Tim Keller, and TE Phil Ryken as members, subject to their willingness
to serve. Its grounds listed six questions from Overture 9 that many had found
the BCO insufficient to answer.
