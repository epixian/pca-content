Three overtures on women in MTW leadership came before the 48th General Assembly: Evangel Presbytery's Overture 14, Heritage Presbytery's Overture 3 (which would allow an unordained man in extraordinary cases), and Heritage's Overture 41 from 2019, recommitted to MTW. The MTW Permanent Committee's recommendation was replaced by the committee of commissioners' substitute (918-841). The Assembly then answered Overture 14 in the affirmative (961-814) and answered the two Heritage overtures by reference to it.
