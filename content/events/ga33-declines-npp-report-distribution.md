Mississippi Valley Presbytery asked the Assembly to send its study report on the New Perspective on Paul, N. T. Wright, Norman Shepherd and the "Federal Vision" to all presbytery clerks. It had first sent the report as a communication, and then withdrew it and sent it again as Overture 14. The 33rd General Assembly answered the overture "in the negative without prejudice as to its content," because the Assembly usually distributes only reports that it has debated and adopted.
