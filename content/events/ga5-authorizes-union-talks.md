The 5th General Assembly acted on the report of its Committee on Interchurch Relations. It appointed an ad interim committee of six to write a paper on the Biblical basis of church union and fraternal relations. It let the committee continue talks with other Reformed bodies, including talks on possible union with the RPCES and the OPC. Official action on union was to wait for the paper. In the same action the Assembly answered Grace Presbytery's Overture 9 on ARP membership in NAPARC in the negative.
