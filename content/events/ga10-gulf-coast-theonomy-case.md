A judicial commission of the 10th General Assembly heard a complaint against Gulf Coast Presbytery, which had not sustained a licentiate's ordination examination because of his theonomic views. The commission sustained three of five specifications and instructed the presbytery to re-examine him, without directing the outcome. It urged the presbytery to consider the 7th General Assembly's statement, and it judged that a teaching elder had "helped to aggravate the problem of dissension over theonomy" with intemperate materials.
