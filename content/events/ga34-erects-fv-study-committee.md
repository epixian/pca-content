Four overtures asked the 34th General Assembly to act on the Federal Vision and the New Perspective on Paul. The Bills and Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer to Rocky Mountain Presbytery's Overture 2 and Blue Ridge Presbytery's Overture 11, saying the issues "can be addressed through the judicial process." The Assembly adopted a minority report as a substitute and answered Overture 2 in the affirmative as amended. It erected an ad interim committee to study "the soteriology of the Federal Vision, New Perspective, and Auburn Avenue Theologies," to decide whether these views conform to the Westminster Standards, and to present a declaration. TE Paul Fowler was its chairman. The Assembly answered Missouri Presbytery's request to commend its own Federal Vision report (Overture 26) and Central Carolina's Overture 23 in the negative.
