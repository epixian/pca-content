At a called meeting, Missouri Presbytery considered the 115-page report of its Committee to Investigate Memorial. It approved nine theological judgments and one judicial judgment about TE Greg Johnson and Memorial Church, and required Memorial and TE Johnson to respond. TE Ryan Speck complained against the nine judgments on July 8, 2019.
