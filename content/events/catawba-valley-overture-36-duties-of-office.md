Catawba Valley Presbytery asked the 53rd General Assembly to add BCO 8-11 and 9-8, so that the duties and authority of elders and of deacons are exercised only by ordained men. It said that some congregations let women perform functions "substantially similar to those of deacons and elders" without ordination or title. The Assembly answered the overture with reference to its answer to Overture 37.
