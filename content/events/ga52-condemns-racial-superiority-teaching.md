The 52nd General Assembly answered Tennessee Valley's Overture 48 in the affirmative as amended (1098-231). In the printed minutes, the Overtures Committee's amendment strikes the request for a pastoral-letter committee and puts in its place a resolution that joins the ARP and the RPCNA "in condemning without distinction any theological or political teaching which posits a superiority of race or ethnic identity born of immutable human characteristics." The resolution calls to repentance any who promote or associate themselves with such teaching.
