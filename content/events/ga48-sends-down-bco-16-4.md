The 48th General Assembly answered Gulf Coast Presbytery's Overture 23 in the affirmative as amended (1438-417) and sent it to the presbyteries. The Overtures Committee had moved it into a new BCO 16-4. It said that officers must be above reproach, and that those who profess an identity such as "gay Christian" or "same sex attracted Christian" that undermines their identity as new creations in Christ are not qualified for ordained office. Commissioners recorded their negative votes by name.
