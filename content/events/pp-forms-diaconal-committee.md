Philadelphia Presbytery set up a Presbytery Diaconal Committee to equip deacons
and those who assist them, mainly through conferences. Women who assisted the
deacons in their churches, then called deaconesses, served on the committee. The
presbytery's minutes of September 12, 1992 record the committee with women on it.
