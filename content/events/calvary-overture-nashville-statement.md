Calvary Presbytery adopted an overture (Overture 4 to the 47th General Assembly) asking the Assembly to declare the Council on Biblical Manhood and Womanhood's 2017 Nashville Statement "a biblically faithful declaration." It also asked that the statement go to the Committee on Discipleship Ministries for use in teaching materials.
