The 24th General Assembly answered Personal Resolution 7, "Oppose Legalization of Homosexual Marriages," by reference to earlier actions, including the 1993 Declaration of Conscience, and by a statement adopted as amended. The statement said that government action to legalize homosexual marriage "is an abominable sin calling for God's judgment upon any such society."

The resolution itself, written in response to the Hawaii Supreme Court's 1993 ruling, called on judges, office holders and candidates to oppose such legalization. The Assembly did not adopt the resolution's own text; it answered it with the statement.
