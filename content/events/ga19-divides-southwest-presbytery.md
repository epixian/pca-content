The 19th General Assembly answered a Southwest Presbytery overture in the affirmative. Southwest Presbytery kept Arizona, New Mexico and five far-west Texas counties: El Paso, Hudspeth, Culberson, Jeff Davis and Presidio. Colorado and Wyoming became Rocky Mountain Presbytery. The division took effect at the stated meetings of the presbyteries in the fall of 1991.
