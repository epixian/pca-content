In Case 2011-06, Sawyers et al. v. Missouri Presbytery, the Standing Judicial Commission held, 14-1, that the presbytery erred when it found no strong presumption of guilt that TE Jeffrey Meyers holds views contrary to the Westminster Standards. Because the presbytery had already tried him in April 2012, the commission said the remedy was accomplished and further action was moot.
