The 45th General Assembly answered a Southwest Presbytery overture and divided the presbytery in two. Arizona Presbytery took the state of Arizona. Rio Grande Presbytery took New Mexico and El Paso County, Texas. The division took effect on January 1, 2018. The overture does not name the other four Texas counties that Southwest Presbytery had held since 1991.
