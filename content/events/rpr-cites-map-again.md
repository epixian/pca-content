Metro Atlanta Presbytery answered that its resolution did not violate the BCO and
that the BCO does not require a church to have ordained deacons. The 39th General
Assembly adopted RPR's finding that the response was unsatisfactory. RPR's
rationale said a general practice that prohibits qualified men from serving as
deacons undercuts the office as "ordinary and perpetual."
