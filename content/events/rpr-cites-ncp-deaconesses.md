The 35th General Assembly adopted the RPR recommendation on the minutes of
Northern California Presbytery. It took two exceptions of substance to the
minutes of March 3-4, 2006: the diaconate of a new church included two
commissioned deaconesses, and its deacons were commissioned without election,
ordination, and installation.
