Ascension Presbytery overtured the 19th General Assembly to amend BCO 34-1. If a presbytery refused to act in a doctrinal case or a case of public scandal, the Standing Judicial Commission, at the request of two other presbyteries, would assign the case to a neighboring presbytery. No such case would be tried de novo by a General Assembly commission. The overture said that the General Assembly's judicial commission should be "a court of review and appeal and not a court of default" (BCO 40-5). The 19th General Assembly deferred it, and in 1992 the 20th General Assembly answered it in the affirmative and sent the amendment to the presbyteries.
