In January 2009 Rocky Mountain Presbytery sustained a candidate's ordination exam. The candidate held that 1 Timothy 2:11-12 forbids a woman to preach but lets her teach Scripture to men and women under the session's authority. In Case 2009-7, TE James Urish complained, and the Standing Judicial Commission ruled that the presbytery had not erred, with 20 judges concurring. Some members wrote that they had questions about the candidate's exegesis, and a concurring opinion criticized it.
