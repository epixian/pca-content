The 29th General Assembly answered Western Carolina Presbytery's Overture 35 in the negative, with reference to its action placing Communication 7, a communication on the concurring opinion, at the end of the SJC report. It directed the Standing Judicial Commission to draft procedures to examine concurring and dissenting opinions for temperate language before they are added to its reports. The committee also recommended that the Assembly remove the first concurring opinion "on the basis of justice and fairness". The Moderator ruled that recommendation out of order because it was unconstitutional, and the Assembly sustained his ruling.
