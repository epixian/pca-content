The 29th General Assembly answered Western Carolina Presbytery's Overture 35 in the negative, with reference to its action placing Communication 7, a communication on the concurring opinion, at the end of the SJC report. It directed the Standing Judicial Commission to draft procedures to examine concurring and dissenting opinions for temperate language before they are added to its reports. The Moderator ruled out of order, as unconstitutional, a recommendation to remove the first concurring opinion, and the Assembly sustained the ruling.
