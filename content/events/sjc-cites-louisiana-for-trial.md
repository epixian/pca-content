In Part II of the memorial case (2006-02), the Standing Judicial Commission found that Louisiana Presbytery had met its procedural directions, but it referred to Case 2007-8 on the substance. Because the presbytery had declined to refer the matter to the Assembly, the commission stressed that what was before it was the presbytery's handling, "not allegations against TE Wilkins per se." It cited Louisiana Presbytery under BCO 40-5 and appointed RE Samuel J. Duncan to prosecute it as Case 2007-14, with a plea due by February 1, 2008, and a trial set for March 5, 2008.
