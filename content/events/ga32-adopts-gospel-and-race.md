The 32nd General Assembly adopted "The Gospel and Race: A Pastoral Letter," drafted under Mission to North America, as its reply to Overture 17 of 2003, and it held a fifteen-minute season of prayer. The letter said that both the Northern and Southern Presbyterian traditions had allowed the teaching that some races are inferior and that slavery and segregation are justified, and that "God calls us to repent of the sins of our history." A minority report asked the Assembly to send the letter to the presbyteries for discussion and revision first; the Assembly defeated it.
