Central Carolina Presbytery sent a memorial to the General Assembly. It said that TE Steve Wilkins "has persisted in teaching and publishing doctrines in flagrant contradiction to our Standards" on election, the church, perseverance and baptism, and that Louisiana Presbytery had wrongly exonerated him. It asked the Standing Judicial Commission to take jurisdiction over the investigation of his teaching or, if it declined, to cite Louisiana Presbytery under BCO 40-5. The Stated Clerk referred the memorial to the commission, and the 34th General Assembly concurred. The attestation dates the presbytery's action January 28, 2006; the commission's summary of facts gives January 26.
