Southeast Alabama Presbytery (through a commission, on April 14, 2022) and Grace Presbytery (on May 10, 2022) sent overtures to the 49th General Assembly. Under BCO 34-1, they asked the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction to address alleged doctrinal errors of TE Greg Johnson. The Stated Clerk referred them to the Standing Judicial Commission as Case 2022-12.
