Central Carolina Presbytery adopted Overture 12 to the 39th General Assembly, asking the PCA to withdraw from the National Association of Evangelicals (NAE). The overture quoted the Westminster Confession that synods and councils are to handle "nothing, but that which is ecclesiastical", and it said that "the NAE has frequently intermeddled in public affairs".
