Southwest Florida Presbytery adopted Overture 14 to the 53rd General Assembly, to amend BCO 7-3, 7-4, 9-2, and 9-7 on titles and offices for unordained people. Its rationale said that some churches were functionally creating a second class of offices for women, and it cited recent public efforts to document such practices. An appendix listed titles given to unordained women on church websites "as documented at" savethepca.com.
