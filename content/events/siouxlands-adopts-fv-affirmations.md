Siouxlands Presbytery received the report of the study committee it had formed in 2005 and approved affirmations and denials that compared the New Perspective, Norman Shepherd and the Federal Vision unfavorably with the Westminster Standards. It adopted the finding that their proponents "are outside the system of doctrine." TE Greg Lawrence and two others recorded negative votes. In April 2007 the session of Good Shepherd Presbyterian Church protested, and the presbytery declined to reconsider.
