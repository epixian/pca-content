The Overtures Committee recommended that Pee Dee Presbytery's Overture 3 be answered in the affirmative as amended, so that BCO 53 would gain constitutional status and its last paragraph would read that "only qualified men may be invited to preach" in PCA churches, with session consent. The Assembly defeated the recommendation (857-906-13), answering Overture 3 in the negative. The Assembly answered a related session overture (Overture 29, Bryce Avenue PC), on women preaching in college, seminary and campus ministry services, with reference to Overture 3.
