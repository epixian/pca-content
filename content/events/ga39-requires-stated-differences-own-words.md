The 39th General Assembly amended RAO 16-3.e.5 on the review of presbytery records. The new rule says that presbytery minutes shall record the stated differences of ministers and ministerial candidates with the standards in their own words. The Assembly defeated an amendment to add licensure candidates to the rule. The Committee on Constitutional Business had advised that such an addition conflicts with BCO 19-2.
