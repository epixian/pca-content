Central Carolina Presbytery asked the 37th General Assembly to amend BCO 34-1 so that five presbyteries could ask the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction over a minister, after his presbytery had rendered its judgment or had reasonable time to do so. Its rationale cited the Louisiana case, in which "the entire Presbytery was eventually put on trial" instead of the minister. The Assembly answered the overture in the negative. The Overtures Committee granted that the overture identified "a real problem," but said the remedy would prolong cases and raise "the problem of double jeopardy."
