Overture 41, from the session of Bethel Christian Church in Chicago, asked the Assembly to declare that "Critical Race Theory is seriously flawed in many respects." The 49th General Assembly postponed it indefinitely (1193-303). The Overtures Committee cited Robert's Rules: the motion disposes of a main motion "that cannot be either adopted or expressly rejected without possibly undesirable consequences."
