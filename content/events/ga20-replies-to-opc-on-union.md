The 58th General Assembly of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church (1991) wrote to the PCA. It reaffirmed its desire for closer union and asked the PCA to reconsider its "rejection of organic union by any process except J & R." The 20th General Assembly adopted a reply. The reply said that joining and receiving remained the best route to unity, that the PCA stood ready to consider a request from the OPC to be received, and that the Interchurch Relations Committee remained available for discussion. One teaching elder recorded a negative vote.
