Tennessee Valley Presbytery adopted Overture 48, asking the 52nd General Assembly for an unfunded committee to write a pastoral letter on Christian citizenship and church-state relations. It argued that "an explication of principles may prove more helpful than a focus on persons or rapidly changing movements."
