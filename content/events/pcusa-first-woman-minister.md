The Presbyterian Church in the U.S.A. (PCUSA), the Northern Presbyterian church, ordained Margaret Towner as its first woman Minister of the Word and Sacrament. In the first decades of the twentieth century, the PCUSA had changed its Book of Order to let women be ordained as elders.
