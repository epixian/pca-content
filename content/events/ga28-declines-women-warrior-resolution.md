A personal resolution from a ruling elder asked the 28th General Assembly to advise the chaplains' commission (PRJC) that PCA chaplains had denominational license to counsel women against enlisting or remaining in combat roles. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures, the Assembly answered the resolution in the negative. The grounds said that action would be premature before the study committee had finished its work.
