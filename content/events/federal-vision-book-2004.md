TE Steve Wilkins and Duane Garner edited a collection of essays by the movement's proponents, The Federal Vision (Monroe: Athanasius Press, 2004). The General Assembly's study committee used the book's title for the movement: the name "Federal Vision" "appears to be the name preferred by proponents, who together authored a book entitled The Federal Vision."

Critics and proponents had already debated the views at the Knox Theological Seminary Colloquium on the Federal Vision (August 11–13, 2003), published in 2004 as The Auburn Avenue Theology, Pros & Cons.
