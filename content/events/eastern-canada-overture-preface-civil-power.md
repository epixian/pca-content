Eastern Canada Presbytery's Overture 22 to the 17th General Assembly asked the Assembly not to enact the amendment to BCO Preface II(1) that the 16th General Assembly had sent down. The overture argued that the sentence "No religious constitution should be supported by the civil power" makes no distinction between Christian and non-Christian religious groups, and that this is contrary to WCF 23.3 on civil magistrates as "nursing fathers" of the church.
