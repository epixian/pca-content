Representatives of more than 260 PCUS congregations met in Atlanta at a Convocation of Sessions, called by thirty churches. The ruling and teaching elders voted 349 to 16 for a new denomination in 1973, adopted the Reaffirmations of 1973, and called an advisory convention to plan it. The Advisory Convention minutes say the churches "sent official representatives"; Christianity Today reported that the elders "voted as individuals, without power to bind their churches."
