St. Louis Presbytery sent Overture 37 to the 11th General Assembly. Its own committee had studied the 10th General Assembly's paper "Confessional Subscription" and found some differences with it. The overture favored the Hodge and Warfield approach of subscribing to the whole system of doctrine, not to every proposition of the Westminster Standards. It asked the Assembly to share the presbytery's conclusion with the other presbyteries. The Minutes do not print the date on which the presbytery adopted the overture.
