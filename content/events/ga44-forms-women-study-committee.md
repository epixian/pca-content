The 44th General Assembly adopted a recommendation of the Administrative Committee (767-375-12) to form a study committee on women serving in the ministry of the church. The recommendation asked the committee to give attention to ordination, the office of deacon, and the ordination or commissioning of deacons and deaconesses. The Assembly first defeated a substitute motion of the Committee of Commissioners to answer the recommendation in the negative.
