Illiana Presbytery adopted Overture 8 to the 28th General Assembly. It said that a presbytery of the Evangelical Presbyterian Church (EPC) had received a teaching elder whom Illiana had indefinitely suspended, while that censure was still in force. It asked the Assembly to take this into advisement in its present and future relations with the EPC.
