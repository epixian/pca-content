At its annual meeting, the Cooperative Ministries Committee heard a preliminary report from its subcommittee on the role of women. The subcommittee said it had discussed ways to give women a greater voice and more visible roles in PCA church life, and it noted that the OPC and the former RPCES had discussed proposals to recognize deaconesses. The CMC report to the 43rd General Assembly (2015) recorded this, and the CMC extended the subcommittee for another year.
