At its stated meeting, Missouri Presbytery partly sustained TE Ryan Speck's July 2019 complaint and voted to reconsider the nine theological judgments of May 2019. It referred the outside requests to investigate TE Greg Johnson to an existing committee (the CRM) and told it to begin a BCO 31-2 investigation. It also formed a committee to write affirmations and denials on human sexuality.
