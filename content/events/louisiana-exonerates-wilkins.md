In July 2005 Louisiana Presbytery adopted the report of its ad hoc committee on Federal Vision theology. The report recommended that TE Steve Wilkins, pastor of Auburn Avenue Presbyterian Church in Monroe, be "publicly exonerated" and declared faithful to the PCA's confessional standards. The Auburn Avenue session had adopted a Summary Statement on covenant, baptism and salvation on April 3, 2005. The Standing Judicial Commission later found that neither the committee nor the presbytery had met with TE Wilkins to examine his views.
