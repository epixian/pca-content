The Stated Clerk's lists of churches lost from the denomination record that Cedar Springs Presbyterian Church in Knoxville, of Tennessee Valley Presbytery, went to the Evangelical Presbyterian Church in 2001. The 29th General Assembly's list gives the date as April 8, 2001, and the 30th General Assembly's list gives July 10, 2001.
