Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 50 to the 12th General Assembly on April 14, 1984. It said that mandatory church participation in Social Security, in force from January 1, 1984, was a tax on the church. It asked the Assembly to petition the President and Congress to acknowledge Christ's Lordship over His Church and to repeal those laws.
