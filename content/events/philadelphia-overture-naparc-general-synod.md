Philadelphia Presbytery adopted Overture 8 to the 27th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to direct the Interchurch Relations Committee to explore a general assembly or national synod that would meet every few years with the NAPARC churches and other churches committed to the Westminster Standards or the Three Forms of Unity. The overture cited the proposal "A Reformed Dream".
