James River Presbytery sent Overture 24 to the 11th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to continue negotiations so that the Orthodox Presbyterian Church could also enter the PCA by joining and receiving. Warrior Presbytery's Overture 35 asked the Assembly to issue the invitation to the OPC again. The Minutes do not print the date on which either presbytery adopted its overture.
