A personal resolution from a teaching elder noted a bill in Congress to change the day of national elections from Tuesday to Sunday. The 9th General Assembly adopted it in amended form. The resolution opposed the change as a "usurpation of God's prerogatives by the civil magistrate". Its grounds cited WCF 21.7 and said that a Sunday election day would force many PCA members to violate their consciences. The Stated Clerk was directed to send the resolution to the President and Congress.
