After the 35th General Assembly's citation, Liberti Church submitted an overture
to Philadelphia Presbytery. It asked the presbytery to overture the General
Assembly for a change to the BCO. The change would allow women to be elected
deacons equal with men, with freedom to ordain both or to commission both.
