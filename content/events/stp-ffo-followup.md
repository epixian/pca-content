Save the PCA posted answers to questions about the report's methods. It defined a "functional female officer" as a named role in which a woman did work usually reserved for elders or deacons and went through some form of commissioning. It said that it left out roles named as assistants to the diaconate, and that not every entry in its published dataset was counted as a functional female officer.
