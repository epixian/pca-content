Overture 5 to the 30th General Assembly, from Ohio Valley Presbytery and nineteen other presbyteries, asked the Assembly to change "two other" in BCO 34-1 to "at least ten percent of all the Presbyteries". Under BCO 34-1, when a presbytery refuses to act against a minister, the request of other presbyteries brings the question of original jurisdiction to the Assembly; the overture said that the existing wording allowed only two presbyteries to do so. Overture 30, from Philadelphia, Iowa and Southern Florida Presbyteries, proposed the same change. The Minutes do not print adoption dates for these overtures.
