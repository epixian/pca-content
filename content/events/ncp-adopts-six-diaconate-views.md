After the Review of Presbytery Records exceptions on its churches' diaconal practices, Northern California Presbytery adopted its Procedural Committee's recommendations. It acknowledged tensions between these practices and the BCO but placed them within the breadth it had historically allowed. It voted on six views one by one, and then declared that ministers and sessions could hold and practice all six. The views ranged from deacons who are only ordained men (30-0), through women appointed or elected as deaconesses to assist the deacons (29-3 and 26-4), to commissioned deaconesses serving as equal partners with ordained deacons (21-9), and to a diaconal ministry of men and women with no one ordained (21-9).

The session of Grace Presbyterian Church, Pleasanton, complained against views 3 to 6, and TE David Brown complained against views 5 and 6. On October 9, 2009, the presbytery denied both complaints.
