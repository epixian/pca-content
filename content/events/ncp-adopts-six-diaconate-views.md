After the Review of Presbytery Records exceptions on its churches' diaconal practices, Northern California Presbytery adopted its Procedural Committee's recommendations. It acknowledged tensions between these practices and the BCO but placed them within the breadth it had historically allowed. It voted on six views one by one, and then declared that ministers and sessions could hold and practice all six:

1. Only men are ordained as deacons, and they do the diaconal work of the congregation (30-0).
2. Only men are ordained as deacons, and the session appoints men or women to assist them (30-0).
3. Only men are ordained as deacons, and the session appoints women as deaconesses to assist them (29-3).
4. Only men are ordained as deacons, and the congregation elects women, with the session's approval, as deaconesses to assist them (26-4).
5. Men are ordained as deacons and women are commissioned as deaconesses; the congregation elects both, and they serve as equal partners (21-9).
6. Men and women serve as equal partners in diaconal ministry, often called "deacon" or "deaconess," and no one is ordained (21-9).

The session of Grace Presbyterian Church, Pleasanton, complained against views 3 to 6, and TE David Brown complained against views 5 and 6. On October 9, 2009, the presbytery denied both complaints.
