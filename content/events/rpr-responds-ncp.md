The 36th General Assembly adopted the RPR recommendation that Northern California
Presbytery's response was unsatisfactory and that it respond again to the 37th
General Assembly. The rationale said that BCO 9-7 covers people the session
appoints, not members of a diaconate. It also said that the practice denied
qualified men their right to be considered for the office of deacon.
