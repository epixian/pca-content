The 47th General Assembly answered James River Presbytery's Overture 37 ("Assembly Statement on Same-sex Attraction") and Westminster Presbytery's Overture 28 ("Affirmations and Denials regarding Homosexuality") by reference to its vote on the Nashville Statement. Minority reports that would have adopted amended versions of the two overtures failed, 492-724 and 210-967. During the debate on Overture 28, the Moderator ruled that arguments about Revoice were in order because the overture named the conference.
