The 12th General Assembly heard Case 7, a complaint by the Session of Faith Presbyterian Church, Akron, Ohio, and others against Ascension Presbytery. The complaint said the presbytery had not carried out the 11th Assembly's directives in Case 2 and had tried to reopen that case by a "Reference" of May 5, 1984. The complainants said the women-deacon issue was being raised to retry the case. The commission sustained non-compliance 15-0-0 and did not sustain contumacy. The Assembly directed the presbytery to re-examine the candidate, Kurt Lutjens, by September 1984 and to appoint a new pastoral committee. Cases 4 and 5 against the same presbytery were decided by reference to Case 7.
