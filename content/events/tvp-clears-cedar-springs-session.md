In July 1999 Western Carolina, Calvary, and Ascension Presbyteries asked the General Assembly to assume original jurisdiction over the Cedar Springs pastor, TE John Wood, under BCO 34-1. At its fall stated meeting on October 12, 1999, Tennessee Valley Presbytery found that the Cedar Springs session was in conformity with the standards of the PCA and had not acted injuriously. It also declared that it is not proper for women to preach and teach in the church's corporate worship.
