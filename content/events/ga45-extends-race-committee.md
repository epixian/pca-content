Moderator George Robertson had appointed the committee, with TE Kevin M. Smith as convener. In its initial report the committee said that it had chosen LifeWay Research for a survey of the PCA "to obtain a quantitative baseline measure of where the Presbyterian Church in America stands on racial reconciliation." The 45th General Assembly approved a follow-up study in three years, and, on the Administrative Committee's recommendations, extended the committee for another year and added $50,000 to the 2017 budget for the research, to be funded by designated gifts.
