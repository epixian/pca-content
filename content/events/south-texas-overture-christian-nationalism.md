South Texas Presbytery adopted an overture to the 52nd General Assembly for a study committee on Christian nationalism. It stated that Christian nationalism "has been promulgated theologically by Moscow Theology and Postmillennial Theonomic Reconstructionism," and that many members met these teachings "from various online sources."
