On the report of its Committee on Christian Education and Publications, the 7th General Assembly affirmed WCF 19 and WLC 93-150 as "a broad but adequate definition of theonomy." It declined further study at the Assembly level. As printed in its minutes, it also affirmed that "no particular view of the application of the judicial law for today should be made a basis for orthodoxy or excluded as heresy."
