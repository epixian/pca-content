A personal resolution (Personal Resolution 4) asked the 24th General Assembly to consider a statement to the Episcopal Church. A court of the Episcopal Church had decided on May 15, 1996, that no core doctrine prohibited the ordination of a noncelibate homosexual person in a committed relationship. The resolution enclosed, as a model, a letter that the Presbytery of New Jersey had sent to the Presiding Bishop. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations, the Assembly answered the resolution in the negative. Its first ground was that the PCA had no ecclesiastical relationship with the Episcopal Church and therefore no basis to address it. The Assembly also encouraged its members to pray for and support those within the Episcopal Church who opposed the decision.
