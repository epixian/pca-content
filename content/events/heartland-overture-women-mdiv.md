Heartland Presbytery overtured the 18th General Assembly not to support seminaries that admit women to the Master of Divinity course. It also asked the Assembly to direct the Covenant Seminary trustees to prohibit that practice. The overture said that the M.Div. often meets part of the requirements for ordination as a teaching elder, and that the PCA does not ordain women.
