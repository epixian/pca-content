The Overtures Committee of the 36th General Assembly recommended that Overture 9
be answered in the negative. It reminded presbyteries that they could bring such
issues to the Assembly by overtures to amend the BCO or by reference. It
recommended that Overtures 15, 17, and 19 and Communication 2 be answered with
reference to that action.
