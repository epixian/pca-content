A group of Southern Presbyterians opened Reformed Theological Seminary in Jackson, Mississippi, in the fall of 1966, independent of the PCUS. Its board said the seminary was committed to "the verbally inspired, infallible Bible and to the Westminster Confession of Faith." Christianity Today's issue of September 2, 1966, said the opening convocation would be held the next week.
