In The Aquila Report, TE Chris Hutchinson, a member of the 2015 Overtures Committee, wrote that the referral showed that "Presbyterianism worked." He described the committee's debate as one between those who wanted to repent that year and those who wanted to act "more carefully, thoroughly and through the presbyteries." He asked how the church could "add feet to our repentance" while it kept its "purely spiritual mission and authority."
