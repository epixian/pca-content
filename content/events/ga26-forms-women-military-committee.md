The Bills and Overtures Committee recommended that Overture 26 from Philadelphia Presbytery be answered in the negative, but the 26th General Assembly adopted a substitute that answered it in the affirmative and formed a privately funded committee of seven elders, later named the Ad Interim Study Committee on Women in the Military. Its task was to consider the theological and ethical issues raised by women in combat roles or military service. The committee reported in 1999 and 2000, and both Assemblies gave it more time.
