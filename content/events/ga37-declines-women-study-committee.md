The Overtures Committee recommended that the 37th General Assembly answer Susquehanna Valley's Overture 10 in the negative, and that it answer James River's Overture 5 by reference. Its grounds said that the role of women in the church "is not a new or unstudied issue." A minority report proposed a pastoral letter instead, with up to two advisory members who could be women. The Assembly defeated the minority report as a substitute by 427-446 and adopted the committee's recommendation.
