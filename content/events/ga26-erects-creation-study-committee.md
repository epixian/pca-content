The Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures recommended that Overtures 5 and 18 be answered in the negative. The 26th General Assembly instead adopted a substitute, 521-371. It answered Central Carolina Presbytery's Overture 5 in the affirmative and erected a committee to study the interpretations of Genesis 1-3 and the Westminster Standards' phrase "in the space of six days." It answered Westminster Presbytery's Overture 18 by reference to that action.
