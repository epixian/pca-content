The Synod of the Christian Reformed Church (CRC) recognized two views of women in office as both honoring Scripture. It decided that a classis may declare the word "male" in Church Order Article 3-a inoperative and authorize its churches to ordain women as elders, ministers and evangelists. In a classis that did not make this declaration, a church could still take exception and ordain women elders for local service. Regulations kept women officebearers from synod, and the arrangement was to be reviewed in 2000.
