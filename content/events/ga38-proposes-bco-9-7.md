The 38th General Assembly answered Evangel's Overture 7 in the affirmative as
amended, proposing that assistants to the deacons "are not officers of the church"
and "are not subjects for ordination." It answered Overtures 2, 9, 13, and 16 by
reference to that action and Northern California's Overture 10 in the negative.
The grounds said the committee would neither create nor forbid terms in use, such
as "deaconess," as long as none is taken to mean a church office.
