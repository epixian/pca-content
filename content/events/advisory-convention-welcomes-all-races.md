The Advisory Convention was not a denomination. It was a meeting of congregations leaving the Presbyterian Church in the United States (PCUS, the Southern church), called to plan the structures of a new denomination. "Continuing Presbyterian Church" was the working name of that movement until the first General Assembly gave the church a permanent name.

On the third day of the convention, four months before the first General Assembly, the convention adopted item 47, "All Races Welcome": the movement would "welcome fellow believers in Christ regardless of race." The 2018 report of the Ad Interim Committee on Racial and Ethnic Reconciliation cited this resolution as evidence of concern about race before the PCA was founded.
