On the third day of the [Advisory Convention of the Continuing Presbyterian Church](/actors/advisory-convention/), four months before the first General Assembly, the convention adopted item 47, "All Races Welcome": the movement would "welcome fellow believers in Christ regardless of race." The 2018 report of the Ad Interim Committee on Racial and Ethnic Reconciliation cited this resolution as evidence of concern about race before the PCA was founded.
