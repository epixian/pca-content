Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 7, "Concerning the Duties Required by the
Second Commandment," for the 54th General Assembly. It asks the Assembly to
recognize publicly that civil officials, especially state governors and local
officials, have a duty under Larger Catechism 108 to oppose all false worship and
to remove it and its monuments within their jurisdictions.

The overture contrasts nations shaped by Christianity with nations shaped by Islam.
It says that people of other religions must be treated with kindness, justice and
mercy, and that private worship in the home is outside the magistrate's reach. It
reads the call in Confession 23.3 to protect religious assemblies as applying only
to Christian assemblies, not to the public worship of other religions.
