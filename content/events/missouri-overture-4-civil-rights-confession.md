Missouri Presbytery adopted Overture 4 to the 44th General Assembly, "Confession of Sin and Commitment to Racial and Ethnic Diversity." It asked the Assembly to "recognize and confess our church’s covenantal and generational involvement in and complicity with racial injustice inside and outside of our churches during the Civil Rights period". Many presbyteries sent the same text or a close version, and several sent overtures "In Support of" Overture 4. Some versions added to the text: Heritage Presbytery's Overture 18, for example, rejected any teaching that a race bears "the curse of Cain". The 44th General Assembly answered these overtures by reference to its action on Potomac's Overture 43.
