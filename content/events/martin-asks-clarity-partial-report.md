In The Aquila Report, Isaac Martin asked whether the 1788 revisions were "a theological correction" or "a contextual adjustment," and wrote that the report "appears to jump to conclusions on one side." He also asked how its denial of "preferential treatment of one’s own ethnic group" fits its affirmations of kinship and peoplehood.
