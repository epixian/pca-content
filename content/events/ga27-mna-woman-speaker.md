A personal resolution asked the 27th General Assembly to have MNA investigate a report that a woman had exhorted church planters and applied Scripture as a featured speaker at an MNA conference in February 1999. On the recommendation of the Committee of Commissioners on MNA, the Assembly found that MNA staff had not violated the 25th Assembly's grounds on women's leadership in MNA seminars, and some commissioners recorded negative votes. It told MNA to study women speaking and teaching in public and to bring guidelines to the 28th Assembly; a recommendation for an Assembly position paper on women's public ministry was ruled out of order.
