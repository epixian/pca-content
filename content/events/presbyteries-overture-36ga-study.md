Philadelphia Presbytery adopted Overture 9 on January 26, 2008, and reaffirmed it
on February 10. It asked the 36th General Assembly for an ad interim committee to
study women in diaconal ministry, and its preamble cited the Crossroads complaint,
the RPR exception for Liberti Church, and Liberti's overture. Northern California
Presbytery concurred with Overture 9 on March 7, 2008; Western Canada Presbytery
(Overture 15, March 8) asked for a similar committee; and Rocky Mountain
Presbytery (Overture 17, April 24) asked that any such committee's scope be
expanded.
