During the report of the Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations, the 28th General Assembly adopted a motion that authorized the Interchurch Relations Committee to move the expulsion of the CRC from NAPARC if the CRC did not rescind its position on the ordination of women. The IRC's report had noted that the CRC would reconsider that position at its 2000 Synod.
