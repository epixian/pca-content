Revoice, an organization outside the PCA, held its first conference at Memorial Presbyterian Church (PCA) in St. Louis on July 26–28, 2018. Its stated mission was to support "gay, lesbian, same-sex attracted, and other gender and sexual minority Christians" while they keep "the historic, Christian doctrine of marriage and sexuality." TE Greg Johnson, Memorial's pastor, taught one breakout session.
