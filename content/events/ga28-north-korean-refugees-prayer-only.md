The 28th General Assembly answered Korean Southeastern Presbytery's Overture 25 in the affirmative as amended. The amended answer kept the time of prayer at the Assembly, the prayer sign-up sheet, and the letter to the churches. It did not include the overture's requests for official letters to the United Nations and to the President of the United States. The Assembly also let a ruling elder from Seoul address it for up to 10 minutes on the matter.
