At its annual meeting, the North American Presbyterian and Reformed Council (NAPARC) voted 6 to 1 to begin the process of suspending the CRC. Only the CRC voted against. The PCA's interchurch relations chairman said that the CRC's 1995 decision on women's ordination had led the PCA to propose the suspension. The motion then went to the member churches for ratification.
