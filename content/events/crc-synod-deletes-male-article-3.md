CRC Synod 2007 adopted the change that Synod 2006 had proposed and deleted the word "male" from Church Order Article 3-a. Congregations may, but are not required to, allow women to serve as pastor, elder or ministry associate. A classis may decide that women officebearers will not be delegated to classis.
