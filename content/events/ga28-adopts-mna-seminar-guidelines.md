The 28th General Assembly adopted the MNA Committee's response to the 27th Assembly's request for guidelines on women speaking and teaching in public. The response said that the preaching of the Word in MNA worship services is done by elders, but that seminars are not worship and that MNA may ask godly men and women who are not elders to give presentations. The Assembly defeated an amendment that would have barred MNA from using women as keynote speakers or expositors of the Bible in seminars except for audiences mainly of women.
