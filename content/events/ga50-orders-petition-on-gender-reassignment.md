Evangel Presbytery's Overture 12 asked the 50th General Assembly to petition the government to end sex-change procedures for minors. The Overtures Committee (72-50-1) proposed that the Moderator appoint a commission to draft a "humble petition (WCF 31-4)," to be sent by the Stated Clerk to the President, congressional leaders and the Chief Justice, and by presbytery clerks to state officials, condemning surgical and medical gender reassignment, especially of minors. The Assembly adopted the recommendation (1089-793-28), and a motion to reconsider failed (655-962-23).
