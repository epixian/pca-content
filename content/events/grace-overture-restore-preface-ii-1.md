Grace Presbytery's Overture 8 to the 16th General Assembly asked to restore the second and third sentences of BCO Preface II(1). Those sentences had dropped out when the 13th and 14th General Assemblies amended the principle. The overture said that Overture 30 to the 12th General Assembly had asked to change only the first sentence, and that a clerical error at the 13th General Assembly omitted the words "first sentence" from the committee's report.
