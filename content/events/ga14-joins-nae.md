On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Interchurch Relations, the 14th General Assembly voted 352-274 that the PCA enter into full membership in the National Association of Evangelicals. It answered Westminster Presbytery's Overture 48 by reference to this action. Commissioners recorded negative votes, and a protest signed by a group of commissioners was entered in the Minutes. The protest said that the Assembly had refused to wait for a study of principles for such an association, and that it had bound members who did not wish to be associated with the NAE.
