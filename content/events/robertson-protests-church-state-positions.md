TE O. Palmer Robertson of Delmarva Presbytery filed a protest against the summary positions adopted by the 16th General Assembly. He objected to their treatment of resisting or overthrowing governments, church taxation and civil disobedience, and he wrote that "the politicizing of evangelical churches may be the greatest single cause" of a possible loss of liberties.
