Western Carolina Presbytery's Overture 16 and Mississippi Valley Presbytery's Overture 8 asked the 31st General Assembly to erect an ad interim committee on doctrinal subscription. Overture 8 asked if exceptions may be taught and what the non-negotiables are. The Assembly adopted a minority report, 530-477, that answered Overture 16 in the negative. Its grounds advised living with good-faith subscription for a few years.
