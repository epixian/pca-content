Georgia Foothills Presbytery adopted Overture 6, "Child Protection in the PCA." It asked the General Assembly to exhort church leaders to prevent child sexual abuse by screening and training staff and volunteers and by keeping child protection policies. It also asked the Assembly to remind churches that child sexual abuse must be reported to the civil authorities, and to direct the permanent committees and agencies to review their child protection policies and report to the 43rd General Assembly. Many presbyteries sent overtures that endorsed it, among them Metro Atlanta (Overture 7), Southwest Florida (12), Ohio (18), Nashville (19), and others up to Blue Ridge (51).
