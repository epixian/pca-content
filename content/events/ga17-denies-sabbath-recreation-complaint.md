A member complained that the session of Immanuel Presbyterian Church, Miami, broke WCF 21.8 by holding games for adults and children on the church playground on a Sunday evening in 1987. Southern Florida Presbytery found no violation. The judicial commission of the 17th General Assembly did not sustain the complaint (13-6 and 14-6) and confirmed the presbytery's decision. It also encouraged all sessions to consider carefully the activities their churches sponsor on the Lord's Day. A minority of the commission dissented, and two protests were recorded, which said that recreations are forbidden on the Sabbath by WCF 21.8.
