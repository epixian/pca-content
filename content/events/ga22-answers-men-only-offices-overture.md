Southeast Alabama Presbytery overtured the 22nd General Assembly to reaffirm that the offices of elder and deacon are open to men only. The Assembly answered that the Constitutional Standards were a sufficient testimony (BCO 7-2, BCO 8-1, BCO 8-2, BCO 9-3). It also urged sessions and presbyteries to consider the profit of overtures that ask for reaffirmation of doctrines already in the Standards.
