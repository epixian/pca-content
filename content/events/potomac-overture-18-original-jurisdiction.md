Potomac Presbytery's Overture 18 to the 30th General Assembly proposed to amend both BCO 33-1 and BCO 34-1. A presbytery would assume original jurisdiction over a church member only at the request of at least five percent, but not less than two, of its sessions, and the Assembly would assume it over a minister only at the request of at least five percent of all presbyteries. The Minutes do not print an adoption date for this overture.
