Grace Presbytery adopted Overture 13, "Adopt Danvers Statement on Gender Issues." It asked the 37th General Assembly to adopt the Danvers Statement of the Council on Biblical Manhood and Womanhood, with changes, as the PCA's statement on gender issues. The overture noted that the 36th General Assembly had not formed a study committee on women deacons. One change replaced the Statement's words on "some governing and teaching roles" with a reference to the offices of elder and deacon.
