The 49th General Assembly answered Ascension Presbytery's Overture 13 in the affirmative as amended. In the printed minutes, the Overtures Committee's amendment strikes the presbytery's "humble petition" text and directs the Stated Clerk to send the President, the leaders of Congress, and the governors and legislative leaders of the 50 states the 1978 statement that begins, "God declares in Sacred Scripture that civil government, no less than the Church, is a divine institution."
