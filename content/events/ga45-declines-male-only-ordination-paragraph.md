Northwest Georgia Presbytery's Overture 4 asked the 45th General Assembly to add a BCO 24-11 that would call ordination of elders and deacons "limited only to males" an essential part of PCA ecclesiology. It said that it offered the overture as one way to address possible proposals for women's ordination. The Committee on Constitutional Business advised that the overture conflicted with the Constitution. The Assembly answered it in the negative on the Overtures Committee's ground that the BCO was already clear on the matter.
