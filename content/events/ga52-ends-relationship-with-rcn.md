The Interchurch Relations Committee reported that the Reformed Churches in the Netherlands had changed their position on homosexuality to one "out of accord with the PCA's position." On its recommendation, which the committee of commissioners approved 42-0-2, the 52nd General Assembly dissolved the PCA's corresponding relationship with that church by voice vote.
