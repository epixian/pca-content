The Committee of Commissioners on Bills and Overtures recommended that the 21st General Assembly answer Rocky Mountain Presbytery's Overture 40 in the negative. One ground cited WCF 31.4: the Assembly's actions in civil affairs "should generally be restricted to humble petitions." The Assembly instead adopted a substitute motion to answer the overture in the affirmative. One commissioner recorded a negative vote.
