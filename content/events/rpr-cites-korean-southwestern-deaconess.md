On the report of its Committee on Review of Presbytery Records, the 23rd General Assembly took an exception of substance to the October 13, 1992 minutes of Korean Southwestern Presbytery. The exception said that "deaconess" is not an office recognized in the PCA (BCO 9-7). In 1996 the 24th General Assembly noted that the presbytery had not responded, and it carried the exception to the 25th General Assembly.
