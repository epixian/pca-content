The 30th General Assembly answered Nashville Presbytery's Overture 20, "Racial Reconciliation," by adopting a statement of its own. The statement said that "unbiblical forms of servitude—including oppression, racism, exploitation, manstealing, and chattel slavery—stand in opposition to the Gospel." It confessed "our involvement in these sins," publicly repented "of our pride, our complacency, and our complicity," and pledged to strive for racial reconciliation and for urban and minority congregations. Four teaching elders recorded negative votes. The Assembly answered a personal resolution on the same subject by reference to this action.
