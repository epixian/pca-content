The Standing Judicial Commission answered Suncoast Florida's Overture 22 as "out of accord with the BCO." It said that the Assembly "could not by fiat declare a Presbytery heterodox"; a court can be tried only with proper process. BCO 34-1 original jurisdiction over TE Wilkins required that his presbytery refuse to act, and Louisiana Presbytery had acted by investigating and deciding not to indict. The commission noted that it was already dealing with the matter through Central Carolina's memorial. The answer appears in the commission's report to the 36th General Assembly. The minutes do not give its date. Its statement that no elder had filed a complaint suggests a date before TE James Jones complained in February 2007.
