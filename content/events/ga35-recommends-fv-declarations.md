On June 13, 2007, the 35th General Assembly defeated a motion to postpone and adopted all five of the committee's recommendations. It commended the report to elders and congregations "for careful consideration and study." It recommended the nine declarations "as a faithful exposition of the Westminster Standards" and reminded elders whose views differ to make their differences known to their courts. It also reminded sessions and presbyteries of their duty "to condemn erroneous opinions." The Assembly then dismissed the committee.
