The Standing Judicial Commission had voted on March 3, 2006, to cite Louisiana Presbytery under BCO 40-5. After it heard the presbytery's representative in October, it held that the presbytery had not shown that it dealt "with due diligence and great discretion" with the allegations against TE Steve Wilkins. Among other things, its committee kept no minutes and the presbytery never examined him as a court. The commission directed the presbytery to examine TE Wilkins on the memorial's concerns, to transcribe the examination, to say where his views or terms differ from the Standards, and to report by February 16, 2007. It adopted the decision on October 20, 2006, with 17 concurring.
