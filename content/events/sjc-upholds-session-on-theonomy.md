In Case 91-5 the session of Community Presbyterian Church, Live Oak, Florida, had declined two ruling elder candidates, resolving that "theonomy is out of accord" with WCF 19.4 and divisive. Central Florida Presbytery annulled that clause "based on insufficient clarity," and the candidates complained to the General Assembly. The full Standing Judicial Commission denied the complaint on March 6, 1992. It held that a court may judge the acceptability of "theological issues that have not been clearly defined by the broader Church," and it wrote that prior Assemblies "have not spoken clearly on theonomy." The 20th General Assembly approved the judgment, and seven commissioners recorded negative votes.
