The 46th General Assembly adopted without objection the recommendations of its Ad Interim Committee on Racial and Ethnic Reconciliation, which asked the Assembly to receive the report and distribute it. Among its guiding principles the report stated, "We reject as aberrant and anti-biblical any theological formulations incorporating racism or racial superiority (e.g., Kinism)."
