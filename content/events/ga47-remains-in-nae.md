Central Carolina Presbytery's Overture 23 asked the PCA to withdraw from the National Association of Evangelicals. It argued that the label "evangelical" had come to designate political distinctives and that the NAE "has frequently intermeddled in civil affairs," contrary to WCF 31 and BCO 3-3. The Interchurch Relations Committee answered that there are "differences among us on the present application of the doctrine of ‘the Spirituality of the Church.’" The Assembly defeated the Committee of Commissioners' substitute to withdraw (590-845) and adopted the negative answer by voice vote.
