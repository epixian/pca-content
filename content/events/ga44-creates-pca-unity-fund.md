After it acted on Overtures 43 and 45, the 44th General Assembly took up the Mission to North America committee's recommendation on Potomac Presbytery's Overture 44 and approved the overture as amended (728-149-36). It created a "PCA Unity Fund" to help raise up "future generations of godly, reformed African American and other minority Ruling and Teaching Elders," to be managed by Mission to North America.
