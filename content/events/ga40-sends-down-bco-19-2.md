Western Carolina Presbytery (Overture 1) and Northern New England Presbytery (Overture 2) asked the 40th General Assembly to amend BCO 19-2 so that candidates for licensure must state their differences with the standards, as candidates for ordination do. The Assembly answered the overtures in the affirmative and sent the amendment to the presbyteries. The grounds said that the overture "makes consistent the treatment of “exceptions” or “scruples”".
