In Case 2016-16, Sartorius v. Siouxlands, the Standing Judicial Commission denied the complaint against the second acquittal. The roll call shows one dissent (Fowler) and no other negative vote. It held that the complainant had not shown error from the record, and that it had "not commented on" TE Greg Lawrence's actual views. A concurring opinion noted that the matter had been before the commission for over nine years in three cases.
