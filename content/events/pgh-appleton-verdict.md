After an all-day trial on the weekend of July 18-19, 2026, Pittsburgh Presbytery found Appleton guilty on both charges and deposed him, according to posts on X and two blogs. One X post gave the vote on the first charge as 41-0 with one abstention. As of July 24, a blog said that the full verdict had not been released.
