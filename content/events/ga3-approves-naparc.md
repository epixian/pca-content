The 3rd General Assembly approved the formation of the North American Presbyterian and Reformed Council (NAPARC). It accepted the proposed constitution and by-laws and let the Committee on Interchurch Relations help set up the Council and appoint four delegates. The Assembly also changed the name of its relation with other churches from "Churches in Fraternal Relation" to "Churches in Ecclesiastical Fellowship."
