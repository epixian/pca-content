Eastern Carolina Presbytery licensed a candidate 12-11 and forbade him to teach his views on creation and the flood. The Antioch session complained about his divorce and his views on creation, the age of the earth and a local flood. The SJC ruled 13-4 that the presbytery did not err. It said that his views were not theistic evolution, and that it was not its task to decide which formulation of creation or the flood the church should hold. The 22nd General Assembly approved the judgment.
