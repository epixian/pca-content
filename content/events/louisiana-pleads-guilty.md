The indictment of January 2, 2008, had two specifications: that Louisiana Presbytery failed to address and record TE Steve Wilkins' differences with the Standards, and that it failed to find a strong presumption of guilt about some of his views. On January 19, 2008, the presbytery voted 9-6, with 4 abstentions, to plead not guilty to the first specification, to plead guilty to the second, and to "reference the matter to the SJC."
