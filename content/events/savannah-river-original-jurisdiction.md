Savannah River Presbytery adopted Overture 4 to the 48th General Assembly. It concurred with Central Georgia's Overture 2 and asked the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction of the case investigated by Missouri Presbytery. BCO 34-1 requires such a request from two presbyteries.
