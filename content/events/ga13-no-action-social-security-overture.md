Central Georgia Presbytery's Overture 35 said that the federal government was "usurping the authority of the Church" by requiring Social Security taxes for church employees. It asked the 13th General Assembly to declare the church immune from that tax by filing IRS Form 8274. On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Administration, the Assembly took no action because the filing deadline had passed. Five commissioners recorded negative votes.
