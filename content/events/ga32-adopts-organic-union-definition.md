The Interchurch Relations Committee reported to the 32nd General Assembly that all but one of the NAPARC churches had said they were not prepared to discuss organic union with the PCA at that time, because their representatives had no authority from their churches for such discussions. On the committee's recommendation, the Assembly adopted the NAPARC definition of organic union, which would require the eventual integration of church courts and administrative and legal structures.
