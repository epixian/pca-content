The 23rd General Assembly answered Western Carolina Presbytery's Overture 17 in the negative. It said an ad interim committee could not "adequately address or solve" the theological differences in the PCA. It answered two similar overtures by reference to that action. These were Overture 20, from a session in Oak Ridge, TN, and Overture 22, from a teaching elder. It received Covenant Presbytery's Overture 13, a response to a proposed "Statement of Identity for the PCA," as information. Overture 22 named the call for strict subscription and a proposed "Statement of Identity" as signs of unrest.
