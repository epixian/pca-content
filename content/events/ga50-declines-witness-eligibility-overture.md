Northern California Presbytery's Overture 13 asked the 50th General Assembly to amend BCO 35-1 and 35-7 so that persons who do not believe in God or a future state of rewards and punishments would no longer be barred as witnesses. It said that the Ad Interim Committee on Domestic Abuse and Sexual Assault had recommended the change, to allow testimony from persons such as medical professionals, especially in cases of abuse. The Overtures Committee recommended a negative answer. A minority report, presented by TE Timothy LeCroy, said the change mattered for the protection of children and victims of abuse; the motion to substitute it failed, 871-999-30. The Assembly answered Overture 13 in the negative, 1091-751-41, and answered Tennessee Valley's Overture 2021-41 with reference to it.
