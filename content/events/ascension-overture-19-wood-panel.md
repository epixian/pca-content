Ascension Presbytery adopted Overture 19 to the 29th General Assembly. The 28th Assembly had directed that representatives of the petitioning presbyteries be permitted to represent their allegations before the Standing Judicial Commission. The overture said that the SJC's investigative panel did not allow Ascension's representatives to speak, and it asked the Committee on Constitutional Business to examine the SJC minutes on this point.
