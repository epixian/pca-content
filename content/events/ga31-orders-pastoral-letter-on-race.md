Nashville Presbytery's Overture 17 said that the 2002 statement "has exposed some divisions within the PCA regarding the issue of racism" and asked for an ad interim committee to write a pastoral letter. The 31st General Assembly answered it in the affirmative as amended: it assigned the drafting of the letter to Mission to North America, not to a new committee. A minority report, signed by eight commissioners, asked for a negative answer; among its grounds it said that one can disagree for reasons other than racism, for example "a desire to safeguard the spirituality of the church." The Assembly defeated the minority report.
