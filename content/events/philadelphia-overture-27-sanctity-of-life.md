Philadelphia Presbytery adopted Overture 27, "Reaffirm the Sanctity of Life," for the 27th General Assembly. It asked the Assembly to send the 6th General Assembly's 1978 report on abortion to every church and pastor added since 1980, and to send current officials a communication in the language of that report's recommendation 8, which had called on the President, Congress and the Chief Justice to renounce the sin of abortion. It asked that both actions be repeated for new churches, pastors and administrations until legalized abortion ended. It also asked for sanctity of life committees, a subcommittee under Christian Education and Publications, and a day of prayer and fasting in 2000.
