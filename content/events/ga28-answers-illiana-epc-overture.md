In answer to Illiana Presbytery's Overture 8, the 28th General Assembly said that the EPC, although it may have acted within its own constitution, had disregarded biblical judicial process when it received a teaching elder under Illiana's discipline. It asked the EPC to honor the judicial process and censures of PCA courts. It directed the Stated Clerk and the chairman of the Interchurch Relations Committee to meet with their EPC counterparts on how to respect each other's judicial processes, and to address other Reformed bodies that disregard PCA discipline. The committee reported to the 29th Assembly that the meeting with the EPC took place in December 2000.
