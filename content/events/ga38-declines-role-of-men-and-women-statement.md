Northwest Georgia Presbytery adopted Overture 25, "The Role of Men and Women to Office in the Church," on May 22, 2010. It asked the 38th General Assembly to declare that "by God’s design only men are called to ordained roles in Scripture". The Assembly answered it in the negative. The grounds said that such a statement "cannot achieve the ends desired", and they referred to an earlier deliverance of the 22nd General Assembly.
