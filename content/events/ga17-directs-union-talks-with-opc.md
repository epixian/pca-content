The 17th General Assembly defeated a recommendation to adopt the "Proposed Guiding Principles for Ecumenical Relations," which the 16th General Assembly had sent to presbyteries and sessions for comment. It directed its Committee on Interchurch Relations to continue face-to-face discussions with the Orthodox Presbyterian Church's Committee on Ecumenicity about "the possibility of movement toward union," with attention to the proposed principles.
