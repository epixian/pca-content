At trial, Rio Grande Presbytery found Garris not guilty on the first charge (posts about slavery) and guilty on the second ("unwholesome speech" toward a professor in 2023), and suspended him indefinitely, according to The Tennessean and to Christian Post and Roys Report accounts that cite Sean McGowan. The presbytery did not answer requests for comment, and Garris declined to comment "due to pending litigation." As of June 9, 2026, the Roys Report said that he was expected to appeal to the Standing Judicial Commission.
