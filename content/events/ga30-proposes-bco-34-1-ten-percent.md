The 30th General Assembly answered Overture 5 in the affirmative. It sent down to the presbyteries an amendment to BCO 34-1 that would require at least ten percent of all presbyteries, instead of two, to bring a request to assume original jurisdiction over a minister. It answered Potomac Presbytery's Overture 18 and Overture 30 by reference to its answer to Overture 5.
