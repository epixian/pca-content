On the recommendation of its Committee of Commissioners on Judicial Business, the 13th General Assembly answered Overture 30 to the 12th General Assembly, from the Session of the Stony Point Reformed Presbyterian Church, Richmond, Virginia, by amending the first preliminary principle in the Preface of the BCO (Preface II(1)) and sending the amendment down to the presbyteries.

The overture had asked to change only the first sentence. The amendment as adopted replaced the whole principle, so the second and third sentences dropped out: "the rights of private judgment in all matters that respect religion are universal and inalienable. No religious constitution should be supported by the civil power, further than may be necessary for protection and security equal and common to all others." The 14th General Assembly enacted the amendment. Grace Presbytery later traced the omission to a clerical error.
