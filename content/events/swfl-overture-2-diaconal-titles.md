Southwest Florida Presbytery adopted Overture 2 to the 54th General Assembly, "Amend BCO 9-2 & 9-7 to Restrict Diaconal Titles and Authority to Ordained Officers." It proposed that assistants to the deacons not be called deacons or deaconesses, and that a church without ordained deacons not appoint such assistants. Its rationale said that some churches installed women into diaconal roles that blur the distinction from ordained deacons, and it cited savethepca.com as one public effort to document such practices.
