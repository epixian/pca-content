On the recommendation of the Standing Judicial Commission, the 29th General Assembly amended RAO 15-2 so that a request to the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction under BCO 34-1 is assigned to the Commission. The Commission also proposed new sections of its Manual, including procedures for assuming original jurisdiction over a minister. The Assembly postponed these Manual sections to the 30th General Assembly so that presbyteries and individuals could send suggestions and concerns.
