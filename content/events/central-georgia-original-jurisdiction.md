Central Georgia Presbytery adopted Overture 2 to the 48th General Assembly. Under BCO 34-1, it asked the Assembly to assume original jurisdiction of Missouri Presbytery's investigation of TE Greg Johnson and the Memorial session "with regard to theological error and involvement in the 2018 Revoice Conference." The Stated Clerk referred the overture to the Standing Judicial Commission.
