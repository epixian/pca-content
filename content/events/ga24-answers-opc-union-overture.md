The 24th General Assembly answered Northeast Presbytery's Overture 5 by a statement. The statement noted that the PCA had said many times since 1979 that joining and receiving was the best means of union with other Reformed churches, and that its Interchurch Relations Committee remained available if the Orthodox Presbyterian Church wished to start a discussion. It also encouraged local churches and presbyteries to cooperate with other Reformed churches in joint worship, fellowship and witness.
