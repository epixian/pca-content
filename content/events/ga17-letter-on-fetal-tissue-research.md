Tennessee Valley Presbytery's Overture 23 asked the 17th General Assembly to tell the executive and legislative branches of the federal government that research using fetal tissue from elective abortions should not be allowed. The adoption line gives no date.

The Assembly answered the overture in the affirmative and adopted a letter to the U.S. executive and legislative branches. The letter said that it is not the church's task to govern the country, but that it is the church's responsibility to warn the government of moral wrongdoing. The first and last paragraphs went also to the Canadian federal government.
