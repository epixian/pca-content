Heartland Presbytery adopted Overture 1 to the 44th General Assembly, the first of the overtures on racial reconciliation sent to that Assembly. It asked the Assembly to exhort all members, especially officers, to examine themselves for "unconfessed and unrepentant sins of partiality, favoritism, or prejudice", and it listed specific sins such as barring people from worship and membership because of their ethnicity. It cited the 30th General Assembly's statement, the paper "The Gospel and Race", and a statement of the RPCES General Synod. The 44th General Assembly answered it by reference to its action on Potomac's Overture 43.
