The 18th General Assembly adopted, as amended, the "Guiding Principles for Ecumenical Relations," written in consultation with the Orthodox Presbyterian Church. The principles said that denominations are obligated by Scripture to seek the removal of barriers to unity. In answer to the six overtures on talks with the OPC, the Assembly told the OPC that the best way for it to seek union was to take its constitutional steps to ask to be received into the PCA. It kept its Interchurch Relations Committee available to discuss the joining-and-receiving process.
