Northeast Presbytery adopted Overture 15 to the 15th General Assembly. It said that membership in the National Association of Evangelicals reduced the Reformed faith to "broad evangelicalism," that the NAE spoke to political and moral issues through its Office of Public Affairs, and that the NAE was an extra-ecclesiastical organization. It asked the Assembly to rescind the 1986 action and withdraw from the NAE at once. Warrior Presbytery's Overture 18, adopted on January 20, 1987, made the same request.
