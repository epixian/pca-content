Oklahoma Presbytery adopted Overture 55 to the 12th General Assembly on April 14, 1984. It said the state had decreed new taxes on churches, such as F.I.C.A. for church employees. It asked the Assembly to study church/state relations on taxation and to make recommendations to congregations, church courts and individuals.
