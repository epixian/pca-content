Westminster Presbytery adopted Overture 11 to the 2nd General Assembly at its July 20, 1974 meeting. It asked the Assembly to encourage the Mission to the World Committee to be "carefully Biblical" in its use of women on the mission field, mainly in evangelism, preaching and church development. The 2nd General Assembly carried the overture over, and the 3rd General Assembly answered it.
