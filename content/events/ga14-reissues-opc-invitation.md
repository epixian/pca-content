The 50th General Assembly of the Orthodox Presbyterian Church had rejected joining and receiving into the PCA. Its vote had been 78-68. The 14th General Assembly of the PCA reissued its invitation to the OPC to join and be received, on the basis of the joint statement adopted by the 11th General Assembly. It directed the Stated Clerk to give the OPC vote to the presbyteries, and it kept its Interchurch Relations Committee as the PCA's representative for further discussion.
