While Overture 26 on titles was before the 50th General Assembly, [RE Matthew Lee](/actors/matthew-lee/) and Liam Carr, a deacon at Covenant Presbyterian Church (PCA) in Fayetteville, Arkansas, published in The Aquila Report a study of church websites in a random sample of 14 PCA presbyteries, with OPC and RPCNA presbyteries for comparison. They counted only women whom a website explicitly called deaconesses, and found that about one in twenty-five sampled PCA churches listed deaconesses.
