The 22nd General Assembly adopted Personal Resolution 6, from TE Peter Lillback. The resolution quoted WCF 31 on synods meddling with civil affairs only "by way of humble petition in cases extraordinary." It declared the EEOC's proposed guidelines on "religious harassment" an extraordinary case and petitioned the EEOC to delete "religion" from the guidelines. Copies went to the President and the Speakers of both Houses of Congress.
