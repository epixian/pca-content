Ascension Presbytery adopted Overture 9 to the 37th General Assembly, to amend BCO 13-6. The amendment would require ordained ministers who come from other denominations to state the specific instances in which they differ with the Confession of Faith and Catechisms, so that the presbytery can judge them.
