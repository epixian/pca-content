The Minority Report was moved as a substitute for Recommendation 9. A point of
order that it contradicted the BCO was ruled not well taken, and the Assembly
sustained the ruling 518-369. After debate the substitute was defeated, and the
Assembly adopted the recommendations to answer Overture 9 in the negative and the
related overtures by reference.
