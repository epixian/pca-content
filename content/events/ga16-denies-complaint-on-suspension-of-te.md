In 1988 a teaching elder in Western Carolina Presbytery confessed to years of homosexual sin. The presbytery suspended him indefinitely from office and from the sacraments and placed him under a pastoral oversight committee. A ruling elder complained that the presbytery should have deposed him, and he cited the 5th General Assembly's 1977 statement. The 16th General Assembly heard the case in executive session and adopted its commission's judgment. The commission confirmed the presbytery's action under BCO 30-3, BCO 30-4 and BCO 34-7. The commission also advised the Assembly that the presbytery later deposed the teaching elder. The Minutes withhold his name.
