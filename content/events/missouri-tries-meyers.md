While Case 2011-06 was pending before the Standing Judicial Commission, Missouri Presbytery tried TE Jeffrey Meyers on April 13 and 14, 2012. The minutes in this graph do not print the verdict. A minority report of the Committee on Review of Presbytery Records at the 41st General Assembly faulted the presbytery for "failing to find TE Meyers guilty in all five areas in which he was charged," and said it had rushed to trial. The presbytery decided a complaint against its action on July 17, 2012 (later Case 2012-09).
