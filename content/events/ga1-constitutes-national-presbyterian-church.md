At the close of the 1st General Assembly, the moderator, W. Jack Williamson, declared the National Presbyterian Church constituted under its newly adopted constitution. Before this, the Assembly had adopted a resolution of thanks that named the boards of the Presbyterian Journal, Concerned Presbyterians, Presbyterian Churchmen United and the Presbyterian Evangelistic Fellowship for their leadership of the Steering Committee.
