The 21st General Assembly spread the memorial on its Minutes and adopted a response. It reaffirmed its Constitution and the 1973 "Message to All Churches." On subscription, it said that the vows were "formally sufficient" and that sessions and presbyteries, and then the higher courts, were the place to correct inadequate practice. It warned that general allegations against the courts, outside the complaint process, were damaging to the church.
