The presbyteries voted on the BCO 34-1 amendment that the 20th General Assembly had sent down. The amendment would have let the Standing Judicial Commission assign a case to a neighboring presbytery when a minister's presbytery refused to act. The 21st General Assembly reported in 1993 that the presbyteries had defeated it.
