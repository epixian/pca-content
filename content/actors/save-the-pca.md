An online advocacy campaign about the PCA, with a website at savethepca.com. Its
About page says it "is an initiative started by Michael Foster and other faithful
churchmen, both named and anonymous," and that its goal "is to stop the
Presbyterian Church of America ... from embracing left-wing practices that are not
rooted in scripture." Its main publication is "The Functional Female Officer
Report" (October 2025).
