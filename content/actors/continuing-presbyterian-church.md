The movement of congregations and ministers that left the Presbyterian Church in the United States (PCUS) and formed the PCA in 1973. "Continuing Presbyterian Church" was its working name, and the name of its corporation, until the first General Assembly chose the name "National Presbyterian Church" on December 5, 1973. The Steering Committee for a Continuing Presbyterian Church led it before the Convocation of Sessions and the Advisory Convention.
